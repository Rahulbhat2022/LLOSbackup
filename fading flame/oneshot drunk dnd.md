### CHAMBER 1: THE HALL OF KEYS

_"Demonstrate Patience and Perception"_

The party enters a long stone chamber. Five iron doors line the far wall, each marked with a different sigil — flame, wave, leaf, stone, star. Only one leads forward. The others are trapped.

At the center of the room sits a stone pedestal holding five iron keys, each engraved with the same sigils as the doors. Carved into the pedestal in old script:

_**"Five keys. Five locks. Four lie. Only the honest key turns."**_

**The Setup:** Four of the keys are cursed. Inserting a wrong key triggers the trap on that door. Only one key matches its sigil truthfully.

**The Clues:** Five clues are scattered around the chamber, each pointing at one key:

- A scorch mark on the floor near the **flame** door — but the soot is dry and ancient, untouched by recent heat. _(Flame key is a lie.)_
- A puddle beneath the **wave** door — but the water flows toward the door, not away. _(Wave key is a lie.)_
- A trail of dead leaves leading to the **leaf** door — but on inspection, the leaves are made of thin painted tin. _(Leaf key is a lie.)_
- The **stone** door is set deeper into the wall than the others — perfectly flush, no gap, no hinge visible. _(Stone key is a lie.)_
- The **star** door has faint silver light bleeding from the seam at the bottom. _(Star key is the truth.)_

Each clue requires an Investigation or Perception check (DC 13) to notice. A natural 20 on any check reveals two clues at once.

**The Traps (if a wrong key is used):**

- **Flame door** — Gout of fire fills a 15ft cone. Dex save DC 14, 3d6 fire damage half on success.
- **Wave door** — Floor floods. Whole party must Strength save DC 13 or be swept to the back wall, prone.
- **Leaf door** — Poison spores fill the room. Con save DC 14 or poisoned for 1 hour.
- **Stone door** — Ceiling drops 10 feet. Crushing damage 2d10, Dex save DC 15 half. Floor returns after one round.

**The Twist:** The party can use a wrong key _deliberately_ — the trap fires, but the door also opens briefly. A creative party might choose to eat a trap to bypass the puzzle. The proctors will note this in the Style column.

**Time pressure:** The other teams are in identical chambers, audible through the walls. The party can hear when a rival team triggers a trap — screams, fire roars, the slosh of water. Vex's team is suspiciously quiet.


### CHAMBER 2: THE VAULT OF WHISPERS

_"Demonstrate Stealth and Discipline"_

A heavy door opens into a long shadowed gallery. Six stone sentinels line the walls — humanoid statues, eyes carved as deep hollows, each holding a halberd. At the far end of the gallery lies a door to the side of it a table with a set of thieves kit. 
The sentinels cannot see. They can hear everything. Speak thrice and pay the price. 

**The Mechanic:**

- The floor is checkered in two types of tile: smooth black stone and rough white stone.
- **Black tiles are silent.** Anyone can walk them freely.
- **White tiles creak audibly.** Moving across one requires Stealth DC 15. Failure means a sentinel turns its head toward the sound.
- The tiles are arranged so there's no straight path of black — you have to weave, jump, or risk the white.

**The Sentinels:** Each statue tracks a "suspicion" level from 0 to 3.

- At suspicion 1, the statue's head turns toward the noise.
- At suspicion 2, it raises its halberd.
- At suspicion 3, it animates — it strides forward and attacks. AC 17, 45 HP, two halberd attacks per turn (+6 to hit, 1d10+3 slashing).

Every noisy action (failed Stealth, spell with verbal component, combat sound, dropped item) raises the suspicion of the **nearest** sentinel by 1.

**The Chest:** Locked. Thieves' Tools DC 16, or a Strength check DC 18 to break it (loud — raises suspicion of every sentinel by 1). Inside: the Key Sigil, and a small silver bell on a chain. If the bell is removed without first muting it (Sleight of Hand DC 13), it rings — instantly waking every statue at once.



**Creative options:** Silence spells, Pass Without Trace, Mage Hand to retrieve the chest from across the room, levitation, flight — all bypass parts of the challenge. The DM should reward clever thinking with shortcuts.

**Style points** are awarded for parties who get the chest without animating any statues at all.

### CHAMBER 3: THE COLLAPSING BRIDGE

_"Demonstrate Speed, Strength, and Sacrifice"_

The doors open onto the lip of a deep cavern. A natural stone bridge stretches across — broken in three places, with gaps of varying width. Beneath it, far below, a slow river of glowing molten rock. The heat is real. The air shimmers.

At the far side, mounted on the wall: a great brass lever. Pulling it opens the next door.

**The Catch:** As soon as the first party member steps onto the bridge, the rumbling starts. The bridge is collapsing. Each round, **one randomly-determined section of the bridge breaks and falls** into the lava. The party has six rounds to get everyone across and the lever pulled.

**The Bridge Sections (rolled by the DM each round):**

1. The starting platform
2. The first span (10ft gap to next)
3. The middle platform (a small island, good footing)
4. The second span (15ft gap, requires running jump)
5. The third platform (cracked, holds two people max)
6. The final span (8ft gap)
7. The lever platform

Each round the DM rolls 1d6 — that section collapses at the _end_ of the round. A character standing on a collapsing section gets one Dex save DC 15 to leap to an adjacent platform; failure means falling.

**Falling:** A magical sensor catches you before you hit the lava — but you're teleported back to the start of the bridge, and you lose your action for the next round. Public. The crowd reacts. The scoreboard ticks.

**The Jumps:**

- **10ft gaps** — Athletics DC 12 standing, DC 10 with a running start.
- **15ft gap** — Athletics DC 16, or DC 13 with a running start. Anyone who can't make the DC needs a different solution.

**Solutions the party can improvise:**

- **Throwing each other** — a Strength-based character can hurl a smaller party member across (Athletics DC 14, target makes Acrobatics DC 12 to land safely)
- **Rope and grapple** — extending help to teammates who can't jump
- **Spells** — Misty Step, Jump, Fly, Levitate, Featherfall, Spider Climb all trivialize parts of this
- **Carrying** — a strong character carries a weaker one, but moves at half speed

**The Lever:** Once pulled, the next door opens — but the bridge keeps collapsing. The far door stays open for two rounds, then begins to close. Anyone left behind has to take an alternate route (a thin ledge along the wall, Athletics DC 18, three rounds to traverse).

**The chamber rewards sacrifice and clever positioning.** A party that gets everyone across cleanly scores big on the boards. A party that abandons someone to make the timer scores low even if they finish.
