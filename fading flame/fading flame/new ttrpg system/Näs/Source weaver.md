Put 1 point in 2 different schools of magic. 
Go up in tier every odd level: so 1,3,5,7,9 etc.

When you Raw Cast, you do not simply cast a known spell. You _manipulate_ the Weave to replicate or approximate one. Raw Casting requires **Somatic components only** (physical gestures that pluck, twist, or weave the strings). The spell’s success, potency, and safety depend on how well you maintain control of those threads.

- **Declare your intent:** Describe what you want to achieve (usually a spell you know or an intended magical effect).
    
- **“Stability Threshold”:**
    
    - This is the number of **successes** required for the spell to form correctly.
    - The more complex or higher level the spell, the more successes are required.
    - As a base the amount of successes increases linearly. For every spell points spent the amount of successes needed increases by 1. 
    - The DC is usually 6 but may be increased or decreased by the complexity of the task.




**Declare how many Actions you’ll spend:**

- **Minimum:** 1 half action
    
- **Each additional Action** spent focusing reduces volatility by giving you additional rolls and time to stabilize the weave. 
- Each additional half action gives half the rolls (rounded down).
If the choice is purely to deal damage then choose between
Single target:
The damage dealt by the effects of the spell is not determined by these rolls. Instead you roll an amount of d10's equal to the amount of spell points expended. I
AOE:
Reduce the damage by one die for every 5 feet in width. 
Multiple target
Reduce the damage by one die for every additional creature.
