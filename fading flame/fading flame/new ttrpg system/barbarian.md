# Barbarian

The Barbarian is a relentless force of nature — a warrior who survives not by finesse or precision, but by enduring punishment that would kill lesser fighters and hitting back harder. Where others crumble under deep wounds, a Barbarian finds their edge.

---

## Rage

Rage is the Barbarian's core resource. It represents the primal momentum of a fighter who is hurting, furious, and unstoppable.

**Entering Rage:** At the start of your turn or as a reaction to taking a wound, you may enter Rage as a free action. Rage lasts until the end of the encounter or until you go two consecutive turns without attacking or taking damage, whichever comes first.

**While Raging:**

- You gain **+1 die on all Strength-based attack rolls.**
- You gain **+1 soak against all physical damage.**
- You **cannot be Frightened** and any existing Fear conditions are suppressed.
- You cannot cast spells or use features requiring Intellect or Composure rolls.

**Rage Points:** Certain features cost Rage Points (RP). You have a maximum of **Fortitude score** Rage Points. You gain 1 RP when:

- You take a deep wound while Raging.
- You kill a creature while Raging.
- You succeed on a Fortitude + Toughness roll.

Rage Points reset to 0 at the end of an encounter.

---

## Level Progression

Gain **2 points per level.**

- **Level 1:** Gain 1 additional point. Gain proficiency (+1) in two weapons of your choice.
- **Level 2:** Ability score increase by 1 point and gain one feat. Gain proficiency (+1) in two weapons of your choice.
- **Level 3:** May pick class features from **Tier 2.**
- **Level 4:** Gain an additional half action.
- **Level 6:** Ability score increase by 1 point and gain one feat. Gain proficiency (+1) in two weapons of your choice.
- **Level 7:** May pick class features from **Tier 3.**
- **Level 9:** Gain an additional half action.
- **Level 10:** Ability score increase by 1 point and gain one feat. Gain proficiency (+1) in two weapons of your choice.
- **Level 12:** May pick class features from **Tier 4.**
- **Level 13:** Gain an additional half action.
- **Level 14:** Ability score increase by 1 point and gain one feat. Gain proficiency (+1) in two weapons of your choice.
- **Level 17:** May pick class features from **Tier 5.**
- **Level 18:** Ability score increase by 1 point and gain one feat.
