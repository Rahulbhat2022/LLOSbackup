## **Range Rules**

**Range Penalties:**
Melee: +2 Dc
- **Close Range**: No penalty
- **Medium Range**: +1 DC to hit
- **Long Range**: +2 DC to hit
- **Extreme Range**: +3 DC to hit, only available to certain weapons

**Ammunition:**

- **Arrows/Bolts**: Standard cost, can be recovered on successful hits (50% chance)
- **Sling Stones**: Nearly free, rocks and pebbles
- **Throwing Weapons**: Must be recovered or replaced

**Special Ranged Combat Rules:**

- **Cover**: Target behind cover gains +1 DC to hit per level of cover
- **Moving Target**: +1 DC if target moved on their last turn
- **Composure Focus**: Spend a half-action to aim, reduce DC by 1 on next ranged attack

**Rock**: A heavy weapon cannot be parried and can only be blocked  by an environmental obstacle or by a shield. Armors can soak 1 less point of damage from Rock weapons. 

**Paper** Think paper less like "cover" and more like "keep at bay" Any weapon with the paper attribute has "reach" , allowing an additional square in attack range. A creature of your size or smaller must make a dodge, (needing to roll as many successes as your proficiency in the weapon) as part of their movement to be able to move within melee. If they fail you get an opportunity attack(cost half action) against them.

**Scissor**
Scissor weapon are quick and agile, and excel once distance has been closed. Scissor weapons can be used to dual wield allowing the wielder to gain a **slight** weapon attack as a bonus action with your off hand weapon. 


Any weapon used against each other gain + or -1 dc when rolling successes against an attribute that beats it. ie a rock weapon has -1 dc against rolling successes against a scissor weapon.

**Alacrity**- Once a turn you may take the dodge action for free. Any unarmored person also gains this feature.  
**Loud**-Any stealth checks made while wearing equipment with this feature increases the stealth dc by the amount in the parentheses. 
**Encumber sum**- Wearing this kind of equipment makes it harder to move for longer duration. Thus any athletics checks made for sprints are made with +2 dc. When moving through difficult terrain your movement is quartered. And standing up from prone costs an action.
