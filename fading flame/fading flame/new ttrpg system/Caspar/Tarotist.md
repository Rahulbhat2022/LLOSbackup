
## The Deck

**NOTE** You get 4 points in your subclass every level instead of 2. first level you get 5. 

The Tarotist carries a physical deck of tarot cards. Every card you have purchased is in the deck. Cards you have not purchased do not exist in your deck.

**Drawing:**
Spend a **half action** on your turn to draw one card at random. The effect lasts until the **end of your current turn**. Once a card is drawn it must go into effect the same turn. A card going into effect is part of the same half action but the card must not go into effect imminently after drawn as long as it does by the end of the turn. 

**Orientation:**
After drawing, immediately choose **Upright** (the effect benefits you) or **Reversed** (the effect targets one enemy of your choice in line of sight). You choose after seeing the card, never before.

**One card at a time:**
Only one card's effect may be active.You cannot draw another one until the previously drawn has gone into effect. 

**Reshuffling:**
The deck reshuffles fully at the start of each encounter. Drawn cards are put in the discard pile. Once all cards are drawn you may spend an action to reshuffle the deck.

## Starting Cards

When you first take this subclass, choose **1 card for free** from the 1-point list. These enter your deck immediately. All other cards must be purchased. The devil card is also added for free into your deck.

## 1-Point Cards

### 0 — The Fool

**Upright:** Your first movement this turn does not cost a half action, and the movement you make gain the benefits of careful movement and quick movement and none of the drawbacks. 

**Reversed:** One enemy must immediately move 4m in a direction you specify. They resist with Resolve + Focus DC 5. On failure, the movement happens before any of their other actions this turn.

---

### II — The High Priestess

**Upright:** Learn one of the following about any creature you can see — their current shallow wound total, their current deep wound total, or one condition they are currently suffering.

**Reversed:** One enemy loses any hidden advantage they currently hold. If hidden or obscured, they are immediately revealed. If they have a declared readied action, it is cancelled.

---

### III — The Empress

**Upright:** You and one adjacent ally immediately recovers 1 shallow wound.

**Reversed:** One enemy loses 1 shallow wound or if they have a stabilized wound it ruptures reapplying the effects.  — old injuries reopen at the wrong moment. This cannot be blocked or dodged.

---

### V — The Hierophant

**Upright:** Your next block or dodge reaction this turn reduces incoming damage by 1 additional success.

**Reversed:** One enemy's next block or dodge reaction this turn is reduced by 1 success before it applies. Your strike finds a gap.

---

### IX — The Hermit


**Upright:** Until the end of your turn, you cannot be flanked and do not suffer the Distracted condition from any source.

**Reversed:** One enemy loses their flanking bonus this turn. Any Distracted condition they are applying to allies is negated until the end of their next turn.

---

### XII — The Hanged Man


**Upright:** Your next defensive reaction this turn — block, parry, or dodge — costs no action debt from your next turn.

**Reversed:** One enemy's next reaction this turn costs double its normal action debt from their following turn.

---

### XIV — Temperance


**Upright:** Your next attack or defensive roll this turn may reroll 3 dice that showed a failure. Take the new results.

**Reversed:** One enemy's next attack this turn has its damage dice reduced by 1 (minimum 1 die).

---

### I — The Magician


**Upright:** Your next roll this turn may use any stat you choose instead of the one it normally requires. Declare the replacement before rolling.

**Reversed:** One enemy's next roll this turn uses their lowest stat instead of the one it normally requires. They cannot choose — you impose the substitution.

---

## 2-Point Cards
*Meaningful purchases. Every one of these should feel like it earned the half action.*

---

### IV — The Emperor


**Upright:** Until the end of your turn, you cannot be externally moved, knocked prone, disarmed, or otherwise displaced by any means. Anything attempting to do so simply fails.

**Reversed:** One enemy has +1 DC on every roll they make this turn.

---

### VI — The Lovers


**Upright:** Choose one — add 2 automatic successes to your next attack roll this turn, or grant one ally within line of sight a free half action on their next turn. You cannot take both.

**Reversed:** Force one enemy to choose between two options you name — both must be tactically meaningful (e.g. "move away or drop your weapon"). Enemy must make a resolve+composure needing as many successes as your threshold. They must commit to one and cannot do neither. GM rules validity. 

---

### VII — The Chariot

**Upright:** Your movement is doubled this turn and does not trigger reactions from any enemy. Move through threatened spaces freely.

**Reversed:** One enemy cannot take any movement-based reaction or pursue you this turn. 

---

### VIII — Strength

**Upright:** Next Strength roll made by you or an ally within line of sight has the dc lowered by 2.

**Reversed:** Next strength based roll made by an enemy has its dc increased by 2. 

---

### XI — Justice

**Upright:** Untill the start of your next turn when an enemy deals damage to you, immediately deal that same number of wounds back to them as a free reaction, no attack roll required. Only once. 

**Reversed:** One enemy must make a Resolve + Focus DC 6 roll. On failure, they cannot target your allies this turn, only you. Justice has turned its eye on them.

---

### XVII — The Star


**Upright:** You or one ally within line of sight immediately stabilises one deep wound without rolling. The countdown stops and the wound's active condition ends.

**Reversed:** In midst of the battle you wish for a miracle. The enemy must make a resolve+faith roll against your threshold. On fail a small miracle of your choice befalls the enemy. Example: The enemy suddenly looses grip and drops their weapon, the ground beneath their foot becomes unstable, etc. 

---

### XVIII — The Moon

**Upright:** Until the end of your turn, all ranged attacks against you have +1 DC and enemies have +1 DC to track your exact position or movement.

**Reversed:** One enemy is Confused until the end of their next turn. They may take one less action their next turn, and count as distracted until the end of their next turn.

### XIII — Death


**Upright:** Remove all your current shallow wounds.

**Reversed:** The next time the enemy takes damage they cannot reduce the incoming damage in any way. 

---

### X — Wheel of Fortune
*The Wheel turns for no one.*

**Upright:** Roll 1d10.
- **1–3:** Recover up to 2 shallow wounds.
- **4–6:** Gain one additional half action this turn.
- **7–9:** Your next attack roll has an additional 2 dice. 
- **10:** All three.

**Reversed:** Roll 1d10 for one enemy.
- **1–3:** They lose all shallow wounds recovered this round.
- **4–6:** They lose their half action on their next turn.
- **7–9:** Their next attack's DC has +2.
- **10:** All three.

---

### XIX — The Sun

**Upright:** Until the end of your turn, every roll you make adds 1 automatic success beyond what the dice show.

**Reversed:** Until the end of their next turn, one enemy treats every roll as producing 1 fewer success than the dice show (minimum 0).
