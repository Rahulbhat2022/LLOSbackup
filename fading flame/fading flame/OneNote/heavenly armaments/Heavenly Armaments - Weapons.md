# Heavenly Armaments — Weapons
*These weapons are not merely old. They carry the weight of the myths they were born from. Each grows with its wielder — features marked as stubs will be developed when a player claims the item.*

> [!info] Heavenly Armaments grow with the player. Current features represent the weapon at its most dormant. Maintain a relationship with the spirit or force within — some weapons have opinions about their wielders.

---

## Trident of Poseidon
*Greek — Controls seas and storms; symbol of maritime authority.*

The sea does not ask permission. Neither does this weapon.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Tide's Authority** | Attacks with this trident push the target 2m on a hit. The sea recedes and advances at your command. |
| **Storm Call** *(stub)* | *Grows with wielder — unlocked later.* |
| **Lord of Depths** *(stub)* | *Grows with wielder — unlocked later.* |

---

## Mjölnir
*Norse — Thor's hammer; returns when thrown; strikes with thunder. Legendary.*

> [!warning] Legendary item. Mjölnir carried the weight of protecting all of creation. A wielder who takes it lightly will find it heavy.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Returns** | When thrown, Mjölnir returns to your hand at the end of your turn. No action required. |
| **Thunder Strike** | Once per day, a hit with Mjölnir deals an additional deep wound to the target automatically. Roll on the wound table normally. |
| **Worthy** *(stub)* | *The hammer judges. Grows with wielder.* |

## Excalibur
*Arthurian — Sword of rightful sovereignty.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
