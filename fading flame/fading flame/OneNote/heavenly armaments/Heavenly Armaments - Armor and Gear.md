# Heavenly Armaments — Armor & Gear
*Divine protection from across the mythological world. Each piece carries the identity of its origin.*

---

## Aegis
*Greek — Shield/breastplate of Zeus or Athena; bears Medusa's head.*

> [!warning] Legendary item. The Aegis was worn by gods in cosmic conflict. Its full power is far beyond its current state.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Medusa's Gaze** | Once per day when a creature attacks you in melee, you may reveal the Aegis — they must make Resolve + Focus DC 6 or become Frightened until the end of their next turn, suffering +1 DC on all rolls. |
| **Divine Shield** | +1 soak against all attacks while wielding the Aegis. |
| **Olympian Authority** *(stub)* | *Grows with wielder.* |

---

## Golden Armor of Achilles
*Greek — Crafted by Hephaestus; worn in the Trojan War.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Hephaestus-Forged** | This armor reduces piercing damage soak requirement by 1 — it was made to turn blades. |
| **Hero's Burden** | While wearing this armor, you cannot retreat or be compelled to flee by fear effects. Achilles did not run from battle. |
| **The Heel** | This armor has one known weak point, determined secretly by the GM at the time of acquisition. A creature that discovers it gains +2 die to damage rolls against the wearer. |
