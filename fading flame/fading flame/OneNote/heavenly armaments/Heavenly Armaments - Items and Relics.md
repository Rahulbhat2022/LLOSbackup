# Heavenly Armaments — Items & Relics
*Not everything divine is a weapon. Some of the most dangerous things ever made fit in a pocket.*

---

## Ring of Gyges
*Greek — Grants invisibility; explores morality without accountability.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Invisible** | Once per day, become invisible for up to 1 minute (10 rounds). The invisibility ends if you attack or cast a spell. |
| **Unaccountable** | While invisible via this ring, you leave no tracks, make no sound, and cannot be detected by non-magical means. |
| **Moral Weight** *(stub)* | *Using this repeatedly has consequences the GM tracks quietly.* |

---

## Holy Grail
*Christian / Arthurian — Cup of divine grace.*

> [!warning] Legendary item. The Grail does not belong to anyone. It chooses who may hold it.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Divine Grace** | Once per day, completely heal one creature — remove all shallow wounds and stabilise all deep wounds. The creature must be present and willing. |
| **Worthy's Cup** | The Grail cannot be used by a creature who has committed an act of deliberate cruelty within the past 24 hours. The GM determines if this applies. |
| **Eternal Life** *(stub)* | *Grows with wielder — if worthy.* |

---

## Cintamani Stone
*Hindu / Buddhist — Wish-fulfilling jewel.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **One Wish** | Once per long rest, state one specific desired outcome. The GM determines how it manifests — not always literally, but always honestly. The stone does not trick. It simply fulfills. |
| **Spiritual Wealth** | Creatures of spiritual or divine origin treat you as neutral unless given specific cause not to. The stone radiates the right kind of presence. |

---

## Soma
*Vedic — Ritual drink granting divine strength.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Divine Draught** | Once per day, drink Soma as a half action. Until the end of the encounter: +1 die to all attack and defence rolls, and you do not fall unconscious from wound thresholds — you remain standing until death. The drink replenishes each dawn. |
| **Immortal Taste** | While the Soma effect is active, you are immune to poison and disease. |

---

## Apple of Discord
*Greek — Golden apple inscribed "To the fairest"; triggered the Trojan War.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Sow Discord** | Once per day, introduce the Apple into a social situation. Up to 3 creatures who can see it must make Resolve + Focus DC 6 or become immediately hostile to one another (not necessarily to you). The GM determines initial targets of their hostility. |
| **The Fairest** | Whoever holds the Apple has +2 on all Presence-based social rolls. Everyone wants what it offers. |

---

## Gjallarhorn
*Norse — Horn blown to signal Ragnarök.*

> [!warning] Legendary item. Blowing this horn is an event, not an action. Use carefully.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **The Call** | Once per long rest, blow the horn as a full action. Every creature within 300m hears it and knows something significant has been declared — the sound carries intent as well as noise. Allies within earshot recover 2 shallow wounds and lose the Frightened condition. Enemies within earshot make Resolve + Focus DC 7 or become Frightened until the end of their next turn. |
| **Ragnarök's Echo** *(stub)* | *Some horns should only be blown once. Grows with wielder.* |

## Sampo
*Finnish — Magical mill producing endless prosperity.*

## Flying Carpet
*Islamic / Arabian — Enables flight; symbol of status.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Flight** | The carpet carries up to 4 passengers and flies at twice normal movement speed. Requires concentration — the rider cannot attack while piloting unless they have a relevant feature. |
| **Navigation** | The carpet always knows the direction of any location the rider has previously visited and can travel there without getting lost. |

## Aladdin's Lamp
*Arabian Nights — Houses a wish-granting jinn.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Three Wishes** | The lamp grants 3 wishes total — not per day, ever. Each wish is negotiated with the GM. Wishes cannot kill, resurrect the dead, or compel love. Everything else is open. The lamp is then empty. |
| **The Jinn Within** *(stub)* | *If handled respectfully, the Jinn may offer more than wishes. Grows with wielder.* |

## Yata no Kagami
*Japanese — Sacred mirror of Amaterasu; reveals truth.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Truth Revealed** | Once per day, hold the mirror toward a creature or object. The GM must reveal one truth about it — a hidden nature, a lie it carries, a concealed feature. The mirror shows what is, not what appears. |
| **Sun's Lure** | Once per encounter, reflect light from the mirror toward one creature. They must make Resolve + Focus DC 5 or be Distracted until the end of their next turn — drawn toward the light. |

## Feather of Ma'at
*Egyptian — Represents truth, order, and cosmic balance.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Truth's Weight** | A creature holding the Feather cannot lie. They may refuse to answer, but cannot speak falsely while it is in their hand. |
| **Judgment** | Once per day, place the Feather against a creature's chest. The GM secretly determines if the creature has committed acts of great cruelty or injustice. If they have, the Feather is heavy — the creature has +1 DC on all rolls until dawn. If not, the Feather is light — they recover 1 shallow wound. |
