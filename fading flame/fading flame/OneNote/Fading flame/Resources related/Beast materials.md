**Beast Cores and Unique Beast Materials**  
**Beast Cores**  
Beast cores are the living heartstones of ancient creatures — crystallized reservoirs of their life force and power. They act as potent power sources for magical items. By incorporating a beast core into your work, you can craft any magical item(existing, ex Flame tongue, ring of protection, etc) of equal rarity to the core itself at a fraction of its usual cost.  
**Unique Beast Materials**  
Beneath the surface, in the forgotten depths of the world, remnants of the Old Beasts still roam — creatures from an age long before recorded history. Unlike the abundant metals of the last campaign, these beings are rare, singular, and perilous to hunt. Their bodies yield exceptional crafting materials, each one unique and finite. Once claimed, such materials cannot be easily replaced.
 
**Crafting Rules**  
A crafted weapon or piece of armor can be enhanced with multiple unique properties, provided they follow these limits:

- **Magic Items:** A pre-existing magical weapon or armor can gain **up to two** of the following features.
- **Artifacts:** May gain **one** of these features.

**Feature Sources:**

1. **Smith’s Enhancement:** A flat bonus of **+1, +2, or +3** to attack rolls, damage rolls, or AC.
2. **Beast Material:** Properties harvested from a unique Old Beast.
3. **Enchantment:** Magical effects granted by NPCs, rituals, or other creative sources.
 
**Example Beast Material**  
**Bone of Jormungrandr, the World Serpent**  
The colossal serpent’s bone still hums with the echo of its venomous wrath. Weapons or armor forged from this relic carry the lingering threat of poison.
 
**Weapon Feature — Venom of Jormungrandr**  
When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you may infuse them with a latent poison as part of that strike. This toxin lies dormant for up to **24 hours**, slowly circulating through their body.  
At any point during that time, you can use an action to trigger one of the following effects:

- **Slow Release:** The target must succeed on a **DC (14,15,16,17) Constitution saving throw** or take **(1d4, 1d6, 1d8, 1d10)** poison damage at the end of each of its turns for the next minute. On a success, the damage is halved.
- **Immediate Release:** The target must succeed on a **DC (14,15,16,17) Constitution saving throw** or take **(3d8, 4d8, 5d8, 6d8)** poison damage and become **paralyzed** until the start of your next turn. On a success, they take half damage and are not paralyzed.

**Uses:** Once per long rest. This ability is refreshed immediately if any creature dies within 1 minute of triggering either slow or immidiate release.
 
**Armor and Shield Features**  
**Light Armor & Bracers**

- Immunity to poison damage and the poisoned condition.
- When you take the **Hide** action, you may release a cloud of noxious gas, creating an area of magical darkness that only you can see through.
- Creatures starting their turn in the cloud must make a dc **(14,15,16,17) Constitution saving throw** or take **(2d6, 3d6, 4d6, 5d6)** poison damage on a fail and half on a success.
- **Uses:** Twice per day.

**Heavy Armor**

- Immunity to poison damage and the poisoned condition.
- Once per day, you can exude a slick, viscous coating across your armor, lasting 1 minute. While active:
    - Gain **+2 AC** against weapon attacks.
    - Advantage on checks to initiate or escape a grapple.
    - Any creature grappling you, or grappled by you, takes **3 piercing damage** and must succeed on a **DC 16 Constitution saving throw** or take **(2d8, 3d8, 4d8, 5d8)** poison damage and be poisoned for 1 minute.

**Shield**

- **Immediate Release:** When struck by an attack, you may cast **Hellish Rebuke** at a spell level equal to your proficiency bonus, dealing **poison damage** instead of fire.
- **Slow Release:** As a bonus action, you may fire a beam of virulent poison, dealing **(2d4, 2d6, 2d8, 2d10)** poison damage. If used in consecutive turns, the bonus action remains free.
- **Uses:** Twice per day.
