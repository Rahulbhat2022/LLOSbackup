
# Quest — The Venom Refined

### _"Whatever it was before it crawled into that forest, it's something worse now. I need the sac intact. Come back alive if you can manage it."_

> _— Vellarian Thorne_

**Quest Giver:** [[Vellarian Thorne]] **Location:** [[The Hollow Reach]] → [[The Silkdeep]] **Type:** Contract / Monster Hunt **Tone:** Witcher-esque — preparation, investigation, difficult choices **Reward:** See [[#Rewards by Poison Grade]] **Recommended Level:** 5–8

---

## Vellarian Thorne — The Client

**What he wants:** A **Silkdeep Venom Sac** — the poison gland harvested from a spider-creature in the Silkdeep, intact and uncontaminated. He has developed a refining process that can convert this venom into a highly potent **corrosive acid** with exceptional crafting applications. The problem is getting one. He has sent two previous couriers. He does not mention what happened to them.

**His manner during the briefing:**

> _"The forest is called the Silkdeep. You'll know you're in it when everything smells like musk and you feel like something is watching from the wrong direction. The creatures there — spiders, mostly, but not the kind you're thinking of — carry venom in a sac near the abdomen. I need that sac. Whole. The venom degrades if the membrane ruptures._ _There are different... grades of creature in there. A brood-spider will give you something workable. Something older, bigger, will give you something remarkable. And if you somehow manage to deal with whatever it is that runs the place — I've heard rumours — bring me what it points you toward. I won't ask how you managed it."_

---

## The Poison Grades

The **Silkdeep Venom Sac** comes in four grades depending on the creature killed to obtain it. Grade determines what Vellarian can refine it into, and what the party can use or sell.

> [!info] Venom Grade Overview

| Grade                 | Source Creature                                                   | Sac Condition                                              | Refined Product       |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| **I — Common**        | Brood Spider (standard)                                           | Intact but thin membrane                                   | Weak Silkdeep Acid    |
| **II — Notable**      | Elder Brood Spider                                                | Reinforced sac, dense venom                                | Strong Silkdeep Acid  |
| **III — Exceptional** | Spiderling Drow (sac harvested from chitin cavity)                | Thick organic sac, venom laced with transformation residue | Caustic Silkdeep Acid |
| **IV — Singular**     | The Monstrous (N'yelthara's discarded — a man mid-transformation) | Massive sac, venom still reactive to Source strings        | Voidsear Acid         |

Each grade requires a successful **Medicine + Investigation check** during harvest to extract the sac intact (DC 6 / 7 / 8 / 9 respectively). On a failure the membrane ruptures — the venom is still usable but degraded by one grade.

## Preparation — What a Smart Party Does First

This is a Witcher quest. Walking into the Silkdeep without preparation is how you end up in the Thread-Archive.

**Researching the Silkdeep:**

- **[[The Obsidian Shelf]]** (Hollow Crown's monster library) has two entries on Silkdeep spiders — a brief anatomical sketch and a warning note from a previous hunter. DC 5 Investigation to find them.
- **[[The Coil & Crucible]]** — Vellarian himself can describe the terrain and the silk-sense network if asked directly. He is more forthcoming than he seems.
- **Rumours in the Scourge** — Several dock workers and one retired militia man have stories about people who wandered into the Hollow Reach and came back wrong, or didn't come back at all. DC 6 Presence + Persuasion to get anything useful.

**Recommended Preparations:**

- **Cursed Oil** — Drow Spiderlings are Cursed Ones. Silver weapons or cursed oil dramatically improves survivability.
- **Insectoid Oil** — For the spiders themselves.
- **Chimera Oil** — For the Monstrous, if they go that route.
- **Antitoxin** — Paralytic venom is the Silkdeep's primary weapon. Having antitoxin prepared halves duration.
- **Non-metal probes** — For testing web tension without triggering silk-sense. A length of wood or bone works. Metal vibrates.

## Entering the Silkdeep

The Hollow Reach boundary is unmistakable. The party should feel it as a change in the air — sweeter, heavier, the ambient sound dropping off in a way that feels deliberate rather than natural.

The Silkdeep itself announces itself by the amber light and the hum.

> [!warning] Silk-Sense Every major web strand in the Silkdeep is connected to N'yelthara's awareness. **Any creature that touches a web strand** alerts every Spiderling and spider within 200m within seconds. The party has a window of approximately **2 minutes** before the first response arrives.
> 
> Avoiding the webs entirely is nearly impossible deeper in. The practical options are: move fast, move quiet, or find a way to announce yourself as non-hostile before the Spiderlings arrive.

**What they encounter upon entering, by depth:**

| Depth              | Encounter                                                         | Notes                                                                |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Outer edge         | 1–2 Brood Spiders (Grade I sac)                                   | Patrolling, avoidable with DC 5 Agility + Stealth                    |
| Mid-forest         | 1 Elder Brood Spider (Grade II sac)                               | Territorial, will not pursue past its web-anchor                     |
| Deep forest        | 1 trio of Drow Spiderlings (Grade III sac from fallen Spiderling) | Will attempt to capture rather than kill — they report to N'yelthara |
| Firstspun approach | The Monstrous (Grade IV sac)                                      | See [[#The Monstrous Encounter]]                                     |

## Branching Paths

The quest has four meaningful approaches, each reaching the objective by different means. These are not mutually exclusive — a clever party may combine elements of several.

---

### Path A — The Hunt (Straight Combat)

The most direct approach. Move into the Silkdeep, kill spiders of escalating size, extract sacs, leave.

**Viable for:** Parties that don't want to engage with N'yelthara at all, or who failed to avoid the Spiderlings and are now committed to fighting their way through.

**How it plays:** The party can achieve **Grade I–III** sacs through combat alone without ever reaching the Firstspun. They will need to deal with one Spiderling trio at minimum once they go deep enough — the trio will attempt to Restrain and capture rather than kill, reporting intruders to N'yelthara through the web. If the party defeats them quickly and moves, they have perhaps 10 minutes before N'yelthara's attention becomes personal.

**Grade IV (the Monstrous)** is only reachable via Path A if the party pushes all the way to the Firstspun approach — which means fighting through the deepest web corridors and likely triggering N'yelthara's direct awareness. See [[#The Monstrous Encounter]].

**Complications:**

- Killing Spiderlings is a significant violation of the Silk Pact. Future interactions with N'yelthara (this quest or future quests) become markedly more dangerous.
- The Elder Brood Spider (Grade II) is anchored in the mid-forest and will not pursue — but its death vibrates through the entire domain. The Spiderlings know something large died.

**Best outcome for Path A alone:** Grade II–III sac, in and out inside 30 minutes, having left at least one Spiderling alive (which N'yelthara interprets as deliberate — she finds restraint interesting).

---

### Path B — The Approach (Attempting Audience with N'yelthara)

The highest risk, highest reward path. Getting an audience with N'yelthara without being killed, cocooned, or turned into something requires either significant preparation, a very compelling argument, or the right object.

**The Problem:** N'yelthara does not receive uninvited guests. The silk-sense will flag them the moment they cross into meaningful depth. Lissiveth the Unspun is her gatekeeper, and Ossian Veld is what happens when Lissiveth decides the visitors aren't worth his time.

**How to signal non-hostile intent and trigger evaluation rather than attack:**

|Method|How|What it signals|
|---|---|---|
|**Don't fight the first Spiderlings**|Allow themselves to be Restrained and escorted without resistance|Composure under pressure — passes to Lissiveth|
|**Wait at the threshold**|Camp at the outer edge without advancing, make no aggressive moves, let the forest watch them|Patience — Lissiveth appears by morning|
|**Send one person in alone, unarmed**|One member walks in openly with no weapons drawn — reads as approach rather than incursion|Willingness to be vulnerable — immediate Lissiveth contact|
|**Invoke the Archive**|State aloud that they know what the Archive is and that they're not here for that|Demonstrates research, signals respect for the domain's logic|
|**Behave consistently for 10 minutes**|Move carefully, treat the web as something to respect, don't rush — N'yelthara watches how people act when they think nobody's made a decision about them yet|This is always happening, whether they know it or not|

**The Audience with N'yelthara:**

If the party reaches N'yelthara, she will already know approximately why they are there. She watched them enter. She felt every thread they touched. She is not hostile — she is _evaluating_, which looks identical to amusement from the outside.

She will not give them a Grade IV sac for free.

**What she wants in exchange:**

> [!quote] N'yelthara's offer _"There is a man — was a man — who became mine some weeks ago. He did not take well to the arrangement. He is somewhere in the deep forest, and he is no longer what he could have been. I want him dead. Bring me his eye — the human one, he will have one left — and I will give you what you came for."_

She is directing them to [[#The Monstrous Encounter]] — but by negotiated terms rather than brute force. She will tell them exactly where he is, describe his abilities accurately, and provide a **safe corridor** through the web that won't trigger silk-sense.

**Her real price — the test:**

N'yelthara does not want payment. She wants to _know what the party is._ The entire interaction is an evaluation, and the kill is just the obvious surface of it. What she is actually watching:

- How they respond to being asked to kill something that was once human — do they hesitate, ask why, demand justification, or simply agree?
- Whether they ask about Darren at all before accepting
- How they treat Lissiveth and the Spiderlings on the way out
- Whether, when they find Darren, they take a moment before killing him — or don't

She will not tell them they are being tested. She will not reveal her criteria. After they return with proof, she will hand over the sac and say nothing about her conclusions. But those conclusions will shape every future interaction she has with the party — whether she considers them worth dealing with again, what she offers and withholds, and whether she ever makes the First Weaving offer to one of them.

**What she may offer additionally based on how the interaction goes:**

- The paralytic antidote brewed from her own glands — immunity to Silkdeep venom 48 hours. Offered if she finds them composed and unafraid.
- Safe passage out without silk-sense triggering. Offered if she finds them respectful of the domain.
- A Grade II sac already detached. Offered upfront only if she decides, partway through the conversation, that they are genuinely interesting — which she will not announce.

### Path C — The Threadwalker (Navigate the Thread-Archive)

A non-combat, high-skill path for parties who have done their research.

The Archive contains preserved figures — men cocooned in silk, held in suspension. Somewhere in its corridors is a **Spiderling killed during an internal dispute years ago**, his body never fully broken down, his venom sac intact and preserved by the silk itself. Vellarian heard rumours of "preserved materials" deep in the forest. A DC 7 Intellect + Arcana or Medicine check during research confirms that spider venom can survive for years in silk suspension.

**Navigating the Archive:** The Archive is a labyrinth of brittle white silk walls. Moving through it without disturbing the walls requires:

- **Agility + Acrobatics DC 6** each corridor segment (roughly 5–6 segments to reach the preserved Spiderling)
- A **Wits + Perception DC 5** to find the right corridor branch — the body will be visible as a dark shape within the silk when close
- **A total of 3 successes** before **2 failures** (wall torn = silk-sense triggered)

The preserved figures the party passes along the way are part of the experience. Some are clearly long dead. Some — a chest slowly rising and falling through the outermost silk membrane — are not. None of them can speak or move. Whether the party acknowledges them or hurries past is between them and their conscience.

**Aids:**

- A non-metal probe reduces all navigation DCs by 1
- Dim or no light is better than bright — the silk walls are easier to judge by touch than by sight

**Success:** They find the Spiderling's preserved body. Grade III sac, intact. The harvest DC is **7** — the silk has toughened the membrane over time.

**On disturbing the walls:** N'yelthara doesn't send Spiderlings immediately. She appears herself, at the far end of the corridor — suspended at ceiling height, watching. She waits. This becomes a conversation, not a fight, if the party is composed enough to begin one. She is more impressed than angry. She will deal.

---

### Path D — The Informant (City-Side Investigation)

A party that wants to approach the Silkdeep from a position of knowledge can investigate its city presence first.

**The Lead:** Vellarian knows the Silkdeep has someone in Haven — he's heard things from black market contacts about a man who seems to be quietly watching people without obvious purpose. DC 6 Presence + Persuasion after accepting the quest gets him to share this. From there, asking around the Hollow Market or near the Dustmeadow Docks surfaces a description: a man with silver eyes, unhurried, asks questions that don't feel like questions. DC 7 Wits + Investigation to locate where he can be found.

**Meeting Lissiveth:** He presents as entirely human at first glance — the last time that mistake is easy. He is charming, unhurried, and already knows who the party is. He was watching when they spoke to Vellarian. He does not confirm his nature immediately.

If the party is direct about wanting entry to the Silkdeep, he will consider them for a long moment and then ask them a single question — something specific, personal, and seemingly unrelated to anything. He will not explain what it's for. The answer, and how they answer it, is reported to N'yelthara before they arrive.

If his assessment is that they are worth bringing in, he arranges guided entry to the Silkdeep: bypassing silk-sense entirely, brought to a threshold where a proper meeting can happen. This is the smoothest version of Path B. The cost is not material. The cost is that Lissiveth has now formed an opinion of them that N'yelthara shares, and the meeting that follows will reflect it.

**What makes a good impression on Lissiveth:** He is not looking for strength, charm, or competence. He is looking for _consistency_ — people who answer the same way whether or not they know they're being assessed. Trying to perform for him is immediately detectable and counts against the party.

## The Monstrous Encounter

The target at the end of this quest — regardless of which path reaches him.

His name was **Darren Ashfeld.** He was a 34-year-old courier from the Strand who wandered into the Hollow Reach three months ago during a late night job delivery, took a wrong turn past the northern checkpoint, and kept walking until the amber light found him. N'yelthara found him interesting. He did not fall for the charm. She offered the First Weaving anyway, as she occasionally does for men she respects. He accepted, hoping he could endure it and escape. He was wrong on both counts.

The transformation went incomplete — his body and the spider-form merged incorrectly, producing something that is neither a Spiderling nor fully The Monstrous. He retains fragments of memory and a broken, partial intelligence. He is in constant pain. He cannot leave the Silkdeep — he has tried. He wanders the deep forest, attacking anything that enters his territory reflexively. N'yelthara has not killed him because the venom his malformed form produces is extraordinary, and she has been waiting for it to reach maturity. It has.

> [!warning] Read This Before Running the Encounter Darren is not a monster. He is a man who made a desperate choice and paid for it in the worst way possible. How the party handles this — whether they talk to what's left of him before killing him, whether they try to — is where the Witcher tone lives. There is no saving him. There is, possibly, giving him a decent death.

**Finding him:**

- Via N'yelthara's directions: directly to a collapsed web-chamber 400m northeast of the Firstspun
- Via combat path: he finds them when they approach the Firstspun area — he is territorial and attacks on sight
- Via Thread-Archive navigation: he is visible through a gap in the Archive's eastern wall; they can observe him before engaging

**What he looks like:** A humanoid shape that has been pulled in two directions and settled uncomfortably in between. Four legs where two should be, erupting from his lower back. His torso is elongated, ribcage visible through thinned skin. His face is still mostly human — one eye normal, one milky and compound. He moves in jerking, asymmetric bursts that suggest something inside him is still trying to walk like a man and losing.

**What's left of his mind:** If the party speaks to him before attacking — calls out, asks his name — there is a pause. He stops. The jerking movement stills. He looks at them with the human eye.

He cannot form complete sentences. He can say: _"Darren."_ He can say: _"It hurts."_ He can say, once, clearly: _"Don't leave me like this."_

This is not a skill check. It just happens, if they bother to try.

> [!statblock] Darren Ashfeld — The Monstrous (Incomplete) **Type:** Chimera (former mortal — transformation incomplete) **Former Identity:** Courier, age 34, the Strand
> 
> |STR|AGI|CMP|FOR|INT|RES|WIT|PRE|
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
> |6|3|—|5|2|1|2|—|
> 
> **Shallow Wounds:** 5  |  **Deep Wounds:** 2  |  **Mental Wounds:** 1 **Actions:** 1 Action, 1 Half Action
> 
> **Attacks**
> 
> - _Leg Burst_ — 2 attacks per action, 2d10 wounds, reach 2m. The asymmetry means one attack each round is always at +1 DC to hit — the limb misfires.
> - _Venom Bite_ — Melee. Fortitude + Toughness DC 9 or paralysed 2 rounds. This venom is the Grade IV source — extraordinarily potent.
> - _Silk Spray (vestigial)_ — Half action, 4m cone, Agility DC 6 or Restrained 1 round. Unreliable — roll 1d6 before use; on a 1–2 it fails to fire.
> 
> **Special — Incomplete Form:** When Darren takes a deep wound, roll 1d6. On a 1–3 he collapses for 1 round — the body and the spider-form fighting each other for control. On a 4–6 he enters a frenzy: +1 attack per action for 2 rounds.
> 
> **Oil:** Superior Chimera Oil (full effect) **Note:** He does not heal between rounds. He does not call for help. He does not flee.

**Harvesting the Grade IV Sac:** The sac is large — palm-sized, pale amber, pulsing faintly with residual venom. Medicine + Investigation DC 9 to extract intact. On success: **Grade IV sac**, one of a kind.

If they spoke to Darren before killing him and gave him a clean death (no unnecessary wounds, no desecration), the extraction DC is **8** — grief has a focus to it.

**Proof for N'yelthara:** She asked for proof. The human eye — the normal one — is what she expects. This is not cruelty on her part. She simply knows what he looked like. She wants to be certain.

## Rewards by Poison Grade

Vellarian takes the sac, works for two days in his back room at the Coil & Crucible, and produces the refined acid. He gives the party their share first, then keeps the rest for his own commissions.

> [!success] Refined Silkdeep Acid — Properties by Grade

### Grade I — Weak Silkdeep Acid

_3 vials_

- Applied to a bladed weapon: ignores 1 point of soak from armour for 3 hits
- Applied to a lock or hinge: automatically succeeds on one forced-entry attempt
- **Crafting use:** Can be used as a corrosive component in basic oil or poison crafting, reducing DC by 1 for one batch

---

### Grade II — Strong Silkdeep Acid

_3 vials_

- Applied to a bladed weapon: ignores 2 points of soak, +1 damage die for 3 hits
- Applied to armour: reduces armour soak by 1 permanently (can be repaired by a smith, DC 7 Intellect + relevant craft)
- **Crafting use:** Component for Tier 2 oils and poisons; DC reduced by 1 and duration extended by 1 application

---

### Grade III — Caustic Silkdeep Acid

_2 vials_

- Applied to a bladed weapon: ignores 3 points of soak, +2 damage dice for 3 hits; any creature that takes wounds from an acid-coated strike must Fortitude DC 6 or take 1 additional shallow wound at the start of their next turn (acid burn)
- **Crafting use:** Can substitute for Concentrated Acids in any poison or oil recipe; also enables crafting of **Corrosive Bolts** (ammunition that deals 1d10 acid damage on hit, ignores 2 soak)
- **Special:** One vial can be thrown as a weapon — 10m range, 3d10 acid wounds on direct hit, Agility DC 6 splash (1d10) for adjacent creatures

---

### Grade IV — Voidsear Acid _(Singular — 1 vial + process)_

_1 vial, plus Vellarian teaches the party his refining notes_

The vial:

- Applied to a bladed weapon: ignores all armour soak for 3 hits; each hit deals +3d10 acid damage; any creature killed by an acid-coated strike cannot be resurrected by standard means — the venom dissolves the Source-strings in the wound
- Thrown: 12m range, 5d10 acid on direct hit; anyone in a 3m radius must Fortitude DC 8 or take 2d10 and lose 1 shallow wound to acid burn per round for 2 rounds
- **Crafting use:** Can be used as the base for a **singular named item** — Vellarian will craft one unique weapon or piece of armour incorporating the acid at no additional cost. The item retains acid-damage properties permanently (1/day use)

**Vellarian's Refining Notes:** A document he hands over as part of the deal. Allows the party (or a trusted alchemist contact) to process any Grade III sac into Grade IV acid — but requires the specific transformation-laced venom of a creature that was once human. Standard spiders won't produce it.

## Consequences & Threads

**If they killed Spiderlings:** N'yelthara notes it. She does not retaliate — violence in response to violence would be beneath her, and tells her nothing useful about the party. She simply has a lower prior on them now. If they later seek an audience, she'll grant it, but with less initial warmth and a harder first question from Lissiveth.

**If they dealt with N'yelthara and she found them interesting:** She will not say so. She will hand over the sac, thank them neutrally, and let them leave. But Lissiveth will be slightly more forthcoming the next time they encounter him in the city. Small doors will be slightly more open. She considers this ongoing — they have passed a first test, which simply means she is now running a second one.

**If they dealt with N'yelthara and she found them uninteresting:** The sac will be delivered as agreed — she keeps her arrangements. She simply won't initiate contact again. The party can seek her out if they want. She will deal with them transactionally and without investment.

**If they gave Darren a clean death:** She notices. It tells her something about what they value when no one is requiring them to value it. This weighs positively in her evaluation, not because she cared about Darren, but because consistency under no pressure is the hardest quality to fake.

**If they were cruel to Darren:** She also notices this. Same logic, opposite conclusion. The sac is still delivered. She is simply done being curious about them.

**Vellarian, after delivery:** Pays on time, no complaints. Goes quiet when he sees the Grade IV sac — the first time his composure slips. _"Where did you get this?"_ He doesn't press the answer. Two days later he finds the party and puts the vial and his notes on the table without ceremony. _"Don't go back there unless you've made a proper arrangement with whatever runs it. You got lucky."_ He means it.

## GM Notes

> [!tip] Tone and Pacing The Witcher feel comes from **preparation mattering** and **moral weight landing.** Reward the party for researching oils, for the non-metal probe, for knowing the Archive exists before they walk in.
> 
> N'yelthara's testing logic means the party is _always being assessed_ once they enter her domain, whether they know it or not. The GM should make quiet notes on how the party behaves — how they treat the Spiderlings, whether anyone acknowledges the preserved figures in the Archive, how long they pause before accepting the Darren assignment. None of this needs to be announced. It should simply inform how N'yelthara behaves toward them in all future encounters.
> 
> Darren is the emotional core. Don't rush past him. The quest is nominally about acid for crafting. It's actually about what N'yelthara learns about the party from watching how they handle a man who can only say three things.

> [!tip] Scaling
> 
> - **Lower level party:** Remove the Spiderling trio from the mid-forest, make the Elder Brood Spider the deepest enemy before the Monstrous. Grade III becomes the top natural reward, Grade IV only via N'yelthara.
> - **Higher level party:** Add a second Monstrous near the Firstspun (an older incomplete transformation), give N'yelthara a more demanding price for her cooperation.

> [!tip] Follow-up Hooks
> 
> - Vellarian has a second commission — he needs something from the Bloatwood. Mother Muckrot is involved. It's worse.
> - N'yelthara sends Lissiveth with a message: she has been watching a specific person in the party's circle and thinks he may be worth testing. She would like to arrange an introduction. She is asking permission, which is itself unusual, and Lissiveth will say so if asked.
> - Darren's family in the Strand is still looking for him. They haven't stopped. Someone is going to have to tell them something.
