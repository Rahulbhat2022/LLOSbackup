# KAVA QUEST — The Heart of the Mountain

---

## Setup

Kava Iseldi has been expanding her forge complex into a deeper, older tunnel system beneath the Crucible Vault. Her research indicates that somewhere in a specific direction is a **Heart of the Mountain** — a legendary forge-core of immense alchemical and metallurgical value. She has sent **three separate warforged excavation parties** into the tunnels over the past four weeks to locate it.

All three parties reported back: nothing found. No anomalies. No notable discoveries.

Kava does not believe them. Not because she distrusts her constructs — she trusts them absolutely. She distrusts the *result*. Her sixth sense for materials tells her something is down there. She sends the party as non-warforged investigators to determine why her parties are coming back empty.

**What Kava does not know:** The tunnels are the Tomb of Hephaestus. An ancient forge-entity has inhabited this space since before the Satarum. It consumes things — specifically the warforged units — and their records of each other. The creature does not move. It hammers. The hammer rhythm is the creature feeding. Everything in proximity is slowly drawn toward the sound.

**The three parties:**
- **Excavation Alpha** — sent 4 weeks ago, 6 units dispatched, 3 remain. Stationed near the entry.
- **Excavation Beta** — sent 2 weeks ago, 5 units dispatched, 2 remain. Stationed mid-dungeon.
- **Excavation Gamma** — sent 4 days ago, 4 units dispatched, 1 remains. Stationed deep.

None of the three parties have any record of each other. Kava sent them independently and briefed each one on the others. They should know. They do not.

**The hammer rhythm** is present from the moment the party enters. It never stops. It sounds exactly like Kava's Foundry Choir working — because Kava told them the Choir would be working down here. This is the entire disguise. Do not draw attention to it. Let it be background.


---

## Area 1 — The Entry Hall

**Description:** A wide corridor of Kava's construction — fresh excavation marks, her precision evident in the stonework. A supply depot has been established here: crates of materials, maintenance equipment, charging alcoves carved into the walls.

**Tells:**
- The supply depot is stocked for approximately 15 units. Three parties of the sizes dispatched would be 15. Currently there are 6 warforged total in the dungeon. The depot is not depleted — the missing units were consumed before they could use their supplies.
- Allocation tags on equipment crates list unit designations. Several designations on the tags do not appear in any active party roster. The tags are recent.

**Environmental:** Kava's map shows this corridor running straight. It has a slight curve to the left that isn't on the map. The warforged from Alpha, when asked about the discrepancy, check their own survey data and confirm the corridor is straight. Their survey data is wrong.

---

## Area 2 — Excavation Alpha Camp

**Description:** A working camp. Three warforged units, professional and cooperative. They provide a full briefing on their progress: they have been excavating in the designated direction for four weeks and have not located the Heart. They are currently awaiting further instruction.

**Tells:**
- The camp is configured for 6 units. Six maintenance stations. Six charging alcoves. Six sets of personal tool storage, each labelled with a designation. Three of the designated storage units are labelled for units not present. When asked, Alpha units check their roster. Those designations are not on their roster. They flag this as a configuration error and move on.
- The production log runs from day 1 to today. On day 1, 6 units are listed as active. By day 8, the number is 5. By day 15, 4. Today, 3. There is no comment on the changes anywhere in the log. No incident report. No transfer notice. The numbers simply decrease.
- Mid-briefing, one unit calls out: *"Confirm eastern passage status with Unit 4."* Unit 4 is not present. No response. The unit pauses — one second — then continues the briefing. It does not note the failure to respond.
- The shift rotation schedule shows 6 slots. Three slots are blank — not cancelled, not reassigned. Simply empty. The remaining units are rotating through 3 slots and leaving 3 blank every cycle without flagging it as incomplete coverage.

**Environmental:** The stone walls here have two layers of marking. On top: Kava's excavation notations, fresh. Underneath, older and deliberate: hammer-struck patterns that predate Kava's work entirely. They are not decorative. They form something — a geometry that becomes clearer the further into the dungeon the party travels.

---

## Area 3 — The Deep Corridor

**Description:** An unoccupied connecting passage between Alpha and Beta territory. Long, dimly lit by Kava's installed fixtures, several of which have gone dark.

**Tells:**
- Unit designation scratches on the walls — warforged marking their passage as per Kava's protocol. Three distinct sets of designations are visible: Alpha's, Beta's, and a third set. The third set does not match any unit currently active in the dungeon. These scratches are recent — within the last week.
- The third set of designations belongs to Gamma units that no longer exist. Gamma does not know they passed through here. The scratches are the only remaining evidence those units were ever in this dungeon.

**Environmental — The Shift:** On the way in, this corridor is approximately 60 feet long. On the way back out, it is longer — closer to 90 feet. A door that was on the left wall is now on the right. A crate left as a marker has moved. The warforged, if the party asks them to verify, measure the corridor and confirm 60 feet. Their measurement is wrong.

**Party Tell — The Contradiction:** At some point in this corridor, two party members have different memories of which direction they entered from. Both are certain. Neither is wrong in the conventional sense — the creature has lightly touched one of them. Do not resolve this. Move on.

---

## Area 4 — Excavation Beta Camp

**Description:** A less organized camp than Alpha — not through sloppiness but through visible strain. Two warforged units, both in motion, both doing more than one task simultaneously.

**Tells:**
- The task assignment board lists 5 units with individual task assignments. Two units are present and are collectively attempting all 5 assignments. They are failing at the edges — work that requires simultaneous action in two locations is being done sequentially, poorly. They do not flag a capacity problem. They do not know they are understaffed.
- The headcount log runs daily for two weeks. Every entry reads: *"Unit count: 5. All nominal."* The last entry was this morning. There are 2 units present.
- Tool allocation: 5 tool sets, each signed to a unit designation. 2 units are present. 3 sets are signed to designations not on the current roster. The tools are here. The units are not.
- When the party asks about the eastern passage, one unit responds: *"Unit 3 conducted the most recent survey of the eastern passage. Unit 3 would have the relevant data."* Unit 3 is not here. Unit 3 is not on the roster. When asked where Unit 3 is, the unit pauses, accesses its records, and says: *"Unit 3 is in position."* It cannot specify what position.

**Environmental:** Some of the forge equipment in this camp is running without anyone operating it. A bellows cycles. A small furnace maintains temperature. The warforged do not react to this. When asked, they confirm the equipment is functioning normally. They do not register that no one is running it.

---

## Area 5 — The Old Forge

**Description:** An ancient chamber predating Kava's work entirely. The forge infrastructure here is original to the tomb — massive, elaborate, built for something larger than human hands. All three parties have passed through and noted it in their logs.

**Tells:**
- Work of extraordinary quality sits mid-completion on the central workbench. A weapon or tool partway through being made — the metalwork done so far is far beyond what any current party could produce at this depth with these tools. No current unit claims to have worked on it. All three parties' logs reference the chamber but with contradictory descriptions of what was on the workbench.
- A safety protocol board, installed by Kava's teams, specifies a minimum of 4 units to operate the main forge apparatus. The operation log shows the apparatus was run yesterday. One unit ran it alone. The safety minimum is not flagged as violated.
- All three parties have left logs referencing this chamber. The logs contradict each other on details — the size of the room, what equipment is where, what was found. The contradictions are not about opinion. They are about facts. The room has been different for each party.

**Environmental — The Drift:** Small tools left on flat surfaces in this room move. Not quickly. Not visibly. But a chisel left on a workbench at the start of the room will be three inches closer to the far wall by the time the party reaches the far wall. The movement is toward the hammer rhythm. If the party leaves an item and returns, it has moved further. This room is the clearest demonstration that the environment is not static.

---

## Area 6 — Gamma's Last Position

**Description:** A single warforged unit, alone, running at full capacity. The chamber shows signs of recent occupation by more units — four charging alcoves used, four maintenance stations recently serviced.

**Tells:**
- When asked how many units are in the Gamma party, the unit answers: *"Four."* Without hesitation.
- When asked to indicate where the other three units are, it pauses. Longer than any previous pause from any unit. Then: *"They are in position."*
- The unit's own maintenance log from yesterday includes entries for maintaining three other units. The entries are detailed — specific diagnostic checks, lubrication points, calibration records. For units that are not here.
- When the party points out that only one unit is visible, the unit runs a headcount. The headcount returns: *"4 units present, all nominal."* It counts itself four times without knowing it is doing so.
- The charging alcoves are warm from recent use.

**Environmental:** The walls in this chamber have been reshaped. Not excavated — the stone has moved and reformed around the existing equipment, as though the chamber grew to fit the things inside it rather than the things being placed in the chamber. The Gamma unit, when asked about the walls, confirms they are standard excavated stone.

**Party Tell — The Flash:** One party member (choose based on who is most attuned to the campaign's memory themes — Siobhan or Jack are strongest candidates) receives a brief, involuntary sensory impression: the feeling of holding a hammer, the smell of hot metal, the specific physical sensation of work nearly finished. It is vivid and then gone.

It feels exactly like one of their lost memories trying to surface.

It is not. It is a fragment of a consumed warforged unit, leaking through the creature's proximity into a mind already structurally open to this kind of intrusion because of the campaign-start memory wipe. The mechanism that wiped their memories and the creature's consumption mechanism are not the same thing — but they left the same door open.

Do not explain this yet. Let the player decide what it means to them.

---

## Area 7 — The Sealed Chamber

**Description:** All three parties' logs mark this as a dead end — a collapsed section of tunnel, impassable. Kava's map agrees. The map is based on the parties' reports.

**Tells:**
- The "collapse" is too clean. Natural collapses are irregular. This one is flush with the corridor walls, smooth, complete. It looks like a wall pretending to be a collapse.
- Sound comes through it. The hammer rhythm, which has been present throughout the dungeon, is loudest here and is clearly sourced from the other side.
- The wall is warm. Stone does not retain heat this consistently. The warmth pulses faintly, at the rhythm of the hammering.

**Environmental:** The wall is the creature's outer surface. It has restructured itself to seal the entrance to its core. It looks like stone. It is not stone.

**Party Tell — The Pull:** The party member who received the sensory flash in Area 6 feels drawn toward this wall. Not compelled — just an inclination, a pull they can resist easily. The creature recognises the open door.

---

## Area 8 — The Core Chamber

**Description:** Beyond the false wall is a vast chamber, older than anything else in the dungeon. The forge infrastructure here is not Kava's and not human-scaled. It is enormous, elaborate, and still functioning — bellows the size of walls, crucibles that could hold a house. The hammer rhythm here is not heard. It is felt. The floor vibrates with each strike.

**The Creature:** At the centre of the chamber is the creature — the entity that was once the greatest forge master of its age, who was left here when the world above burned and never came back for it. It worked because work was all it had. It consumed because it could not bear to be alone. It has forgotten everything it has consumed, including the ones it was trying to keep.

It is massive. It has too many arms, some ending in hammer-shapes, some in articulated hands. Its body incorporates centuries of materials — metal, crystal, stone, and underneath all of it, something organic that is very, very tired. The warforged parts of the consumed units are visible in its body, integrated into its form, still running partial functions.

The hammer rhythm is its heartbeat.

It does not attack immediately. It hammers. Things in the chamber are slowly drawn toward it. The party will feel the pull increase the longer they remain. It is not malicious. It simply does not want to be alone again.

**The Heart of the Mountain:** The Heart is visible at the creature's centre — a crystallised core, warm gold and deep red, pulsing with each hammer strike. It is what the forge master was before the transformation: their original self, compressed and crystallised, still faintly present. Everything the creature has consumed over centuries has been absorbed into and around this core — the knowledge, the skill, the craft of every being that came down here. This is why it would be an extraordinary forging material. It is also why it is what it is.

Removing the Heart requires reaching the centre of the creature. This kills it — or more accurately, releases it from something that has lasted too long. The creature does not fight to survive in the way a predator does. It fights because the Heart is the last thing it has of itself, and something in it knows this, even if it cannot articulate it.

---

## The Reveal

The *"of course it's a false hydra"* moment should arrive in stages:

1. **The first crack** comes in Area 6, when the Gamma unit counts itself four times and the party begins to articulate what they've been seeing across all three parties.

2. **The second crack** comes at the sealed wall, when the party connects the hammer rhythm — which has been present since the moment they entered — to the creature on the other side.

3. **The full break** comes in the core chamber, when they see the consumed warforged parts integrated into the creature's body, and understand that the Choir Kava said would be working down here has been here the whole time. The hammering never stopped being the Choir. It just changed what the Choir was.

4. **The party-specific beat:** The member who received the sensory flash in Area 6 now understands what it was. A fragment of a consumed unit, reaching through them because they were already open to it. The question of *why* they were open — what was done to their memories at the campaign's start, and whether the mechanism that did it shares anything with what this creature does — is a question worth leaving unanswered for now.

---

## Kava's Role After

Kava wanted the Heart. She gets it if the party retrieves it. She will not volunteer that she knew what was likely down there — she suspected a dangerous entity, which is partly why she sent warforged rather than human workers. She will not volunteer that the Heart's extraordinary properties come from everything the creature absorbed, including her own units.

If the party asks directly, she answers directly. She does not lie. She simply does not offer information that wasn't requested.

Whether the party considers this acceptable or a form of betrayal is up to them.
