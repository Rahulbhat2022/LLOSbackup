Charged ritual casting  
Spells can be upcast in 2 ways when you don’t have a higher level spell slot.  
If you know the spell you can engage in a ritual to cast the spell(this only works on spells that take an action and do not consume or require any material components that cast gold). If a spell takes an action to cast you can do the ritual to cast the spell. It takes an amount of actions equal to the spell level. you can upcast a spell like this only by 2 levels over your highest spell slot(expended or nonexpended) you spend your entire action as if you are focusing on a spell. Remember that this is only upcasting spells so you need to at least have the spell slot to cast it at its base level. The amount of actions needed to cast the spell can be reduced by expending a spell slot, an amount of actions equal to a number of spell level is reduced. Thus if you wish to cast a 3rd level guiding bolt but are only level 1. you can spend 2 action top of the original 1 action to upcast it.

**Chokehold**  
The new chokehold move is a way for martials to incapacitate enemies similar to spells like Sleep, hold person and colour spray. If you are in melee range of a creature that is your size or smaller and is not aware of your presence, you can attempt to grapple the target in a chokehold until it falls unconscious.  
As an action you may, Using two free hands, you try to wrap your arms around the target’s throat by making a Strength (Athletics) check contested by the target’s Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check (the target chooses the ability to use). If you succeed, the target is restrained, and cannot speak. This of course won't work for every creature that discretion will be left to the dm.  
You can roll a number of d6 equal to your Strength modifier and if the result is greater than or equal to the target’s hit points, the creature falls unconscious for 1 minute. If the result is not greater than or equal to the target’s hit points, you can use an action to continue choking them, rolling again and adding the new result to the previous result if you are successful. You can repeat this process until the creature is unconscious or you fail to maintain the chokehold.  
A creature that does not require air cannot be choked out. If the character performing the chokehold is a Rogue, they may add their Sneak Attack to the roll. If the character is a Monk, they may use their Martial Arts dice instead of d6.

T**actical Actions - A New Combat Option**  
**Overview:** During your turn in combat, you gain access to an additional type of action called a **Tactical Action**. These actions represent small, dynamic maneuvers that happen naturally within the flow of combat and don’t consume your regular action, bonus action, or movement. Instead, they enhance your combat style and create opportunities for more creative, cinematic moments.

- **Dirty Trick (Rogue/Skirmisher):** After making an attack, you kick up sand or debris, causing an enemy’s next opportunity attack to be made with disadvantage.
- **Riposte (Duellist):** After dodging or parrying an attack, you make a quick, precise counter-strike.
- **Defensive Stance (Tank/Defender):** When holding position with a shield, you can brace yourself to impose disadvantage on the first attack made against an adjacent ally.
- **Momentum Sprint:** After making a melee attack, you can dash a short distance, using the momentum to evade danger.
- **Flanking Feint:** When flanking an enemy, you distract them, allowing your ally to make their first attack with advantage.

You can work with the DM to create unique tactical actions tailored to your character’s theme and abilities.  
**Creative Freedom:** Beyond the pre-defined tactical actions, you’re encouraged to think creatively! If you want to cut a rope mid-attack, shove a lever while sprinting, or knock a lantern onto a barrel of oil — that’s exactly what a tactical action is for. As long as the action fits the flow of what your character is already doing, it’s fair game.

- **Body Block:** Throw yourself in front of an ally to absorb a hit.
- **Quick Catch:** Snatch an object thrown towards you or an ally.
- **Timed Button Press:** Wait and push a button or pull a lever at the precise moment an event occurs.

This combination lets you interact with the environment or protect allies without feeling like you’ve wasted your turn.  
**Why Add Tactical Actions?** The goal is to give players more room for creative, in-the-moment decisions without punishing you for not choosing a standard attack or spell. Tactical actions reward improvisation and storytelling, turning combat into a more vivid and dynamic experience.  
 
Melee tactical action

- **Pommel Strike:** Use the hilt of your weapon to make a quick bash, dealing 1d4 damage and potentially staggering the enemy (they must make a DC 10 Constitution save or lose their reaction until the end of your turn).
- **Weapon Flourish:** Make an intimidating display with your weapon, forcing a nearby enemy to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency + Strength or Dexterity modifier) or be frightened until the start of your next turn.
- **Shield Bash:** If you’re wielding a shield, you can shove an enemy 5 feet.
- **Guarded Step:** After making an attack, you can shift 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Shoulder Check:** As part of your movement, you can bump into an enemy, forcing them to succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 10 + your Strength modifier) or be knocked prone.
- **Weapon Lock:** When grappling, you can use your weapon to pin an opponent's weapon, forcing them to make a Strength saving throw or be unable to attack with that weapon until the grapple ends.
- **Quick Parry:** When an enemy makes a melee attack against you, use your tactical action to reduce the damage by 1d6 + your Dexterity modifier.
- **Combat Roll:** When an area effect targets you (like a fireball), you use your reaction and tactical action and can roll 10 feet to try to escape the area, making your saving throw with advantage.
- **Blade Catch:** When attacked with a bladed weapon, use your tactical action combined with your reaction to catch the blade with your own, reducing the attack roll by 1d10.
- **Rallying Shout:** Let out a powerful war cry, giving allies within 30 feet temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.
- **Flanking Feint:** When flanking an enemy, you use the clank to distract them, giving your first attack advantage.   

- **Quick Search:** Glance around for hidden objects or clues nearby (requires an Investigation or Perception check).
- **Check for Weakness:** Analyze an enemy for weak spots or vulnerabilities (requires an Insight or Arcana check).
- **Environmental Scan:** Assess the battlefield for useful environmental features (like unstable structures or flammable objects).

- **Spell Weave:** Use a bonus action to subtly manipulate magical energy, causing your next spell to ignore half cover.
- **Arcane Insight:** Use an Arcana check to identify a magical effect or gauge a creature's magical defenses.
- **Mystic Ward:** Summon a brief, shimmering barrier that gives an ally +1 AC until the start of your next turn.
- **Elemental Surge:** Briefly enhance a spell's element (like making a flame spell emit light or a cold spell create difficult terrain for a moment).
- **Focused Chant:** Channel your magic more precisely, allowing you to reroll a low damage die once.
