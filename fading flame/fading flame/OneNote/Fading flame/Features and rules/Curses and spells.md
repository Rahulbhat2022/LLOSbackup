Curses. Created by people of the natural order. Inbuing magics into curses. Curses are not well known or well documented. Oftentimes only high level bad know of them and pass them on to those that wish others harm. This is largely because a curses caster is many times easily identifiable. Traces of magic linger around the caster untill the spell has completely dissapated.
   

Make them riddle like, play on opposites and analogies and associations.  
Base a cursed meter. Point system.  
Powerful people can affect your curse meter.  
Cursed objects.  
Combined curse buildup works like exhasution and could lead to exhastion.
 
The caster must make a arcana check to do the ritual properly and a wisdom saving throw to not grow mad.  
**1.Curse of Shattered Resolve**

- **Effect:** The cursed loses courage over time, becoming increasingly fearful and unwilling to engage in combat or face danger. Eventually, they may even become paralyzed by fear.
- **Ritual Setup:** Perform under a waning moon. The caster needs a cracked mirror, a black feather, and a vial of stale blood.
- **Ritual:** The caster speaks the target’s name into the mirror three times, then drops the feather into the blood. Shattering the mirror seals the curse. The feather and blood are to be buried where the target often walks to spread the curse over time. Each time the target walks over they must make a dc wisdom saving throw or the effect worsens.
 
**2. Curse of the Silent Heart**

- **Effect:** The target slowly becomes emotionless, finding it difficult to connect with others and unable to express any form of joy or love. Eventually, they become almost ghost-like in demeanor.
- **Ritual Setup:** This curse requires rose petals, ash, and a mirror submerged in a bowl of salt, and tears of a widow.
- **Ritual:** Under a cloudy sky, the caster sprinkles the rose petals over the mirror and speaks of the target’s last words of love or kindness. Dropping the ash into the water, the caster watches as the petals drift apart and dissipate. The target must make a dc cha save first to 3 or this takes effect.
 
**3. Curse of Eternal Blisters**

- **Effect:** The target’s skin breaks out in painful, stinging blisters anytime they touch metal, making it almost impossible for them to wield weapons or armor.
- **Ritual Setup:** The caster needs a piece of cloth from the target’s clothing, hot coals, and the dried skin of a snake.
- **Ritual:** The caster lights the coals and places the cloth on top, allowing it to burn. They wrap the snake skin around their fingers, chanting a phrase three times to direct the burning pain to the target. The target must make a dc con save first to 3 or this takes effect.
 
**4. Curse of Splintered Thoughts**

- **Effect:** The cursed’s thoughts become jumbled and fragmented. They struggle to recall memories or maintain focus, impacting spellcasting, planning, and decision-making.
- **Ritual Setup:** A needle, an small obisdian bowl, and a piece of parchment with a handwritten thought of the target.
- **Ritual:** Under moonlight, the caster pricks their finger with the needle, digging the needle into your finger hard as you must fill the obsidian bowl to the brim blood fall onto the. Using that same needle you write fill in the words written on the parchment.

**5. Curse of the Dripping Shadows**

- **Effect:** The target becomes haunted by creeping shadows that drip around them. They see shadowy figures out of the corner of their eye, unsettling and causing them to be constantly on edge.
- **Ritual Setup:** A candle, ink harvested from a squid killed by necromancy spell, a filed down coin.
- **Ritual:** In a darkened room, the caster places the coin under the candle and lets ink drip onto it while chanting. They then extinguish the candle, placing it into the vial to hold the darkness for the target.
 
**6. Curse of Wilted Vitality**

- **Effect:** The cursed finds their physical strength waning and body weakening, as if they are gradually withering like a dying plant.
- **Ritual Setup:** Dried lavender, a wilted leaf, and a needle pricked with the target’s name carved into it.
- **Ritual:** On barren land, the caster buries the lavender and the leaf together, speaking words to sap life. They press the needle into the ground at the spot, sealing the energy drain.
 
**7. Curse of Flickering Sight**

- **Effect:** The cursed intermittently goes blind, often at critical moments. Sometimes, they see visions of terrifying creatures just beyond the veil of the physical world.
- **Ritual Setup:** A black silk cloth, a spider’s leg, and powdered bone.
- **Ritual:** The caster wraps the spider leg in the cloth and chants over it, sprinkling the powdered bone into the wind. The cloth is then burned, releasing the darkness to cloud the target’s eyes.
 
**8. Curse of Endless Hunger**

- **Effect:** The cursed becomes insatiably hungry, no matter how much they eat. They lose weight and energy, as their body cannot retain the nourishment.
- **Ritual Setup:** A piece of stale bread, a small animal bone, and a goblet of salt water.
- **Ritual:** The caster gnaws on the stale bread while dipping the bone into the salt water. Speaking a phrase of eternal emptiness, they crush the bone, sealing the target’s endless hunger.
 
**9. Curse of the Hollow Voice**

- **Effect:** The cursed’s voice becomes faint and hollow, making it difficult for others to hear them. Over time, it becomes even harder to speak until they can only whisper.
- **Ritual Setup:** A piece of parchment, a single raven’s feather, and a thin piece of obsidian.
- **Ritual:** The caster writes the target’s name on the parchment, recites a phrase three times, and uses the feather to spread ink over the obsidian. The parchment is then shredded and thrown into the wind.
 
**10. Curse of the Creeping Chill**

- **Effect:** The cursed is haunted by a chill that they can’t escape, even on the warmest days. Over time, they feel frost creeping into their bones, slowing their movements and causing fatigue.
- **Ritual Setup:** A piece of ice, a handful of black sand, and a candle that has been previously frozen.
- **Ritual:** Under the winter stars or in the coldest part of night, the caster lets the candle drip wax onto the ice, reciting an invocation to chill the target. The black sand is then cast around, sealing the curse.
 
**1. Curse of Shattered Resolve**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must find a source of bravery—a symbol or item of a person known for their courage. Only once they face a fear while holding this item can _Remove Curse_ work, symbolizing their rekindling resolve.

**2. Curse of the Silent Heart**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must perform an act of genuine kindness toward someone else, showing that they can still connect emotionally. Only after an act of compassion will _Remove Curse_ be effective.

**3. Curse of Eternal Blisters**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must cover their hands with a thin layer of silver dust and place them on a piece of metal for at least one minute. Once they endure the sting of touching metal with their silvered hands, _Remove Curse_ can release them.

**4. Curse of Splintered Thoughts**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must piece together a fragmented memory from before the curse by speaking with someone who was there or using a keepsake from that moment. Only after regaining a coherent memory can _Remove Curse_ succeed.

**5. Curse of the Dripping Shadows**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must immerse themselves in complete darkness for an hour, embracing the shadows that follow them. This ritual forces them to confront their fears of darkness, allowing _Remove Curse_ to dispel the shadowy presence.

**6. Curse of Wilted Vitality**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must consume a fresh plant or flower, from the waste to symbolize the renewal of life. This act of consuming vitality helps them reconnect with their own life force, enabling _Remove Curse_ to lift the withering effect.

**7. Curse of Flickering Sight**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must cover their eyes with a cloth soaked in goatsblood, staying blindfolded for an entire night. This needs to be done three times in three different times in three different places and with three different goats. This symbolic acceptance of darkness prepares them for _Remove Curse_, which can then restore their sight.

**8. Curse of Endless Hunger**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must go without food for an entire day, fasting to prove their resilience against hunger. This intentional deprivation allows _Remove Curse_ to end their insatiable appetite.

**9. Curse of the Hollow Voice**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must sing or recite a poem aloud, even if it’s difficult, and be heard by someone else. This effort to be vocal will empower _Remove Curse_ to restore their voice.

**10. Curse of the Creeping Chill**

- **Extra Step:** The cursed must spend time near a fire or in a warm place, embracing the warmth and allowing it to seep into their bones. This warmth prepares them for _Remove Curse_, allowing it to lift the chill from their body.
