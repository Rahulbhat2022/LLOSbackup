Raw Arraknir is the langauge of of raw magic. Currently magic is held together by the old forces of nature. In speech it will be close to akkadian. Speaking the words in this languages directly translates to magical spells of high power. Any being of influenced by the old ones will use these.  
The usage of these come at a cost though. Making you lose more and more of yourself and serving more and more the forgotten ones.
