**The Hollow Reach**  
**Overview:**  
The Hollow Reach is a vast, ancient forest caught between **decay and refusal to die**. Its trees are unnaturally tall, their bark gnarled with bark-scars that whisper when brushed. Light rarely reaches the forest floor. The air is dampYou may craft oils of Tier II. Requires level 8., sweet with rot, and heavy with Ether-taint. It is **not quite wild**, but not tame either — something old, broken, and aware lives in every inch.  
Though largely avoided by the people of Haven, the Hollow Reach is whispered about in hushed tones, said to be home to **things that still cling to their power**, domains carved into reality like scar tissue.  
￼Hidden in these forests are the 3 domains. They stay away from the hollow crown at all costs as they will absolutly kill them.  
**🕯️** **Domain 1: The Bloatwood — Domain of the Hag,** **Mother Muckrot**  
**Theme:** Swamp decay, disease, gluttony, illusion

- **Description:** A bubbling swamp hidden in the heart of the forest, where trees weep tar and frogs croak in backwards voices. The ground is soft with thick, **flesh-like moss** and the water glows faintly green.
- **Ruler:** _Mother Muckrot_, a bloated, long-armed hag with a face that stretches into a maw when she screams. She **brews dreams and curses** in cauldrons sunk into the earth, trading secrets and futures for teeth, nails, or songs unsung.
- **Her Power:** She feeds on **disease, despair, and addiction**, offering travelers salvation from suffering — always at a greater price.

**Domain 2: The Horngrave — Domain of** **The Antlered Sorrow**  
**Theme:** Primal fear, bone cults, forgotten rites, animalistic spirits

- **Description:** This part of the forest feels **wrongly quiet**. The trees are bone-white and the air is choked with the scent of fur and iron. **Skulls and totems** hang from branches. The canopy is sparse, but the light here is always dusky red.
- **Ruler:** _The Antlered Sorrow_, a silent figure clad in leather and fur, with the great skull of a **giant stag** covering his head. He never speaks — his servants do for him — and **blood always drips** from his antlers.
- **His Power:** He represents **forgotten hunts, old gods of tooth and claw, and the pact between man and beast**. He gives strength in exchange for submission.
 
**Domain 3: The Silkdeep — Domain of** **N'yelthara**  
**Theme:** Spiders, webs of fate, domination, secrets

- **Description:** The forest turns narrow and tall here — the trees grow too close together, their branches fused with thick webbing that **hums in the wind**. Strange symbols crawl across bark. Everything smells like musk and venom.
- **Ruler:** _Matron N'yelthara_, a towering spider-woman with a beautiful, veiled face and a **body of chitin and silk**, suspended in her great weblair like a terrible queen. She weaves **truths and lies** into her webs and speaks only when amused.
- **Her Power:** She is **the master of manipulation, obsession, and surveillance**. Her web touches every corner of the forest — **nothing escapes her gaze**.
