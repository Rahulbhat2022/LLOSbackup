**Abjuration – Power Word: Banish**

- **Minor (3rd)** – **Power Word: Repel** – A creature with **50 HP or less** is **pushed 30 feet away** and cannot approach for 1 minute.
- **Major (5th)** – **Power Word: Ward** – A creature with **100 HP or less** is **shunted into the Ethereal Plane** for 1 minute.
- **Greater (7th)** – **Power Word: Exile** – A creature with **150 HP or less** is **banished to its home plane** for 1 minute.
- **Supreme (9th)** – **Power Word: Banish** – A creature with **250 HP or less** is **permanently banished** to its home plane.
 
**Conjuration – Power Word: Summon**

- **Minor (3rd)** – **Power Word: Call** – Instantly summon **a loyal minion** with up to **50 HP** for 1 minute.
- **Major (5th)** – **Power Word: Command** – Summon a **powerful entity** with up to **100 HP** that obeys you for 10 minutes.
- **Greater (7th)** – **Power Word: Legion** – Summon **two creatures with up to 150 HP each** or **one with 300 HP** for 1 hour.
- **Supreme (9th)** – **Power Word: Army** – Summon **five creatures with 200 HP each** or **one with 500 HP** for 24 hours.
 
**Divination – Power Word: Know**

- **Minor (3rd)** – **Power Word: Reveal** – Instantly learn a target’s **alignment, current hit points, and resistances**.
- **Major (5th)** – **Power Word: Sight** – Instantly learn a target’s **location, true form, and surface thoughts**.
- **Greater (7th)** – **Power Word: Truth** – The target is **forced to speak only the truth** for 1 hour.
- **Supreme (9th)** – **Power Word: Omniscience** – You learn **everything the target knows**, including memories, secrets, and future intentions.
 
**Enchantment – Power Word: Dominate**

- **Minor (3rd)** – **Power Word: Daze** – A creature with **50 HP or less** is **stunned** for 1 round.
- **Major (5th)** – **Power Word: Fear** – A creature with **100 HP or less** is **terrified** for 1 minute.
- **Greater (7th)** – **Power Word: Command** – A creature with **150 HP or less** obeys **one command of up to 10 words**.
- **Supreme (9th)** – **Power Word: Subjugate** – A creature with **250 HP or less** is **charmed permanently**.
 
**Evocation – Power Word: Annihilate**

- **Minor (3rd)** – **Power Word: Blast** – A creature with **50 HP or less** takes **30 force damage**.
- **Major (5th)** – **Power Word: Scorch** – A creature with **100 HP or less** takes **60 fire damage**.
- **Greater (7th)** – **Power Word: Destruction** – A creature with **150 HP or less** takes **100 force damage**.
- **Supreme (9th)** – **Power Word: Annihilate** – A creature with **250 HP or less** takes **200 force damage**. If this reduces them to 0 HP, they are **disintegrated**, leaving only ash.
 
**Illusion – Power Word: Conceal**

- **Minor (3rd)** – **Power Word: Blur** – A creature with **50 HP or less** becomes **invisible for 1 minute**.
- **Major (5th)** – **Power Word: Vanish** – A creature with **100 HP or less** becomes **invisible for 1 hour**.
- **Greater (7th)** – **Power Word: Phantasm** – A creature with **150 HP or less** is **permanently invisible** to anyone who fails a DC 20 Intelligence check.
- **Supreme (9th)** – **Power Word: Unmake** – A creature with **250 HP or less** is erased from **memory, history, and reality**.
 
**Necromancy – Power Word: Death**

- **Minor (3rd)** – **Power Word: Drain** – A creature with **50 HP or less** takes **20 necrotic damage**, and you gain that much HP.
- **Major (5th)** – **Power Word: Wither** – A creature with **100 HP or less** **loses half its HP** instantly.
- **Greater (7th)** – **Power Word: Slay** – A creature with **150 HP or less** **instantly dies**.
- **Supreme (9th)** – **Power Word: Erase** – A creature with **250 HP or less** **instantly dies**, and cannot be resurrected.
 
**Transmutation – Power Word: Transform**

- **Minor (3rd)** – **Power Word: Shift** – A creature with **50 HP or less** is **polymorphed into a CR 1 creature** for 1 minute.
- **Major (5th)** – **Power Word: Beast** – A creature with **100 HP or less** is **permanently turned into a harmless animal**.
- **Greater (7th)** – **Power Word: Metamorphosis** – A creature with **150 HP or less** is transformed into **any other creature of equal or lesser HP** permanently.
- **Supreme (9th)** – **Power Word: Ascend** – A creature with **250 HP or less** **evolves into a more powerful form**, gaining **+4 to all ability scores** and **new features of your choice**.
