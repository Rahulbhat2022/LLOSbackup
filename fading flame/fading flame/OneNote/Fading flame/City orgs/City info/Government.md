The ledge and the heart
The governing institution of the ledge also governs the heart. This part of the system is the one that works like most governments in the world. They are concerned over themselves though and have legislations that protect the people
They mostly have the wardens that protect the people but the enforcers are enterily contracted from the iron clutch.
The strand
This is a newly set up area where alot of investments have gone into industry and factories. Therefore most of the legislation is protecting factory and their owners with very little regard to the workers. 
Veils end
Veils end is a anarchist state that is allowed within the confines of the government. They pay their share in taxes and in turn are very little governed. There are very few laws here that protect anyone. People that walk the the veils end have to be careful.
The scourge
The poorest region. There are laws here set by the people but no enforcers of them. People are just trying to survive.
