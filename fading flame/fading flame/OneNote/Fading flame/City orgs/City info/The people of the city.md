**High Born** 1%

- Ring(stamp)**:** A _crown_ on the 2 suns eclipsing each other.
- High borns live in the noble courts of solace. They live a life of luxury while acutally also being plagued by devils from a bygone era. Because of their consumption driven more and more towards their extremities. They embody excess in any shape or form. 
**Wall Born** 9%

- **Description:** Living just outside the High Born’s inner city, the Wall Born are so named because they reside close to the fortified walls that both protect the High Born and restrict the lower classes. The Wall Born consist of moderately successful merchants, skilled laborers, and clerical workers. They’re generally of races considered “good because they are useful,” like dwarves, and gnomes.
- **Role in Society** : They are the people responible for keeping the city functioning. Trade works taht "power" the city. Heating, architecthure. Since there is much less construction nowadays many have gone into working for the government. 
- **Stamp:** A _fortified gate_
