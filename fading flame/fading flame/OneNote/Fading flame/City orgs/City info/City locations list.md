**Crafts & Services**

- **The Ember Anvil** – Renowned blacksmith's forge that works with rare metals (Voidglass smithing optional)
- **Glasswing Atelier** – Jewelry and magical trinkets crafted by a retired spellwright
- **The Coil & Crucible** – Alchemical supply and potion shop; neutral ground for rogue alchemists
- **The Graven Needle** – Tattooist using arcane ink, occasionally works with smugglers
- **Ashroad Smithy(scourge)** – Cheaper forge in the low districts, often refurbishes broken war-gear
   

**Communication & Transit**

- **The Gilded Post** – Central post office with messenger hawks, arcane courier circles, and underground runners
- **Veilwalkers’ Waypoint** – Teleportation nexus (heavily restricted to those with permits)
 
**Commerce & Trade**

- **hollow Market(the scourge)** – Mixed legal/illegal open-air market; some stalls shift depending on the time of day
- **The Thirteen Lanterns Bazaar(the strand)** – Enclosed, upscale trade district where merchants sell artifacts, fashion, and enchanted items
- **Goldspine Bank** – Vaulted financial hub; controls debt, credit, and "arcane collateral"
- **Dustmeadow Docks**(scourge)– Imports, fish, smugglers, and travelers—has warehouses and a fishmonger guildhall
 
**Education & Knowledge**

- **The Quill Scriptorium( in the spires)** – Place for copying texts, magical research, and quiet reading. Restricted to the highborns and mages.
- **The Obsidian Shelf** – Private library collecting books for monsters and bloodhunter related, funded by the Hollow Crown
- **The monestary library-** for anyone who is passing, monk disciplines
- **Arvid voss personal library-** medical journals
- **The valcort library-** Made public, knowledge about the city and some magical sections.     
**Funeral & Faith (non-faction)**

- **The Shrine of Forgotten Names** – A mysterious, neutral shrine where people pray for lost loved ones, bury or burn.
- **The Ferryman’s Hollow** – the place where the scourge drop their bodies of the dead into the water. For the current to carry the bodies away.
