**Veil’s End –** **Where the Fog Never Lifts**  
Veil’s End lies curled against the edge of the city like a secret too dangerous to speak aloud. The air here never quite clears; it’s perpetually choked by incense smoke, coal haze, and the heavy breath of something cant put your finger on. . Buildings lean together as if siloutes leaning towards eachother whispering, their warped eaves and cracked windows forming labyrinthine silhouette tha cant be made out through the smog: Veil’s End is a place where things go missing — names, bodies, memories — and where people survive by being unseen or unremarkable.  
This is not a slum in the traditional sense. It is something stranger. People here barter in secrets and scars, not coins. Superstition is a second currency, and nearly everyone wears a charm, tattoo, or token against unseen eyes. Few speak loudly. Few walk the same path twice.  
The city guard avoids the place. Not out of fear — but because it suits the Highborn to let Veil’s End eat its own.
