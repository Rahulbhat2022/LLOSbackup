**The Scourge – The Drowned Slum**  
The Scourge is where the city’s poorest struggle to survive, a sprawling, makeshift extension of the docks that clings to the city’s edge like barnacles on rotting wood. Built on half-sunken platforms and supported by rusted beams driven into the seabed, the district is a labyrinth of shoddy wooden walkways, sagging homes, and tilting shacks bound together by desperation. Here, the air is thick with salt, sweat, and the ever-present stench of decay, for the tides do not cleanse the Scourge—they drown it.  
The nobility despised the sight of this wretched district, the way its filth and misery clashed with the city's grandeur. Under the guise of charity, they lobbied for a great dome to be built, a grand architectural feat that would shield the Scourge from the worst of the sea’s wrath. But midway through construction, progress halted. A mage—seeking noble favor—created stationary illusions, elegant veils that could be placed on balconies to erase the Scourge from view. No longer forced to acknowledge the district’s existence, the nobility quickly abandoned the dome project, leaving behind only a half-finished wall.  
This half-wall became a curse rather than a blessing. Now, when the tide rises and floods the Scourge, the trapped waters fester, pooling in alleys and homes for days before they can be drained. Illness spreads faster, rot claims more structures, and desperation deepens. The nobles, blissfully unaware behind their illusions, no longer even pretend to care. The Scourge is out of sight—why should it ever be in mind?
    
**Dr. Arvid Voss – The Plague Doctor of the Scourge**  
Dr. Arvid Voss is an enigmatic figure who has long been the lifeline for the residents of the Scourge, a quiet, relentless force in a district where death is constant and health is fleeting. Known simply as _the Plague Doctor_, he wears the traditional mask of his profession—its long beak shaped like that of a crow and filled with herbs, a scent designed to ward off disease. His eyes, obscured by the black glass lenses, are unreadable, and the rest of his face is perpetually hidden beneath layers of leather and cloth. To the people of the Scourge, he is a harbinger of relief, but also a symbol of the suffering that grips their lives.  
The hospital where Dr. Voss works is little more than a ramshackle building at the heart of the district, its creaking walls sagging with age and dampness. It is always overcrowded, and his patients never seem to lessen. Sick children, broken men, women struggling to survive in the filth—all find their way to his doorstep. The place is a chaotic blur of moans, whispers, and the occasional scream. Though crude and cramped, it is the only place in the Scourge that offers any form of medical attention, and as such, Dr. Voss is revered by the residents, though they fear his methods. He operates under no delusions—there is no time for gentleness here, no luxury for slow healing. His treatments are often invasive, using whatever methods are necessary to keep the waves of disease at bay.  
Yet, beneath the mask, Dr. Voss is a man with a mind as sharp as his instruments. He has come to understand the Scourge's grim truths in ways most others cannot fathom: the district is a breeding ground for sickness, both physical and societal. Disease is a mirror of the moral decay that surrounds him, and sometimes he wonders if the two are intrinsically linked. When he’s not tending to his patients, he spends hours in his office, surrounded by old books, questionable tomes on medicine, and jars of preserved organs. He rarely speaks of his past, but there are whispers that his medical knowledge is not purely conventional. Whatever his secrets, one thing is clear: Dr. Voss is indispensable to the Scourge, and he will stop at nothing to keep his hospital running—no matter the cost to himself or those who cross his path.

**Edgar Brine, the Keeper of Tides(devil of storm)**  
Edgar Brine is not just a fisherman; he is a living legend in the Scourge, an enigmatic figure whose knowledge of the sea and its depths has made him an indispensable part of the community. Born into the grime and struggle of the docks, Elgar has lived through decades of hardship and change, witnessing the rise of the floating extensions of the city, the political games of the nobles, and the ebb and flow of the Scourge’s population. He is the only person who truly knows the tides, both literal and metaphorical, and he has become the unofficial leader of the Scourge’s residents. Despite his humble, weathered appearance, Elgar carries a quiet power, respected not only for his deep understanding of the sea, but for his ability to speak to its hidden currents—both of water and of society.  
Elgar’s home, a creaky shack built on stilts, is known as the **Tide’s Refuge**, a tavern and a gathering place for those seeking solace from the crushing pressures of life in the Scourge. His door is always open to those in need. While he may not hold political power, the people of the Scourge turn to him for guidance. Elgar’s reputation has reached beyond the docks; there are whispers in the alleys of nobles who seek him out for his rare wisdom, and some even say that his dealings with the _deep waters_ go beyond what can be explained by mere superstition. It’s said that he has the ability to read the tides in ways that reveal truths no one else can see.  
He has connections to the _unspeakable_, to things lurking beneath the waves. It is said he has a pact with the mysterious, ancient powers that dwell in the depths of the city’s waters— Edgar has been known to occasionally provide _favors_ to those desperate enough to seek his help—offers that are not given lightly, for they come at a steep price. In return for his knowledge and his power, he demands loyalty from those who cross his path, and woe to anyone who betrays him. To be in Elgar’s debt is to be bound by the tides, forever under his watchful eye.
