**The Unblinking**  
**Devouts of the Unblinking One**  
_“Eyes that never close. Minds that never rest.”_  
The Unblinking are a **chain of strange, glowing-eyed wanderers** scattered through the alleys, ruins, and gutters of Haven. They are seen as little more than street lunatics by most—cloaked in rags, muttering riddles, staring unblinking into the void. Yet beneath their quiet presence lies an unsettling **consistency**.  
They are **devotees of the Unblinking One**, a forgotten entity of perception and watching, who they claim sees all paths and never sleeps. They see themselves not as people, but as **its scattered eyes**—placed across the city to observe, to record, and occasionally to intervene.
   

The unblinking are invisible and ever present. Currently spreading across the city they are increasing their network to percieve it all.
