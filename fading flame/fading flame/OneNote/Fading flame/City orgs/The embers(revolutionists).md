**The Embers**

- **Description**: A rebellious faction of workers, the Embers are composed of individuals who have been burned by the system — former Ashenfolk who have become radicalized after being pushed too far by the oppressive ruling class. They live in the abandoned districts, organizing protests, secret meetings, and occasional violent acts to challenge the city's socio-economic divide.
- **Role in Society**: They are a loose underground resistance, seeking to overthrow the existing hierarchy and demand fair treatment for all. The Embers are often seen as terrorists or traitors by the higher classes but viewed as champions of justice by the oppressed.
- **Key Features**: They have no formal organization but operate in small cells. Their actions range from sabotage, theft, and espionage to spreading anti-government propaganda. They are constantly hunted by city guards and spies, but they remain a persistent thorn in the side of the ruling classes.
