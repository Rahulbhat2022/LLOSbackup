**Overview**  
Tucked into the cliffs just beyond the edge of the city—hidden yet accessible—the **Emberveil Monastery** is a place of **serenity, silence, and shadowed strength**. While the world churns with politics, conflict, and divine warfare, Emberveil remains steadfast in its purpose: **perfecting the self**, through discipline, harmony, and mastery of the **inner flame**.  
Though **overlooked by most**, Emberveil is far from defenseless. Its monks walk among the people, robed and hooded, gathering wisdom or offering aid. Those who would challenge them quickly learn: even the quietest monk can be **lethal as lightning**.
 
**Population**

- **~40 Acolytes** (level 1–2): Training in basic martial arts, meditation, and elemental control.
- **~8 Adepts** (level 3–8): Each has chosen a _Way_ and studies it rigorously.
- **3 Masters** (level 8–12): Each embodies their chosen path so fully they’re considered living legends.
- **The firekeeper (Lvl 14+)**: A serene, aging figure shrouded in veils and mystery. Speaks rarely but when they do, their words echo like thunder(esme)
 
**The Library of the Burning Paths**  
Deep in the monastery’s stone halls lies a circular chamber sealed by an intricate **flame-lock**. This library is lined with scrolls, tomes, and tablets that chronicle **all known Ways of the Monk**—some rare, some thought lost. These texts contain not only theory and philosophy but **training rituals, breathing patterns, combat forms**, and esoteric meditations unique to each path.
