# The Entertainer District

**"The best cage is the one that sings."**

---

## Overview

The Entertainer District is the Hearth's exhaust valve. It is where the Marktfolk spend the coin they earned the other six days of the week, where they laugh and gamble and watch things burn and forget, for a few hours, that tomorrow they will be back at the forge or the counter or the vat. It is loud, brightly lit, and entirely controlled by one man who has decided that joy is a commodity like any other.

The district's main avenues are lined with theatres, puppet shows, card halls, performance stages, arcane light displays, and establishments that sell everything from spiced wine to low-grade illusion smoke. The buildings are painted in warmer colours than the rest of the Hearth — deep reds and tarnished golds that look expensive from a distance and reveal their cheapness up close. Music spills from almost every doorway. The crowds are thick in the evenings and almost continuous on rest days.

Beneath the entertainment runs a second economy: unofficial gambling dens, unlicensed performers working the back-alleys for scraps, and a brisk trade in forged invitations to "private performances" — a euphemism that covers a wide range of things the Craft Council would prefer not to legislate.

---

## Dorian Faux — Head of the Entertainer District

Dorian Faux is the most charming man in the Hearth, and he has worked very hard to make that true. He is Wall Born — born into moderate clerical comfort and educated to a level beyond what his station required — but he has spent twenty years building a persona so convincingly Marktfolk that even people who know the truth forget it while he's in the room. He dresses beautifully but not *too* beautifully. He drinks in the same taverns as everyone else. He laughs at the same jokes. He has a handshake for everyone and remembers the name of your mother.

He controls the Entertainer District through the **Performers' Collective**, a licensing guild that ostensibly protects artists' rights and ensures fair venue contracts. In practice, it ensures that no performer works in the Hearth without his approval, and no venue runs an act he hasn't personally cleared. Performers who push back find their licenses suddenly complicated by clerical errors. Venues that refuse his terms find their noise ordinance complaints processed with unusual speed.

His real enterprise is blackmail — not crude, violent blackmail, but the long and patient kind. Dorian's theatres have private boxes. His card halls have private rooms. His establishment records nothing — but Dorian does. For seventeen years he has kept meticulous, encrypted notes on what the Craft Council members, the Warden captains, and several Wall Born families do when they think they are merely relaxing. He has never once used this information. The fact that he has it is enough.

He is also, genuinely, a man who loves performance. His own productions are technically precise and emotionally hollow, which is perhaps appropriate.

**Voice:** Warm, practised, always at the exact right volume. He tends to touch people briefly on the arm when making a point. When he laughs it sounds completely real. He has rehearsed it.

## Notable Locations

- **The Grand Velvet** — The district's flagship theatre. Two hundred seats, a soulgold-enhanced stage that can simulate weather and crowd effects, and private boxes that Dorian rents at a premium. The shows are spectacular and slightly safe.
- **The Tinderbox** — A more intimate venue where newer, edgier performers play to smaller audiences. Dorian uses it to identify talent early and sign them before they know what the contract costs.
- **The Brass Hand** — A card hall with a respectable front and a private floor that operates on an invite-only system. Where the real money — and the real information — lives.
- **Alley Row** — The unlicensed stretch behind the main avenues where performers who couldn't get Collective approval work for coin thrown on the ground. Dorian tolerates it. Desperation performs for free.

## Quest Hook — The Understudies

A fire at the Grand Velvet destroyed a portion of Dorian's archive — or so the official report says. Three people saw him carry crates out himself beforehand. One of them is a young illusionist who won't stop talking about it, and Dorian has quietly arranged for her next venue booking to fall through, and the one after that, and the one after that. She needs someone to help her figure out what was in those crates and why it was worth more than a building.
