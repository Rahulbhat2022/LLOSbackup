# The Ink District

**"Every word written down is a debt. Most people just don't know it yet."**

---

## Overview

The Ink District is quieter than its function suggests. It occupies a cluster of close-built, narrow-fronted buildings where scribes work behind frosted glass windows and the only sounds are the scratch of pens, the press of seal-stamps, and occasionally the faint arcane hum of a contract being magically ratified. Lanterns burn long hours here. The district produces the written infrastructure of the entire Hearth: guild contracts, trade agreements, property transfers, debt instruments, licensing applications, and the tens of thousands of small daily documents that make the city's economy legible to itself.

There is also a second industry — less legal and considerably more colourful: the arcane tattoo parlours that cluster along the district's eastern edge. Arcane tattooing is licensed work in the Hearth; the inks can carry minor enchantments, message-sigils, identity verification marks, or decorative spellwork that responds to the wearer. The regulation of which tattoos are permitted and which cross into "unauthorised arcane self-modification" sits, conveniently, with the Ink District's head guild.

The scribes and the tattooists do not mix socially. They are acutely aware of each other.

## Prudence Vellum — Head of the Ink District

Prudence Vellum has been head of the **Scrivener's Authority** for nine years and has never once needed to do anything that would be described, in a court, as criminal. This is either a testament to her ethics or her caution, and people who know her well would say the latter.

She is a compact woman in her late forties with ink-stained fingers she no longer bothers to fully clean and an expression of patient attention that never quite becomes warmth. She sits with her spine perfectly straight. She listens more than she speaks, and when she speaks she is more precise than comfortable.

The Scrivener's Authority's official function is to certify licensed scribes and maintain quality standards for contracted documents. Its unofficial function — one Prudence has cultivated for nine years — is that every significant contract produced in the Hearth passes through an Authority-certified scribe, and Prudence has a standing arrangement with seven of her senior scribes to provide her with a copy of anything interesting. Not everything. She is selective. She keeps what matters.

Her collection is stored in a system she calls the **Ledger** — a personal archive of partial copies, summaries, and encrypted references to documents that give her context on every significant business relationship in the Hearth. She has never sold information from it. She has, on a few occasions, quietly mentioned to a Craft Council member that she was aware of a particular agreement, in a tone that made the nature of the remark perfectly clear. This has preserved her position through two changes of Council composition and one attempt to revoke her Authority charter.

She is also quietly fascinated by the Ring, which generates no written documentation that she has ever found. This bothers her more than she would admit.

**Voice:** Precise, low, and unhurried. She has a habit of completing other people's sentences correctly and then falling silent, as if the point has been made.

## Notable Locations

- **The Scrivener's Hall** — The Authority's headquarters and the district's largest building. The front offices handle certification and licensing. The back offices, which no one unauthorised has entered in nine years, are where Prudence works.
- **The Ratification Chamber** — A semi-public room where important contracts are formally witnessed and magically sealed. The sealing process produces a faint chime and a brief golden light. It is considered auspicious.
- **Needlepoint Alley** — The eastern corridor where the tattoo parlours cluster. The work ranges from functional (caste-verification marks, trade-sigil stamps) to elaborate decorative. Some parlours quietly offer enchantments they do not advertise.
- **The Counter** — An unmarked document transfer service run out of a stationery shop, used by people who need contracts reviewed without going through the Authority. Run by a retired scribe who knows enough to charge double and not ask.

## Quest Hook — Unsigned

A debt contract surfaces in the Ring's underground economy — a document that, if genuine, would transfer a large property in the Furnace District from a recently deceased worker to a creditor he apparently never met in life. The signature looks right. The seal looks right. Prudence Vellum has not produced a copy. She would like one, quietly, and is willing to offer access to things she knows in exchange. The party is asked to authenticate or disprove a document that powerful people on both sides of the dispute want to be true.
