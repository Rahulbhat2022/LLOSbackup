# The Alchemy District

**"A potion for every problem. The problem is the price."**

---

## Overview

The Alchemy District smells wrong in a way that is difficult to describe. Not bad, exactly — there are sweet notes and mineral notes and occasionally something that smells almost like rain — but it shifts from block to block, layering in ways that make the sinuses uncertain. The district is built around a dense cluster of laboratories, reagent suppliers, compounding workshops, and licensed dispensaries. Steam vents from narrow pipes at irregular intervals. The building walls are stained in faint multicoloured rings where vapour has settled and dried for years.

The district serves the entire Hearth and much of Solace beyond it. Healers' potions, alchemical solvents, fire-starters, preservation compounds, arcane reagents, and a dozen categories of components that other districts' trades require — all of it passes through or originates here. The Alchemy District sits at the intersection of craft and need, and it prices itself accordingly.

Alchemists are a temperamental population. Many have academic backgrounds that leave them convinced they are more intelligent than everyone in the room. The guild system here is more fractious than in other districts — there are a dozen specialist sub-guilds covering different schools of compounding — but the master licensing authority sits with one woman, and the sub-guilds learned long ago that she controls the supply chain.

---

## Sera Brenn — Head of the Alchemy District

Sera Brenn is brilliant in the way that a boiling retort is brilliant: precise, productive, and dangerous to stand too close to. She controls the district through the **Brenn Reagent Authority**, a body that issues compounding licenses, certifies reagent quality, and — most critically — serves as the sole licensed importer of several key components that cannot be sourced locally. Without her supply agreements, roughly forty percent of the district's practitioners cannot legally make anything.

She is a slight woman in her mid-forties who has the distracted manner of someone performing three calculations simultaneously while talking to you. She keeps her workspace in a state of organised chaos that nobody else can navigate. She has never missed a certification deadline in her career, never issued a recall that wasn't her competitors' product, and never been linked to anything illegal by anything so pedestrian as *evidence*.

Her actual experiment — the one she has been running for eleven years — is this: her lowest-price product tier, the "Brenn Standard" line of basic healing compounds sold through licensed dispensaries to Marktfolk and Hearthfolk who cannot afford the premium range, contains trace quantities of a stabilising compound she invented herself. It is harmless at individual doses. Accumulated over years of regular consumption, it creates a mild physiological dependency and, in Sera's estimation, a measurable increase in docility and reduced stress-response. She is not making people ill. She considers herself to be, in a meaningful sense, making the Hearth run more smoothly. She has seventeen years of data. She is considering publishing it, anonymised, to the Wall Born academic circuit.

**Voice:** Precise and slightly impatient. She corrects people when they use imprecise language. She is not unkind — she simply finds accuracy more interesting than feelings.

## Notable Locations

- **The Brenn Authority Hall** — A clean, well-lit administrative building where licenses are issued and reagent certificates approved. Everything here is documented twice.
- **The Crucible Row** — The district's main commercial strip, where dozens of alchemists run storefronts from the same buildings as their labs. The competition is fierce and occasionally explosive.
- **The Sorting House** — Where imported reagents arrive, are tested, certified, and redistributed. Sera's people control every step. The ledgers are immaculate and miss certain shipments entirely.
- **Sub-level Seven** — Not on any official district map. Sera's private lab, accessible through the Sorting House basement. Contains eleven years of anonymised trial records and the only complete synthesis of the "Standard" compound.

## Quest Hook — The Quieting

A Marktfolk woman whose husband has been on Brenn Standard compounds for eight years has noticed something she cannot name: he used to fight with her. Used to come home from work angry, loud, full of opinions. Now he comes home quiet and agreeable, and it frightens her more than the anger ever did. She brings this to anyone who will listen. Most people tell her she should be grateful. She wants someone to listen differently.
