**The Church of the Eternal Light**  
The Church of the Eternal Light is a rigid, absolute religious institution dedicated to the **Brother Sun**, viewing him as the one true source of divine order. They see the **Sister Sun** as an unnatural corruption—an impure force that spreads magic, chaos, and heresy. Their doctrine is unyielding: only through complete devotion to the Brother Sun can the world remain pure.
 
**Doctrine & Core Beliefs**

1. [[The Brother Sun]] – The guiding force of truth, stability, and divine order. His light brings life, prosperity, and righteousness.
2. [[The Azure sun]] – A deceiver, a false light that leads mortals into corruption.
3. **The Sundering Dogma** – A decree that all magic, sorcery, and arcane knowledge stemming from the Sister Sun is heretical and must be purged.
4. **The Light’s Absolute Truth** – There is no balance, no compromise—only the Brother Sun’s divine authority. Doubt is treason, and questioning the faith is a path to ruin.

They believe the **Sister Sun must one day be extinguished**, and only then will the world be free from corruption.

**Architecture & Design**  
The Grand Cathedral is a **monument of blinding opulence**—golden spires, sunburst mosaics, and walls of pristine white marble. But beneath its beauty lies a structure built for **intimidation and control**.

- **The Sunspire** – The tallest tower, said to be **closer to the Brother Sun than any other place on earth**. Only the Aurearch and the most devout are permitted within.
- **The Hallowed Hall** – The vast, echoing nave where thousands gather for daily worship, illuminated by massive stained-glass windows depicting the Church’s victories.
- **The Purging Grounds** – A courtyard where heretics are tried and purified before the eyes of the faithful. Many leave in ashes.
- **The Luminous Archives** – A labyrinthine library that contains scripture, history, and “corrected” versions of texts. It is heavily restricted—only sanctioned scholars may enter.
- **The Chamber of Dawn** – A golden throne room where the High Solars convene, issuing decrees and guiding the faith.
- **The Sunforges** – A hidden armory where **blessed weapons and relics** are crafted—items said to burn magic on contact.
   

**Secrets & Hidden Depths**  
Beneath its holy veneer, the Grand Cathedral holds **dark secrets**:

- **The Black Sepulcher** – A subterranean prison where the most dangerous heretics and political enemies rot away in eternal darkness.
- **The Solar Vaults** – A treasury of confiscated magical artifacts, kept under lock and key to prevent their use (or, in some cases, to be studied for the Church’s own ends).

**Quest: The Burning Crusade** **(External Conflict – Assault on the Night’s Veil)**  
**Problem:** The Church of the Eternal Light has uncovered evidence of a **Night’s Veil ritual** taking place in an abandoned crypt outside the city. The church believes this ritual is meant to **raise an undead army** and seeks to **launch a full-scale assault** before it can be completed. They call upon the party to aid them in this **holy purge**.  
**Objective:** Aid in—or sabotage—the church’s attack.  
**Key Considerations:**

- Is the ritual actually raising an undead army, or is it something else?
- The attack will **escalate the conflict**, forcing both factions into more open warfare.
- The **party’s role** will determine how the battle unfolds.
