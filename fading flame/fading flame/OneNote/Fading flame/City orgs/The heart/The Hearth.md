# The Hearth

**"Work hard enough and you'll be comfortable. Comfortable enough to stop asking questions."**

---

## Overview

The Hearth is the beating middle of Solace — not its brain, not its bowels, but its hands. This is where the Marktfolk live, labour, and call themselves free. They are the smiths and the potion-brewers, the tailors and the cooks, the ink-scribes who seal the contracts that keep the city moving. They are the thirty percent who believe, genuinely and desperately, that hard work is its own reward.

The Hearth does not have the misery of the Scourge or the menace of Veil's End. It has something more insidious: *contentment*. The streets are kept swept. The lanterns are spelled to warm amber rather than harsh white. The food stalls smell good. The wardens nod to you if your stamp is in order. It is a district that has been very carefully designed to feel *enough* — just enough that its people never look up and wonder what *more* might look like.

Beneath this comfortable surface, the Hearth runs on competition, licensing, and quiet debt. Every trade in every district is controlled by a guild, and every guild has a head, and every head has a seat on the Craft Council. Work without a license and the Iron Clutch comes knocking. Fall behind on your guild dues and your stall is forfeit. The Hearth is not a slum — it simply ensures that no one inside it can ever afford to become a threat.

---

## The Plaza of Ambition

At the geographic and spiritual centre of the Hearth stands the **Plaza of Ambition** — a wide, wheel-shaped square paved in dark stone and bordered by tall iron lampposts that burn year-round. In its exact centre sits the **Eternal Hearth**: an enormous open brazier, ten feet across, fuelled by heat drawn through pipes from the Furnace District below. It has not gone cold in living memory. The Marktfolk consider this a point of pride.

The Plaza is where Solace pretends to offer fairness. Craft licenses are issued at the **Registry Booths** that ring the outer edge — small iron counters staffed by Wall Born clerks who scrutinise your stamp, your guild membership, and your last three months' dues before approving anything. First-time applications take weeks. Renewals are automatic if you can pay.

At the northern edge of the Plaza stands the **Council Hall** — a squat, austere building of pale stone where the Craft Council convenes. From outside it looks functional and unglamorous. Inside, the council chamber is upholstered in deep red and lit by suspended soulgold fixtures that keep the temperature precisely warm. Comfort only for those who have earned the right to govern.

The Plaza is also where public announcements are made, caste checks occasionally occur, and the Iron Clutch parks its enforcers when the Council wants to send a message without sending a message.

---

## Governance — The Craft Council

The Hearth is jointly governed by the **Craft Council**, a body composed of the district head from each of the Hearth's slices. The Council sets trade law, licensing rates, and inter-district tarrifs. In theory every head has equal vote. In practice, the heads of the wealthier districts — the Gilded Tongue, the Alchemy District — carry disproportionate weight because they control the debt that the smaller districts run on.

The Craft Council has no standing army. Its enforcement arm is a two-tier system:

- **The Wardens** — uniformed keepers of the peace. They patrol the main avenues, respond to disputes, and handle licensing violations. They are salaried through the Council and technically accountable to it. They are not corrupt so much as *practical* — they enforce the rules that exist and do not ask whether the rules are just.
- **The Iron Clutch** — contracted private enforcers, hired district-by-district when the Wardens are insufficient or when a district head wants deniability. They are brutal, professional, and expensive. Districts that can afford them tend to use them liberally. Districts that cannot learn to keep order themselves.

Decisions that affect the whole Hearth go to the Craft Council. Decisions within a district are the district head's alone. This is sold as autonomy. It is also how the heads keep their people from seeking redress elsewhere.

## District Layout

From the Plaza of Ambition, the Hearth radiates outward in eight distinct slices, each defined by trade and separated by wide spoke-roads that funnel foot traffic toward the centre. The boundaries between districts are marked by painted street lines, guild banners hung between buildings, and the subtle change in smell as one trade gives way to another.

Moving clockwise from the northern spoke:

| District | Trade |
|---|---|
| [[The Furnace District]] | Forge, smithing, and central heating |
| [[The Alchemy District]] | Potions, reagents, and experimental compounding |
| [[The Bone and Needle Quarter]] | Medicine, surgery, and arcane body-work |
| [[The Gilded Tongue]] | Trade licensing, moneylending, and commerce |
| [[The Chimney Ward]] | Food guilds, cooking, and communal kitchens |
| [[The Ink District]] | Scribes, contract-writing, and arcane tattooing |
| [[The Weavers Quarter]] | Textiles, fashion, and caste-coded garments |
| [[The Entertainer District]] | Performance, theatre, spectacle, and vice |

Below all of it — beneath the streets, beneath the spoke-roads, beneath the respectability — runs [[The Ring]].

## The Ring

The Ring is the part of the Hearth that the Craft Council does not officially know about and unofficially depends upon. It is an underground fighting circuit that draws blood money from across every district, gives the Marktfolk somewhere to put their frustration that is not toward the people above them, and quietly launders coin for half the district heads who pretend they have never heard of it.

Its operation tunnels under the Plaza of Ambition itself — there is a grim irony in that which nobody discusses.

## Atmosphere

- Stone streets scrubbed weekly by hired Ashenfolk crews. The dirt is always *present* but never *visible*.
- Guild banners in each district's colour hang from every second building. You always know whose turf you are on.
- The smell of coal and cooking and alchemical smoke drifts differently depending on which spoke you are walking.
- Wardens are visible but not aggressive. They watch your stamp. They watch your hands.
- The Eternal Hearth in the Plaza throws heat and orange light across everything at night. It is beautiful. It is also a constant reminder that the warmth is rationed and the Furnace District controls the valves.
