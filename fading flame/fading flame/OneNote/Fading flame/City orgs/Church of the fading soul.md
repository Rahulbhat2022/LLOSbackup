**The Night’s Veil**  
_"To be forgotten is the only true death."_  
A solemn and enigmatic order of **clerics and paladins**, the Night’s Veil does not worship gods, but rather **serves the echoes of the dead**. They believe that every life, no matter how small or great, must be preserved in some way—through memory, record, or utility.  
They are not necromancers in the traditional sense, but **keepers of death’s final echoes**, wielding the remains of the deceased as tools to shape the present. To them, **undeath is not inherently evil**—it is simply another **use of a fading resource**, and they abhor **waste** above all else.  
**Their Goddess: Y’shira, the Last Whisper**  
_"She does not answer prayers, only listens."_

**Sanctuary of the Veil (Place of Worship)**  
A temple unlike any other, the **Sanctuary of the Veil** is a place of **eternal silence**, where the dead are **recorded, preserved, and put to use**.  
**Appearance & Structure**

- A vast, **cathedral-like mausoleum**, built from **black stone veined with pale silver**, resembling veins of bone.
- **The main chamber** is a vast hall filled with **stone sarcophagi**, each containing a body waiting to serve its final purpose.
- The **ceiling is covered in thousands of small chimes**, each one resonating softly when a soul is properly laid to rest.

**Roles & Practices**

- **The Echo Vaults** – A massive archive of **memories**, where the written records of the dead are kept. Their words, deeds, and final thoughts are stored here.
- **The Ossuary Guard** – A collection of **sacred remains**, animated only in times of dire need, ensuring that no death is ever wasted in vain.
- **The Last Rite** – A ceremony in which the Night’s Veil **retrieves forgotten dead** from battlefields, streets, and abandoned places to ensure their memory is preserved.

**Beliefs on Death & Undeath**

- **"The first death is the body; the second is the memory."**
- The Night’s Veil does not raise **mindless undead**; rather, they **animate bodies temporarily** with their last lingering memories so that they may serve in death before **final rest**.
- The greatest sin is to **abandon a corpse**, leaving it to be forgotten and wasted.
 
**The Night’s Veil in Society**

- Feared by the superstitious, respected by the pragmatic.
- They are often seen **walking battlefields** after wars, retrieving bodies to ensure their deaths **were not in vain**.
- Many rulers **begrudgingly respect** them, as their work prevents plagues and ensures history is recorded.
- Some believe they are **harbingers of death**, appearing only before great tragedies.

**Solenne**  
_"Death does not listen, yet we must still speak for the fallen."_  
Mother Solenne is the **solemn and revered leader of the Night’s Veil**.  
**Appearance & Presence**

- **Austere & Imposing** – She wears a **heavy, dark veil**, obscuring her face entirely, an eternal symbol of mourning.
- **Ritualistic Attire** – Her robes are embroidered with **silver threads**, tracing the names of the dead upon them.
- She wear the **Relic of the Veil: Chime of the Final Rest** _(Wondrous Item, Very Rare) around her hand. Its a chain wrapped around her hand._
- **Her Voice is Rarely Heard** – It is said she only speaks when truly necessary, letting the presence of the Veil’s teachings guide others.
- **Eyes of the Departed** – Those who have seen beneath her veil claim that her eyes are **milky and clouded**, as if she has already crossed into death and returned.

**Role & Influence**

- **Keeper of the Ossuary Guard** – She alone decides **which fallen warriors will rise again** to serve in undeath before their final burial.
- **Her Blessing is a Funeral Right** – When she lays a hand upon the dying, they pass without fear, knowing that they will not be forgotten.

**Personality & Beliefs**

- **Unwavering & Methodical** – She does not allow emotion to sway her, but she is not unfeeling.
- **Sees Undeath as a Duty, Not a Curse** – She does not raise the dead lightly; each one must have purpose before they are put to final rest.
- **Despises Waste & Corruption of the Dead** – Necromancers who **defile** the dead rather than honor them are the greatest blasphemers in her eyes.
- **She Will Not Be Worshipped** – Some among the Veil whisper of her wisdom as divine, but she **rebukes** any attempt to deify her.
