**Church of the Eternal Light Questline**  
_"The Sister Sun is a stain upon our world. Only through the Brother Sun’s pure light can we cleanse it."_  
The **Eternal Light** is a strict, lawful church that seeks to root out corruption, strengthen its influence, and eliminate heretical beliefs. These quests involve exposing heretics, protecting church assets, and striking at the Night’s Veil before they can spread their doctrine.
 
**Quest 1: The Flickering Faith (Internal Conflict – Rooting Out Doubt)**  
**Problem:** A small monastery under the Eternal Light’s banner harbors a priest who secretly studies forbidden texts. The church believes he is a heretic but has no proof. If word spreads that even their own priests doubt the faith, it could cause unrest. In truth, the priest is working for the **Lady of Corpses**, using church grounds to conduct human experiments and necromancy—ironically, to weaken the **Night’s Veil**, not strengthen it. If exposed, the scandal will shake public trust.  
**Objective:** Investigate the priest and determine his true beliefs.  
**Choices:**

- **Turn him in to the church** (He is executed, and the church’s power grows).
- **Help him escape** (You earn an ally, but the church grows suspicious).
- **Convince him to abandon his studies** (Preserves stability but risks future relapse).
 
**Quest 2: Shadows in the Streets (External Conflict – The Night’s Veil Influence)**  
**Problem:** **Eternal Light priests are vanishing**. Witnesses claim to see figures in dark robes at the scenes. The church believes the **Night’s Veil** is abducting members, but upon investigation, it turns out **many priests are willingly defecting** or seeking refuge with the Night’s Veil.  
**Objective:** Investigate the disappearances and choose how to act.  
**Choices:**

- **Eliminate the Night’s Veil helpers** (The church rewards you, and their influence weakens).
- **Negotiate with defectors** (May allow a peaceful resolution but weakens the church’s control).
- **Expose the defectors publicly** (Restores some faith in the church but leads to brutal crackdowns).
 
**Quest 3: The Graves’ Mistaken Curse (Ritual Misunderstanding)**  
**Problem:** The **Eternal Light** reports that the **Night’s Veil** is performing a necromantic ritual in an old cemetery, claiming they plan to **raise the dead** from a mass killing. In truth, the **Night’s Veil** is **purging a deadly plague** from improperly buried corpses to prevent a future outbreak. If interrupted, the disease will spread.  
**Objective:** Decide whether to stop the ritual or let it continue.  
**Choices:**

- **Stop the ritual** (The church rewards you, but disease spreads within weeks).
- **Let it continue** (The church becomes suspicious, but you prevent an epidemic).
- **Sabotage the ritual for personal gain** (The plague mutates, creating a worse problem).
 
**Eternal Light - Quest 4: The Sins of Stone**  
**Problem:** The **Night’s Veil** has been seen **consecrating statues** in the city’s slums. The church believes this is a **dark ritual** meant to bring the stone figures to life as undead guardians. However, the truth is more unsettling—the statues were **once people**, victims of an ancient curse that the **Night’s Veil** is trying to **undo**.  
**Objective:** Destroy the statues and stop the ritual—or uncover the real truth.  
**Choices:**

- **Destroy the statues** (The church rewards you, but you unknowingly kill innocent people trapped in stone).
- **Protect the ritual** (You gain favor with the Night’s Veil but become an enemy of the Eternal Light).
- **Demand the truth** (Revealing the true nature of the curse causes citywide panic).
 
**Eternal Light - Quest 5: The Radiant Thief**  
**Problem:** A **sacred relic** was stolen from the **Solar Vaults** beneath the **Eternal Light’s** main cathedral. The church believes the Night’s Veil is behind it, but evidence suggests the thief may be **someone within the church itself**—perhaps someone powerful.  
**Objective:** Investigate the theft and recover the relic.  
**Choices:**

- **Blame the Night’s Veil and strike at them** (You weaken them, but the real thief escapes).
- **Expose the internal traitor** (The church rewards you, but the revelation weakens its leadership).
- **Sell the relic on the black market** (You gain immense wealth but become an enemy of both factions).
 
**Night’s Veil - Quest 4: The Candle and the Flood**  
**Problem:** The **Eternal Light** is planning a **public execution** of a scholar accused of heresy. However, this scholar **knows the secret history of the city**, including a **great flood that was artificially created centuries ago** to drown political enemies. The church wants him dead before he can speak.  
**Objective:** Rescue the scholar before his execution.  
**Choices:**

- **Stage a dramatic rescue** (You earn the scholar’s knowledge but expose your group).
- **Bribe the guards to fake his death** (Keeps him alive but drains resources).
- **Let him die and steal his research** (The church gets what they want, but you control the secret now).
 
**Night’s Veil - Quest 5: The Stolen Heart**  
**Problem:** The **Eternal Light** has stolen a **powerful artifact** from the **Night’s Veil**, claiming it is a **divine relic**. In truth, it is the **Heart of Ash**, a cursed object that binds **a powerful but forgotten entity**. If the church activates it, it could reshape the entire city—or destroy it.  
**Objective:** Break into the **Solar Vaults** and retrieve the **Heart of Ash**.  
**Obstacles:**

- **Aurelian, the Vault Guardian** – A **gold-plated, nearly indestructible construct** bound to the vault.
- **The Radiant Seal** – A **sunlight-powered ward** that can only be broken with **shadow magic**.
- **The Silent Choir** – A group of **blindfolded monks** who sense even the faintest movements.

**Choices:**

- **Steal the Heart and flee** (The Night’s Veil gains power, but the church begins a city-wide hunt).
- **Destroy the Heart** (Prevents its use but enrages both factions).
- **Claim the Heart’s power for yourself** (Risk becoming something **far worse** than either faction).
