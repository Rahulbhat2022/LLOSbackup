**Anastasia Valcort – The Noble Reformer**  
Lady Anastasia Valcort is a rare anomaly among the [[highborn]]—a noble who does not seek to hoard power but to dismantle the chains that bind the less fortunate. Born into privilege, she grew up in the gilded halls of aristocracy but was never blind to the suffering beyond her family’s estate. While others reveled in excess, she watched the streets fill with the forgotten, the hungry, and the desperate. It was not pity that stirred her, but anger—a quiet, smoldering fire that would shape her every action.  
With a keen mind for politics and an unyielding will, Anastasia has worked tirelessly to erode the entrenched divide between the nobility and the lower classes. She funds education for commoners, challenges exploitative laws, and speaks in courtrooms where her voice is an unwelcome disruption. Many of her peers see her as naïve, a foolish idealist, or worse—a traitor to her class. Yet, she understands the game better than they realize. Influence is not about wealth alone; it is about alliances, leverage, and knowing where to apply pressure. She does not beg for change—she maneuvers to make it inevitable.  
Despite her noble blood, Anastasia walks the streets without escort, unafraid to see the world as it truly is. She speaks with laborers, merchants, and revolutionaries alike, treating them as equals. This has made her both beloved and hated. The common folk whisper her name with hope, while the elite whisper it with disdain. Attempts to discredit her have failed. Threats have been ignored. But in a world where power is hoarded like gold, she knows that true reform is dangerous. The question is not if someone will try to stop her—but when.
