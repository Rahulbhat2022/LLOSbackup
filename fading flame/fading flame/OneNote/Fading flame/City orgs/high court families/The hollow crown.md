**The Guildmaster: Vellorian Thorne, the Pale Fang (victorian witchers)**

- **Race/Class:** Human (Possibly Undying Warlock / Bloodhunter Multiclass)
- **Origin:** Highborn noble turned cursed hunter after his lineage's estate was swallowed by the Hollow Veins. He alone walked out.
- **Appearance:** Impeccably dressed even in bloodstained battlefields, white-haired and hollow-eyed, Thorne carries a boneforged relic axe known as _wraithbone_. His arm is etched with glyphs that rewrite fate.
- **Personality:** Cold, tactical, and grimly charismatic. Chain-smokes cursed ashleaf that lets him commune with slain monsters. Feels nothing for the nobility but wears their sigils as warnings.
