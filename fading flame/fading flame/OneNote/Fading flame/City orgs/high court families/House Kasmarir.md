### House Kasmarir and Its Matriarch

Solene Kasmarir is the reigning matriarch of **House Kasmarir**, one of the oldest bloodlines bound to the Church of the Light. The house is not known for armies or wealth, but for _foresight_. For generations, Kasmarir women have produced Seers—figures feared as much as they are revered. Their words shape doctrine, succession, and war.

The sigil of House Kasmarir is a **blindfolded eye pierced by a halo**, symbolizing truth that must not be seen directly. Their ancestral seat, the **Sanctum of Still Waters**, is a silent citadel where time itself is said to feel slowed. Members of the house are taught from childhood that knowledge is a burden, not a gift, and that compassion clouds vision.

The ruling woman of the house is anointed by the Church as **the Seer**. Any prophecy she utters is recorded as divine testimony. To disobey the Seer is to defy the Light itself.

---

### Loreal, the Heavenly Body

House Kasmarir possesses one of the **Three Heavenly Bodies**: **Loreal**.

Loreal is not a god, but an ancient celestial relic—an intellect bound beyond time. It grants visions, insight, and probability-shaping foresight. Yet its gifts are corruptive. Loreal does not show _what will be_, but what _could be_, and exposes the mind to countless branching futures. Prolonged communion fractures morality and erodes empathy. Many High Court officials guided by Loreal have descended into fanaticism or madness, convinced that atrocity is justified if it leads to a “truer” future.

Loreal whispered only to Solene.

---

### The Deimos Convergence

Obeying Loreal’s vision, Solene commanded her sister to wed **Deimos**, a champion of the Heart’s fighting rings. Deimos was a figure of terror and reverence—an undefeated warrior whose bloodline was traced to **Ares**, the forgotten god of conquest and slaughter.

Unbeknownst to Deimos, Solene orchestrated the ritual extinction of his lineage. Each relative was executed with a consecrated dagger, their blood drained and bound through sigils to awaken the dormant essence of Ares. Deimos himself was slowly poisoned, his strength decaying while Solene observed the futures that unfolded with each passing day. When the time was right, she ended his life with the same blade.

---

### The Failed Ascension

Solene’s sister carried Deimos’s child at the time of his death. During childbirth, Solene murdered her sister with the ritual dagger, believing that death would act as the final conduit—transferring the bloodline of Ares through mother and into son.

The child was named **Victor**.

For two years, Solene raised him in isolation, watching for signs of divine inheritance. None came. Loreal finally spoke: _the ritual had failed_. The blood of Ares had not taken root.

To Solene, this meant only one outcome—the child must die.

---

### The Broken Thread

A servant secretly loyal to **House Valcourt** intervened. Fearing Solene, the Valcourts dared not claim the child as their own. Instead, they sent Victor to the **Scourge Orphanage**, an institution they controlled and used to make unwanted bloodlines disappear.

Victor was recorded as nameless.  
His origin erased.  
His survival an error in fate.
