**House Mourne — The Keepers of Record**
*"Nothing is real until Mourne has written it down."*

---

**Domain:** Legal Authentication, Notarial Records, Contract Arbitration
**Current Head:** Lady Seraphine Mourne
**Cultural Inspiration:** Venetian bureaucratic nobility — power through paperwork, not pageantry

---

**Overview**
House Mourne holds no army, produces no resource, and controls no land. What they hold is simpler and more durable: they hold the record of everything. Every deed of ownership, every inheritance transfer, every contract between noble houses, every title granted or revoked — none of it is legally binding until a Mourne notary has authenticated and filed it. In the High Courts, legal reality and actual reality are the same thing. House Mourne decides which is which.

They do not volunteer this framing. They present themselves as administrators — careful, neutral, indispensable. In practice they are the lock that every door in the High Courts runs through, and they charge a fee for every turn of the key.

They have been doing this for four generations. They remember everything they have ever written down. Their archive contains sealed records that certain families would pay considerably to have lost.

---

**Lady Seraphine Mourne**
The current head of the family is a woman in her mid-sixties who has perfected the art of absolute stillness. She does not fidget, does not shift her weight, does not visibly react to anything she is told. She listens to information the way a ledger absorbs numbers — receiving it completely, giving nothing back in the moment.

She is not cruel and she is not kind. She is precise. Every favor she extends is recorded. Every debt she is owed is tracked. She does not threaten people. She simply knows things, and occasionally she lets them know that she knows.

SPEAKING NOTE: Seraphine never raises her voice. She asks questions rather than making statements. When she wants something she phrases it as a clarification — *"I want to make sure I understand the arrangement correctly."* People find themselves agreeing before they realize they've committed to anything.

---

**The Mourne Archive**
A building adjacent to the High Courts Sanctum — windowless by design, climate-controlled through ether enchantments, staffed at all hours. The interior is organized into vaults by family name, by year, and by category. The oldest records go back to the founding of the High Courts. Notaries work in silence. Visitors are escorted at all times.

There is a secondary vault that does not appear on the Archive's public index. It holds records that were officially sealed, destroyed, or never supposed to have been filed. Seraphine knows exactly what is in it. She has never told anyone how extensive it is.

---

**Relationships**
- **The Seraph:** Mourne and the Seraph operate in parallel — both deal in information and obligation, neither wants the other to become too powerful. A careful mutual respect that has never been tested.
- **House Kasmarir / House Valcort:** Long relationships. These families have had enough transactions pass through Mourne's hands that Seraphine knows the architecture of their finances and alliances better than they do.
- **The Impartial Judiciary Committee:** Mourne supplies the legal record that the judiciary rules against. In theory, this makes them neutral parties. In practice, what Mourne provides and what it withholds shapes the outcome of disputes before they begin.

---

**Secret**
The Scourge case closure forty years ago — "an act of geology" — is in the Archive. So is the communication trail that preceded it. Seraphine was a young notary when it was filed. She knew what it meant then. She has never spoken of it since. Whether she would speak of it now, under the right circumstances, is a question she has not been asked.
