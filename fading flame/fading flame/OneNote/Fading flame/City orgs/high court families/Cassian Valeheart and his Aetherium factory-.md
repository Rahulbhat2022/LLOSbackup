**Lord Cassian Valeheart**  
**Title:** The Gilded Alchemist  
**Role:** Highborn Innovator, Owner of [[Valeheart Inc]]
 
**Identity and Personality**  
Cassian Valeheart is a noble who turned alchemy into empire. Elegant, brilliant, and utterly remorseless, he clawed his way to the top of the arcane economy by replacing volatile Ether with something "better"—a manufactured substance called [[Aether]].  
Once praised as a visionary who brought stable magic to the masses, the truth is far uglier. He discovered that the only way to create Aetherium in large quantities was through the ritual torture of **Veilborn**, magic-sensitive individuals who suffer as their souls are drained into crystal.  
Cassian believes the sacrifice is worth it. Civilization, in his eyes, is built on necessary cruelty.
 
**Key Traits**

- Cold intellect with messianic delusions
- Always meticulously dressed—white suit, gold trim, void-filtering mask
- A mechanical arm powered by Aetherium circuitry
- Speaks like a benevolent surgeon even when ordering executions
- Never lies—but always omits the truth
