**The Dannatum Kemura**  
_"We do not serve kingdoms. We outlast them."_  
The **Dannatum Kemura** are a cabal of arcane scholars, manipulators, and enforcers who operate from behind veils of secrecy and prestige. Founded in the aftermath of the last God’s fall and the rise of the Sister Sun, the Kemura believe magic is not merely a gift—it is a burden only the capable can carry.  
They are bound by neither crown nor church. While they often _appear_ to work for the High Born or advise powerful families, it is understood: no one commands a Kemura—only bargains with one.
 
**Philosophy**  
Magic is power, and power corrupts those who don’t understand it. The _Kemura_ see themselves as wardens of magical order. They do not believe in fairness or equality—only in aptitude. They work like lawyers.
 
They claim to prevent catastrophe. Others say they simply choose which catastrophes are _allowed_.
 
**Hierarchy of the Kemura**  
**Arch Kemura**  
The solitary head of the order. A master of long-term planning, ritual magic, and arcane philosophy. The Arch rarely appears in public, communicating through illusions, proxies, or letters encoded with spells. Their true identity is often debated—even among the Kemura themselves.  
**Known for:** Unshakable authority, access to pre-cataclysmic spells, rumored pacts with the Veil.

**Initiates**  
The youngest and most expendable. Taken from promising bloodlines, these trainees are brutally tested through rituals, duels, and psychological trials. Fewer than 1 in 10 survive their first five years. Those who do are broken down and rebuilt.  
Some never speak again. Others become monsters in fine robes.
