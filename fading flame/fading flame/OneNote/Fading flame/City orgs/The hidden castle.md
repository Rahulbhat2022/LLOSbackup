**Silas, the Pale Warden**  
**Race/Class:** Vampire (custom template) / Cleric 17 (Grave Domain)  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral (with nihilistic tendencies)  
**Appearance:** Draped in tattered white and grey robes over blackened plate mail, Silas’s skeletal visage is hidden beneath a hood. His armor is weatherworn yet polished, like a knight who has fought a thousand battles and still readies for the next.
 
**Castle of the Silent Gate**

- **Location:** Just beyond the outer walls of the city, perched on a low rise with a perfect view of the trade roads.
- **Appearance:**
    - The white stone of the keep is so pristine it reflects the sun.
    - Dozens of poles line the path to the gate — each topped with a desiccated corpse, their faces frozen in anguish.
    - A long shadow is cast over the castle by its own tower at all hours, no matter the sun’s position.
- **Defenses:**
    - Animated suits of armor that patrol the walls, carrying no weapons but killing with sheer crushing force.
    - A moat of brackish, necrotic water that sickens any who touch it.
    - A “Silent Ward” — no sound passes through the gates without Silas’s consent, making coordinated attacks nearly impossible.
 
**Personality & Motivations**

- **View of the City:** Believes the city is rotten to its core and refuses to give aid unless one can prove moral worth.
- **Pessimism:** Claims “no soul in this city is worth saving” — though he secretly hopes to be proven wrong.
- **Code:** Will not harm innocents without cause, but his definition of “innocent” is extremely narrow.
 
**The Holy Blade –** **Sanctis Atra**  
_Weapon (longsword), legendary (requires attunement by a cleric or paladin)_

- **Necrotic/ Radiant Duality:**
    - Can choose to deal either radiant or necrotic damage with each strike.
- **Soul Mark:** A creature struck by the blade must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom save or be marked. Silas can see and hear through marked creatures at will.
- **Funeral Rite:** When Silas reduces a creature to 0 HP, he can choose to instantly perform a last rite, preventing resurrection except by _Wish_.
- **Daylight Blessing:** The blade radiates an aura allowing the wielder to ignore sunlight sensitivity.
