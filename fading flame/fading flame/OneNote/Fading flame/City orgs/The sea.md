**The Neth’rek of the Abyss**  
A reclusive race of deep-sea dwellers, the **Neth’rek** are fishlike humanoids — angular, bioluminescent, with pale, scale-streaked skin and translucent fins. Their large, milky eyes are adapted to darkness, and their voices bubble in alien chords.  
They dwell in the drowned ruins of **Old Drestha**, an ancient sunken city, in vast coral towers entwined with dark anemones and stone idols. Their society is entirely shaped by the **will of the Drowned One**, an Old One who speaks through tides, drowning dreams, and blood-thick currents.
 
**The Drowned One**  
A vast, slumbering entity believed to rest beneath the **Trench of Teeth**, the Drowned One is not merely a god — it is **pressure**, **depth**, and the concept of **suffocation made divine**. Its influence pulses through drowning victims, sunken ships, and the dreams of the desperate.  
￼ **Ritual – “The Calling Below”**  
Every **moontide**, the Neth’rek perform a ceremony known as **The Calling Below** at a deep-sea chasm known as **The Mawrift**.  
**Components:**

- They choose a **single living sacrifice** from captured land-dwellers or failed initiates.
- The ritual takes place in an **open chamber** in the coral cathedral called the **Hall of Airless Grace**.
- Dozens of Neth’rek surround the edges, **chanting in choking gurgles** and slicing their fins to let blood spill into the water.
- The sacrifice is tied in kelp cords, with a **conch of chimes** placed in their mouth.

**The Moment:**

- The conch is **shattered inside the mouth** with a sacred coral hammer, breaking their jaw and silencing them forever.
- The body is dropped into the **Mawrift**, drifting slowly until it is yanked downward by invisible forces.
- When the current turns black and cold, and strange shapes stir in the water, it is said the Drowned One **has answered**.
 
**Magical Artifact: The Spiral of Murmurs**  
_“It pulses with seawater, even in air. When you listen to it too long, your lungs ache, and you forget how many breaths you've taken.”_  
This is a **barnacle-encrusted spiral shell** the size of a child's skull, kept in the inner sanctum of the Deep Priests. It **whispers** with the voice of the Drowned One — not in words, but in **feelings**: hunger, pressure, despair, the echo of screaming from drowning lungs. Once when they were performing their ritual this artifact pulsed towards them.  
**Abilities:**

- **Deep Communion** (1/day): The holder can ask one question. The answer arrives in a **dream-vision** that plays out over 10 seconds — confusing, symbolic, yet always terrifyingly true.
- **Drown the Sound** (3/day): Can create a 30-foot radius of **silence** centered on the spiral for 1 minute, during which nothing can make sound — including spellcasting with verbal components.
- **Tidecaller's Instinct**: While attuned, the user can **breathe underwater**, but suffers nightmares of drowning every long rest.
