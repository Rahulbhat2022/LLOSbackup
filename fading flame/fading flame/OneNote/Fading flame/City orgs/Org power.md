**DnD Faction Evaluation Scale (1–6)**

|   |   |   |   |   |   |   |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Factor**|**1**|**2**|**3**|**4**|**5**|**6 (Extreme)**|
|**Current Influence**|Obscure; barely noticed|Known by a few circles|Has moderate local influence|Respected across several districts|A household name across the city|Shapes public policy and citywide ideology|
|**Current Power**|Powerless|Has minor leverage|Capable of enforcing will locally|Can disrupt or control parts of the city|Strong enough to fight city forces|Can topple governments or bend laws to their will|
|**Wealth**|Dirt-poor|Can fund basic operations|Comfortable and self-sustaining|Affluent; access to luxury or bribes|Rich enough to fund armies or miracles|Controls banks, vaults, or divine material wealth|
|**Membership Size**|Fewer than 5 core members|10–20 scattered followers|20–50 organized members|50–100 active members with reach|Hundreds of active members, larger network|Practically a nation or religion’s worth of members|
|**Leadership Strength**|Ineffective or absent|Minor leaders or contested rule|Competent and organized|Strategic and adaptive leadership|Charismatic or divinely guided figure|Visionary or godlike presence followed fanatically|
|**Territorial Control**|None; operating in secret|One hideout or den|Controls a building or block|Has a secure compound or several locations|Dominates part of a ward or district|Rules entire zones or symbolic spaces|
|**Espionage & Intel**|Clueless; easily caught|Some unreliable informants|Has access to rumors or low-level spying|Targeted surveillance and scouts|Widespread spy network or divination|Nothing happens without them knowing first|
|**Access to Magic Items**|Almost none|A few mundane enchanted items|Moderate access among officers|Most elite members carry items|Regular access to rare or powerful artifacts|Relics and legendary items abound|
|**Magical Capabilities**|No spellcasters|One or two low-level casters|Several trained arcane/divine users|Capable of ritual magic or mid-tier spells|Hosts high-level spellcasters (6–9th level)|Mythic or godlike magical ability; reality-warping|
|**Public Perception**|Hated or unknown|Suspicious or feared|Viewed with mixed feelings|Respected by some, disliked by others|Admired and sought after|Worshipped, idealized, or untouchable|
|**Goal**|Fragmented or unfocused|Self-preservation or survival|Modest goals (wealth, knowledge)|Change something about the system|Topple or replace existing power|Apocalyptic, divine ascension, or total rebirth|
|**Party Affinity**|Openly hostile|Dismissive or opposed|Neutral but wary|Curious or helpful|Friendly and supportive|Deeply allied; consider party champions or icons|
