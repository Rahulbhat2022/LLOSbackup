**Scales of Ma’at** poopy  
Gain a +2 to ac while wearing no armor  
**Truth’s Balance:**  
While wearing the Scales of Ma’at, you can sense when someone is lying to you. When a creature within 15 feet of you speaks, you instinctively feel the weight of their words; lies feel heavy, truths feel light. This doesn’t reveal the actual truth, only that a lie has been spoken.  
**Unique Feature — Judgment in Rage:**  
When you hit a creature with a melee attack when using your **smite**, you can weigh their heart against the scales of Ma’at:

- The target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier) or be **Judged Unworthy** until the start of your next turn.
- While Judged Unworthy, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you, and you have advantage on all attack rolls against it.
- If they succeed, you gain temporary hit points equal to twice your Constitution modifier as the scales tip in your favor.
