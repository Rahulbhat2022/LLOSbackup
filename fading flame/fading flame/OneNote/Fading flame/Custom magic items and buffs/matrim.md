Xolotl twin smoking mirror weapons Matrim  
**Extra Attack**  
When you take the Attack action while wielding both Twin Smoking Mirrors, you can make **one additional attack** as part of the same action.   **Mirror Movement**  
As a bonus action, you can choose a creature within **30 feet** and attempt to swap locations with it. The creature can resist by making an **Intelligence saving throw** against your save DC (8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier). On a failed save, you instantly trade places in a swirl of smoky glass shards. On a success, the swap fails. You can use this feature an amount of times equal to your prof modifier.
 
**Piercing Reflection**  
Twice per long rest, when you hit a creature with a **Sneak Attack** using these weapons, you may immediately make an **Insight** or **Investigation** check (your choice) against a DC set by me the DM). On a success, you discern a significant weakness — such as a lowered AC point, a specific damage vulnerability, or a combat habit you can exploit
      

Later
 
Truesight does not give any benefit against you. Any magics used to hide or obscure you remain.
