**Empowered School** – When casting a spell from your chosen school, your spell save DC increases by +1 and spell attack rolls increase by +2. Additionally, once per turn when you deal damage or restore hit points with a spell from that school, you add extra damage or healing equal to your Intelligence modifier.
 
**Pierce the Veil** – Once per long rest, when you have detect magic active you can use an action to cast trueseeing without expending a spellslot. Lasting the duration of the detect magic.
 
**Magical Operational amplifier**  
Whenever you cast a leveled spell, you may choose to recast it for free at the start of your next turn, targeting the same or different targets — but doing so creates a backlash.  
Roll a d6: 1–2: You take psychic damage equal to twice the spell’s level.  
3–4: You gain one level of exhaustion unless you succeed a DC 15 Con save.  
5–6: The spell casts again perfectly, and you gain temporary HP equal to your INT modifier + spell level. You may use this ability twice per long rest.
