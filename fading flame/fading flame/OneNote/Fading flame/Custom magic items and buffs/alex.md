**Nyx’s Bloom of the Grave:**  
Once per long rest, you can call upon Nyx’s blessing to sow death through rot and shadow:

- As an action, choose a point within 30 feet. A **20-foot-radius sphere** of shadowed fungal growth bursts from that point, lasting for 1 minute. Creatures in the area are considered **difficult terrain** as soft fungal matter coils around their legs.
- Any **living creature** that starts its turn in the area or enters it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier) or take **2d8 necrotic damage**. On a failure, if the creature is reduced to 0 hit points in the area, their corpse sprouts skeletal fungal growths, animating as a **Zombie**. These zombies crumble into harmless dust and mycelium after 1 minute.
   

**Jörmungandr’s Tail**  
_Summoned Creature — Serpentine Avatar_  
**Base Stat Block** **(Level 6 Version)**  
**Medium Celestial/Monstrosity, Neutral**

- **AC:** 15 + PB
- **HP:** 40 + (10 × PB)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
- **STR:** 18 (+3) DEX: 14 (+2) CON: 16 (+3) INT: 6 (−2) WIS: 12 (+1) CHA: 8 (−1)
- **Saving Throws:** DEX + PB, CON + PB
- **Skills:** Perception + PB, Athletics + PB
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 10 + PB
- **Damage Resistances:** Poison, Acid
- **Condition Immunities:** Poisoned
- **Languages:** Understands the languages you speak but can’t speak
 
**Actions**  
**Multiattack** – The Tail makes one attack  
_(Scales: gains extra attacks at levels 8& 13)_  
**Constrict** _(Melee Weapon Attack)_ – +5 + PB to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
Hit: 2d8 + STR bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 12 + PB). While grappled, the target is also restrained.  
**Venomous Bite** _(Melee Weapon Attack)_ – +5 + PB to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
Hit: 1d10 + STR piercing damage plus 1d8 poison damage (poison damage increases to 2d8 at level 11, 3d8 at level 17).
