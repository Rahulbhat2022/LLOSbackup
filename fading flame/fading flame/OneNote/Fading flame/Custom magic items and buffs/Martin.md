**Vajra of Indra (Hilt)**  
_Wondrous Item, legendary (requires attunement by a paladin)_

- **Thunderous Smiteburst** – When you hit a creature with an attack that triggers your Divine Smite, the smite’s energy arcs outward. Choose up to three additional creatures of your choice within 10 feet of the original target. Each must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier). On a failed save, a creature takes radiant damage equal to half the extra damage dealt by your smite.
- **Bolt of the Storm God** – As an action, you can cast _lightning bolt_ without using a spell slot or material components. When cast this way, the damage type changes to radiant. The spell’s save DC equals your paladin spell save DC. Once used, this feature can’t be used again until the next dawn.
- **Divine Reach** – The area within your paladin aura is considered difficult terrain for enemies and allies within the aura have 10ft extra movement.
