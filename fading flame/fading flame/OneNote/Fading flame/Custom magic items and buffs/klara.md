**Charm of Artemis****￼****Arcane Precision** – When making an attack roll with an **Arcane Arrow**, you have advantage on the roll.

- **Step of the Huntress** – Once per turn, when you fire an arrow from this bow, you may teleport up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space you can see as part of the same attack.
- **Hunt of Artemis (1/day)** – As a bonus action, you can mark one creature you can see within range. For 1 minute or until the target drops to 0 hit points:
    - You deal an extra **1d8 damage** with your bow attacks against that creature.
    - You always know the creature’s location while it remains within **120 feet** of you.
    - If the creature moves more than 5 feet without taking the Disengage action, you can make an opportunity attack against it with your bow, provided it is within the weapon’s range.
