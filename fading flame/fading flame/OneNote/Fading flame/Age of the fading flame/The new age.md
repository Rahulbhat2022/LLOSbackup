**The Age of 700 PS (Post Satarum) — The Legacy of the Great Burning**  
**The Myth of the Second Sun**  
According to ancient legends, 700 years ago, a second sun appeared in the sky — a blazing celestial body that scorched the earth and ignited a worldwide inferno. This event became known as **The Great Burning**. People tell stories of its unfiltered light being deadly, and most citizens today believe stepping into it causes madness, disease, or death. In truth, this belief is a myth perpetuated by the ruling elite of **Haven**, the last great city, to discourage exploration and maintain control over the populace. (This narrative ensures safety from external dangers, keeps citizens from uncovering lost technologies or magic, and prevents contact with wild, unstable magic zones.)  
But the second sun was not truly a star — it was **The Source**, a well of pure magic. It came into being when the gods who once managed magic vanished, leaving the arcane energies of the multiverse untethered. Without an Overgod to oversee it, magic coalesced violently, forming the Source — a blinding sphere of condensed arcana, mistaken for a sun.  
**The Truth of the Great Burning**  
In reality, the Great Burning was not divine wrath — it was a man-made apocalypse. Long before the current age, humanity reached a technological peak, developing weapons of mass destruction. Nuclear fire ravaged the world when war broke out, nearly annihilating all life. Only fragments of ancient civilizations survived — hidden, mutated, or warded away by sheer luck or force of will.
 
**Timeline of the Satarum Era**  
**Before Satarum (AS - Ante Satarum)**

- **~700 AS**:￼The Overgod, _Valeria_, departs her watch. After countless ages overseeing the balance of gods and magic, she disappears without warning. Her absence allows **the Forgotten Gods**, ancient and chaotic beings, to stir once more.
- **700–400 AS**:￼The Forgotten Gods battle for dominion over existence. Their wars spill across planes, destroying realities and upsetting cosmic balance. Beings of power — devils, celestials, and others — flee, die, or adapt. Devils begin hiding within human souls. Many planes are obliterated. With their planar sources cut off, most supernatural powers fade. Even martial classes like fighters and barbarians lose their strength. But eventually, they reach a stalemate — their infighting risks destroying all of creation, leaving them nothing to rule. To prevent this, they establish a truce, codifying **rules for a divine game**: a long, cruel contest played through mortals and veiled manipulations. The unblinking steps in a sort of referee. Waiting and staging the game.
- **~400 AS**:￼The **Church of the Burning Light** rises. The unblinking does not intefere as it knows that there will be an opportunity in this. Originally a theocratic government, it seizes control over the fractured remnants of civilization. Obsessed with control and preservation, they declare all other realms and powers as threats. With nuclear weapons at their disposal and a fear of magic's chaos, they initiate catastrophic cleansing operations.
- **0 AS – The Fall and the Founding**
- With most threats destroyed, humans turn on one another. A final nuclear war erupts — **The Great Burning** — and the world ends in fire. Civilizations vanish.￼But not all is lost.￼A powerful wizard, preserved in a demiplane during the collapse, returns to find a scorched world. He salvages remnants of magic from broken planes, gathers survivors, and leads them to the mountains. There, he raises barriers — shielding his enclave from radiation and magical storms — and vanishes from history.￼The survivors call this event **The Satarum**: the cleansing, the reckoning, the end and the beginning.
 
**Post Satarum (PS – After Satarum)**

- **1–200 PS — The Age of Ash****￼**A time of survival. Humanity sekes out an existence in isolated, haunted enclaves. The skies are ash-choked, the earth poisoned. Myths arise — of gods’ wrath, of the Second Sun, of the end-times.￼Many believe the Source is divine punishment. Religion, fear, and superstition rule.
- **200–700 PS — The Age of Haven****￼**Slowly, humanity regroups. The surviving city of **Haven**, founded atop ancient magic and protected by magical wards, becomes a beacon of hope. Magic seeps into the caves beneath condensing into ether.
- Protected from radiation and mutated wilds, it grows in power and influence.￼Exploration is rare and dangerous, but efforts begin to reclaim the world beyond Haven’s walls.￼Most are still afraid of the outside, especially the light of the Second Sun — but again, this fear is state-sponsored, keeping citizens obedient and contained. The true dangers lie in what the rulers of Haven hope never to rediscover.
