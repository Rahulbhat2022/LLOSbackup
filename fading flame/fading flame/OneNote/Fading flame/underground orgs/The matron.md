- Always veiled in layers of fine, **black cloth**, some woven with shifting runes only visible when breathed upon or when caught in candlelight.
- She walks soundlessly, even on broken glass.
- Her voice is soft — not a whisper, but **a presence behind your ear**, a tone that wraps around your skull and makes you want to listen.    
The Matron is not listed in any registry. No known birth. No trail. Not even the city’s spymasters know of her existence.  
She **lives between places** — a hollow behind a wall, a room only entered when unspoken, a home that exists where no one remembers. She is the champion of the whispering old one.  
Where other Old Ones seek raw power or esoteric ascension, **the Whispering One seeks balance by toppling threats to its dominion — even other Old Ones**. It wins by making its enemies destroy themselves.
   

The Matron’s mission is not conquest, but **subtle destruction**.  
She intends to **orchestrate the ruin of at least two Old One-affiliated factions** — not through armies or assassinations, but **dominoes**.
 
Her eyes are on the citadel and later on the advanced city.
    
**Matron’s Interest in the Party**

1. **They survived the Detonation**: No one else did. The Matron believes this proves they are touched by fate — or the Old Ones.
2. **Mental Engrams**: Before being cast out, their minds were implanted with data/memories/experiments stolen from Alexander Troy’s regime. Even they don’t know it.
3. **The Matron's Plan**: She wants to destroy the other Old Ones. The party is a **Trojan horse** — empowered by rival forces but loyal to her cause through necessity.
