**The Dollmaker and Her Marionettes**

- **Leader**: The Dollmaker, a deranged artificer, crafts life-sized marionettes imbued with dark magic and Ether. She treats these creations as her "children" and uses them to carry out her bidding.
- **Purpose**: The Dollmaker operates in the shadows, kidnapping those who wander too close to her lair and transforming them into living marionettes, bound to her will.
- **Key Traits**: Her marionettes are terrifying combatants, moving with inhuman speed and precision. Some retain fragments of their former selves, their muffled cries barely audible beneath their porcelain masks.
- **Interesting Fact**: The Dollmaker's lair is said to be a macabre gallery of her "art," filled with hundreds of lifeless marionettes. Those brave enough to venture inside report feeling as if the dolls are watching their every move.
