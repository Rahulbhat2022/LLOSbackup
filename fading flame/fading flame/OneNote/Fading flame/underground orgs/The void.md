**The Void**  
**Purpose:**  
The Void is a malevolent and shadowy underground organization that preys on the desperation of the gutterborn, using them as disposable tools to build an army of corrupted spellcasters. They have perfected the creation and distribution of [[Voidglass]] — a dark, twisted substance made from consumed and drained ether — turning it into both a drug and a weapon enhancer.  
The Void believes in the power of consumption, corruption, and control, turning the last remnants of magic into something far more dangerous. Their ultimate goal is to harness the dark magic within Voidglass to create a vast army of addicts, all bound to them through their need for the drug, serving as spellcasting shock troops in the Void’s endless expansion of power.  
**Recruitment and Addiction Process:**  
The Void specializes in recruiting the most desperate of individuals — the gutterborn, those living in the depths of poverty, crime, and squalor. The Void offers them an escape, a taste of power that they could never dream of attaining on their own. This is a false promise, however, for their salvation comes at a horrific price.  
The recruitment begins subtly. A gutterborn individual is approached by a Herald of the Void, who offers them a single, seemingly harmless dose of Voidglass. They are told that it will give them strength, power, and the ability to cast spells they could never otherwise wield. The first dose is always an intense, euphoric experience — a surge of magic like nothing they've ever felt before.  
Once they’ve tasted the power, they are hooked. Each dose deepens their addiction, and the Void ensures that their addicts never run dry. They begin to lose themselves to the magic, their bodies slowly changing, their minds warped by the drug’s influence. Over time, the gutterborn are trained to become weapons of magic, their only purpose to serve the Void and carry out its bidding.  
**The Void's Army of Spellcasters:**  
The Void’s army consists of the addicts they’ve created — temporary spellcasters who are more akin to controlled puppets than free individuals. These soldiers are sent into battle, used for assassinations, and employed in high-risk missions where the Void has no qualms about sacrificing their lives. Their powers are fleeting, and their loyalty is not to any cause but the need to feed their addiction to Voidglass.  
The Void’s army is not a traditional military — it is a force of magic-fueled addicts, their power coming from the drug, and their lives constantly teetering on the edge of death due to their dependency. When they are no longer useful, the addicts are discarded or allowed to die, their bodies left behind as husks.



### **1. The Descent – “Ash in the Veins”**

_Environment:_  
The tunnels grow darker the deeper they go. Stone gives way to metallic veins that hum faintly, as though alive. The air grows thin and metallic — not absence of air, but the _presence_ of something that repels breath itself.

Black dust drifts upward rather than downward. Each footstep echoes twice: once in reality, once delayed — as if the world itself struggles to remember sound.

_Encounter / Event:_  
A **Void Echo** manifests — a flickering outline of the party’s own movement, repeating their motions but half a second late.

- **Arcana DC 15** reveals it’s a distortion, not illusion — a side effect of temporal bleeding.
    
- If touched, the echo shatters like glass and reforms into a **Void Wraith** (CR 3) that attacks for a few rounds before dissipating.
    

_Tone Cue:_  
The descent feels like walking into a wound that never healed.

### **2. The Glass Fields – “The Harvest of Light”**

_Environment:_  
The cavern opens into a valley of black crystal spires — the **Voidglass Fields**. These translucent, obsidian structures pulse faintly with trapped light, each one reflecting distorted fragments of the world.

Scattered across the plain, **workers in void-masks** chip shards from the glass and load them into carts drawn by eyeless beasts — **Void Hulks**, creatures whose hides shimmer like oil over water.

_Encounter / Event:_  
The party can observe or infiltrate.

- **Stealth DC 14** to remain unseen by patrolling overseers (Voidglass Wardens).
    
- **Investigation DC 15** to recognize the glass is _not mined but grown_ — the result of captured souls condensed into solid form.
    

Captured workers are dragged to “feed the growths” — being turned into more glass.

If the party acts rashly, a **Void beast** is unleashed — a lumbering beast whose roar bends light and gravity.

_Environmental Mechanic:_  
When a Voidglass shard breaks, it releases a burst of null energy —  
**DC 13 Con save or lose one spell slot / use for 1 minute** as magic is consumed.

### **4. The Void Beasts’ Pens – “The Chained Abyss”**

_Environment:_  
Massive cages line the lower foundry, each holding writhing creatures of unformed flesh and eyes that blink in wrong directions. These are **Void Beasts**, birthed from the leftover essence of failed glass.

They are neither dead nor alive — half in this world, half in the dark between. Chains of obsidian glass keep them bound, siphoning their essence into the furnaces above.

_Encounter / Event:_  
The beasts react to the sister’s presence (if she carries residual spore energy) — they _snarl at the light within her_.

Choices:

- **Free the Beasts:** They turn on their masters, but also risk consuming everything nearby.
    
- **Slay Them:** Brutal, but prevents mass devastation.
    
- **Redirect the Flow:** Using arcane skill (**Arcana DC 17**) to reverse the siphon and collapse the containment field safely.
    

If the beasts are released, the tunnels begin to warp — gravity bends, and light smears into lines.

### **5. The Overseer’s Sanctum – “The Glassmind”**

_Environment:_  
At the factory’s core is the **Overseer** — a being once human, now fused into the foundry’s central lens. Its body has become translucent, riddled with veins of dark glass. The room glows with stolen radiance.

It speaks in fractured voices:

> “All light must feed the dark. The flame above blinds. We will make it clean again.”

_Encounter / Event:_  
This is the **boss encounter**.

- The Overseer commands **two Voidglass Sentinels** that absorb spells and reflect them.
    
- Every 3 rounds, the Overseer drains 10 ft radius of color — anyone within must succeed on **DC 15 Wis save** or be blinded until the next round.
    
- Breaking the **central lens** ends the fight, releasing stored light and shattering the factory’s control network.
    

When the lens shatters, the resulting backlash begins to collapse the foundry into a spiral of imploding glass — forcing the party to escape through twisting corridors as the world eats itself behind them.

### **6. The Collapse – “The Hunger Returns”**

_Environment:_  
Reality fractures — shards of glass hang in midair, each showing fragments of different worlds. Gravity loses direction. The Voidglass Beasts roar somewhere above.

Skill Challenge (5 successes before 3 failures):

- **Dexterity (Acrobatics / Stealth)** to dodge falling shards.
    
- **Constitution (Endurance)** to resist the collapsing pressure.
    
- **Arcana** to stabilize a collapsing portal.
    

If successful, the party escapes through a collapsing maintenance tunnel. Failure results in each character losing a fragment of memory or time (1d4 hours vanish from their recollection).

---

### **7. Aftermath – “The Silent Reflection”**

_Environment:_  
Outside, the voidglass fields are dim. The humming has ceased.  
The air is heavy but quiet — and the stars above seem slightly duller, as if a piece of the night sky has been devoured.

_Consequences:_

- The Void’s production halts. The **Eclipser Engine** project is delayed indefinitely.
    
- Survivors of the foundry whisper of figures made of mirrored glass now wandering aimlessly, repeating, _“It wasn’t meant for us.”_
    
- The party feels faint static in their reflection for several days — as though something still watches from the other side.
