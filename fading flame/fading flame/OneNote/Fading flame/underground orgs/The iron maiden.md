**The Iron Maiden (Kava Iseldi) and Her Steel Defenders**  
**The Lady of Flame and Precision**  
Kava Iseldi, known almost mythically as **The Iron Maiden**, is a master artisan, a keeper of forgotten metallurgy, and the architect of one of the most advanced forge complexes in the world. She is the **High Forgelord of the Crucible Vault**, not by decree, but by supremacy of craft.  
She is cold, calculating, and immaculately precise in everything she does — a being of flesh and Ether-bound alloy, forged in ambition.
   
- **Preserve lost craftsmanship.**
- **Advance the science of Ether-metal integration.**
- **Ensure that the use of Ether-tech remains in controlled, disciplined hands.**
- **Forge a society where every soul is a cog, valued by its function.**

Kava believes unregulated use of Ether will bring ruin. Her solution? Only the most loyal and structured are allowed to wield the power she creates.
    
Her guardians and enforcers, the **Steel Defenders**, are less an army and more a **protective caste** — a military guild of forged-body enforcers who **protect workshops**, **escort Ether caravans**, and **guard secrets**. Their loyalty to Kava is unwavering, and their presence makes theft from her domain a death sentence.
   

- **The Anvil Sanctum**: Kava's private forge, built around an ancient Ether vein. Access restricted to only her closest disciples.
- **The Foundry Choir**: Artisans and assistants trained from youth in the strict rites of precision forging. They work in perfect rhythm, often in complete silence, guided by the beat of hammers on steel.
- **The Tempering Pool**: A lake of still, unnaturally clear water that glows faintly — used to cool weapons and even meditate upon their purpose.
- **The Codex Archive**: A sealed vault of designs and failed blueprints. To enter without permission is to risk death or exile.
   
          
Deep beneath the city lies **The Crucible Vault**, a **sacred, humming forge-cathedral** nestled inside the hollowed skeleton of an old mining system. It is not just a place of production — it is a **shrine to the act of creation**.
