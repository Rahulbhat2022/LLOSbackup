**The Waxed Wick and His Waxed Knights**

- **Leader**: A mysterious figure known as the Waxed Wick, with only a faint flicker of light visible where his face should be.
- **Purpose**: The Waxed Knights are assassins and arsonists who deal in destruction for hire. Their name comes from their signature weapons—candles infused with Ether, capable of burning with unnatural intensity and creating toxic smoke.
- **Key Traits**: The Waxed Wick operates a network of secret hideouts disguised as candle-making shops. His knights are experts in stealth, sabotage, and murder, leaving behind smoldering ruins as their calling card.
- **Interesting Fact**: The Waxed Wick is said to be immortal, his body sustained by the Ether flame that burns within him. Those who work for him must swear their loyalty over a burning candle, binding them to his will.
 
**Melting Chapel**

- A circular ritual room where recruits **swear their loyalty** by letting wax drip onto their bare hands from the **Everburning Candle** — a flame sustained since the Waxed Wick’s rumored rebirth.
- The room is dim, with **no flame allowed but candles**, and the silence is absolute. The air smells like burnt honey and blood.
- Once the oath is taken, the wax hardens black, and the brand remains warm on the skin. Breaking the oath causes the candle to sputter — and the traitor to ignite from within.

Meeting him:￼  
_"Flicker, flutter, dim then bright,_  
_A shadow sways into the room that is dimly lit by candlelights._  
(He steps forward)  
_"The Waxed Wick, that’s my name,_  
_A guide, a whisper, a fleeting flame._  
_Some come seeking, some come lost,_  
_All pay tribute, all count the cost."_  
(He gestures to the flickering lanterns around him, each casting strange, wavering shadows.)  
_"The Wax Knights move where fire bends,_  
_Loyal blades, with melted ends._  
_We shape the dark, we light the way—_  
_Tell me, travelers, do you wish to stay?"_
    
**Yes:**  
_"The flame still burns, the wax agrees,_  
_A flicker bright upon the breeze."_  
_"A steady wick, a candle’s glow,_  
_The answer’s clear—the answer’s ‘go’."_  
_"A nod, a spark, the ember stays,_  
_Yes, my friend, the fire plays."_
 
**No:**  
_"The wax runs cold, the flame says ‘nay’—_  
_Snuff the thought and look away."_  
_"A dying wick, a candle spent,_  
_No light to give, no word is lent."_  
_"The match unstruck, the night stays deep,_  
_No fire here, let silence keep."_
 
**On Power & Control:**  
_"Flames rise, then shrink so small,_  
_The tallest candle still must fall._  
_Those who think they rule the night,_  
_Forget that fire gives us sight."_  
**On Making a Deal:**  
_"A favor owed, a candle burned,_  
_Every debt shall be returned._  
_Speak your price, speak it true,_  
_But know the fire flickers for you."_  
**On Betrayal:**  
_"Drip, drop, wax runs thin,_  
_A broken vow, a burning sin._  
_A traitor’s tongue melts just the same—_  
_No whisper left to speak their name."_  
**On His Organization (The Wax Knights):**  
_"Knights of wax, wicks of fate,_  
_Melting slow or burning great._  
_We do not break, we only bend,_  
_And light the dark where shadows end."_  
**On Death & Fate:**  
_"The flame may dance, the flame may sigh,_  
_But all must dim, all must die._  
_Fear not the dark, nor ember’s glow—_  
_Both shall take you, both shall know."_  
**On Warning Someone:**  
_"Step with care, mind the spark,_  
_Not all lights shine in the dark._  
_Some will warm, some will burn—_  
_Only fools forget to learn."_
 
**On Time & Patience:**  
_"A candle waits, a candle weeps,_  
_Yet steady hands pull secrets deep._  
_Rush the wax, you’ll twist the thread,_  
_And find yourself with flame instead."_  
**On Trust & Loyalty:**  
_"A candle’s glow is soft, yet true,_  
_It does not lie, it follows through._  
_But light will flicker, melt, then wane,_  
_Betray the flame, and know the pain."_  
**On Opportunity:**  
_"A wick unlit will never shine,_  
_A fool ignores the offered time._  
_Take the match, strike it fast,_  
_Or watch the moment flicker past."_  
**On Suffering & Strength:**  
_"Drip by drip, the wax may bleed,_  
_Yet pain gives shape to those in need._  
_Scars will set and harden tight—_  
_From melted loss comes sharpened might."_  
**On Asking for His Help:**  
_"A match is struck, a bargain made,_  
_But fire asks the price be paid._  
_Come close, speak clear, but mind the cost,_  
_For nothing’s gained without what's lost."_  
**On Those Who Threaten Him:**  
_"Strike me down, the wax still flows,_  
_A dying flame still burns and knows._  
_Snuff me out? A fruitless feat—_  
_The fire spreads beneath your feet."_  
**On The Nature of Secrets:**  
_"What melts and drips is soft, yet sly,_  
_A whispered word, a guarded lie._  
_The wax records, the wick does hear,_  
_And in the dark, all things are clear."_  
**On The Future:**  
_"The flame will dance, the flame will wane,_  
_New wicks rise to take the flame._  
_Do not ask what lies ahead—_  
_The candle burns until it’s dead._
