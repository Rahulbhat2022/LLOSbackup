**Spore and Their Myconids**

- **Leader**: Spore, a hive-minded entity, is less a singular being and more a collective intelligence spread among the Myconid colony. It speaks through the Myconids, a species of sentient, fungal humanoids that live in the dampest, darkest parts of the Undercity.
	The spore heals dying humans turning them into one of her myconids. Soldiers under her are essentially warlocks drawing powers from her. She is very neutral morally as she does help dying people but does so by letting them into her hivemind. Preying on the weak.
  
  
The Deep is very much like the commune from arcane. 
  ### ** The Threshold – “The Breathing Walls”**

_Environment:_  
A narrow tunnel choked with moss and mushroom caps. The air is damp, heavy, and alive — it vibrates faintly, like lungs expanding. The walls pulse with bioluminescent veins of green and violet. When light is shone, it reveals moving filaments that retract like worms into the stone.

_Encounter:_  
As they move deeper, the walls whisper. The voices are **echoes of their own**, delayed by a second — the Spore mimicking them to learn their cadence.  
Skill check: **Wisdom (Perception or Insight) DC 14** to notice the synchronization; **Arcana DC 13** reveals the presence of a psychic network — they’ve already entered Spore’s mind.

_Tone Cue:_  
The further they go, the more they feel watched. The Myconids do not attack yet; they _breathe with them_.


### **The Fungal Gate – “The Rootguard’s Warning”**

_Environment:_  
A massive hollow chamber, its floor a mesh of pale tendrils that quiver with each step. In the center stands a cluster of tall, spindly Myconids — their caps dripping with spore-fluid like tears.

_Encounter:_  
The **Rootguards** demand the party remove all fire, open flame, or metal that “cuts the living earth.” They speak in unison, a chorus of different tones forming one sentence.  
Refusal causes the tendrils to constrict, separating the party and forcing a fight — but not to kill, only to **test submission**.



## **“The Garden of Renewal”**
	 
_Environment:_  
The path opens into a vast, softly glowing cavern. Great pillars of translucent fungus rise like cathedral columns, releasing gentle motes of light that drift lazily through the air.  
Lush moss and coral-like growths coat the stone; decayed matter has been repurposed into beauty.

_Encounter / Event:_  
Here the Myconids tend to **The Remembered Garden** — a place where the remains of the dead nourish new life. Every patch of moss, every bloom, is someone’s memory reborn as living color.  
A Myconid Elder approaches slowly, offering spores that allow communication. The Elder explains that the dead are not forgotten. 
The spore is building an undead army for the worst case scenario.
