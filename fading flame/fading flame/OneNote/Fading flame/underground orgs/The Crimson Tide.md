- **Purpose**:￼The Crimson Tithe deals in **blood, flesh, and life itself**. They believe the body is currency — to be bought, sold, traded, or claimed. They control illegal healing, body part replacement, and organ trade.￼The poor sell bits of themselves to survive; the rich buy new parts to extend their lives.
- **Black Market Connection**:￼They are **closely tied to the Iron Clutch**, an underground black market syndicate run by the Iron Maiden’s enforcers.￼The Iron Clutch handles **smuggling**, **processing**, and **distribution** of bodies and parts.￼The Crimson Tithe provides the "stock" — living or dead.
- **Symbols and Appearance**:
    - Their symbol is a **twisted red spiral**, resembling a vein or blood droplet.
    - Members wear **dark crimson cloaks**, often lined with hidden tools like bone saws, syringes, and Ether-needles.
    - In deals, they carry ornate **blood-letting knives** as ceremonial tokens 
The make the ether blood potions.


## **Scene 1 — The Blood Bazaar**

**Theme:** “Flesh is coin, blood is speech.”  
**Setting:** Beneath the Iron Clutch’s smuggler tunnels lies a cavernous market illuminated by red glass lanterns filled with circulating blood. The air hums with the faint pulse of ether-pumps.

**Description:**  
As the party descends through the tunnels, the air turns thick and metallic. The _Blood Bazaar_ sprawls before them — a half-market, half-operating theater. Stalls of grafted limbs hang like butcher’s meat. Glass tubes carry glowing crimson fluids through the ceiling, pumping toward unseen chambers.  
Vendors shout prices in heartbeats: “Three pints for a night’s shelter! Half a lung for bread!”

**Encounters:**

- **A “healer” offers to buy the party’s blood** for an Ether infusion, explaining how “every drop funds salvation.”
    
- The group can see **Iron Clutch smugglers** offloading crates — some writhing faintly — bound for the Tithe’s upper chambers.
    
- Optional Skill Challenge: Insight or Arcana to notice that the Ether-Blood flowing through pipes isn’t just harvested — it’s alive, reacting to their presence.
    

**Tone:** Disquieting commerce. Life and death priced like spices.

## **Scene 2 — The Surgeon’s Way**

**Theme:** Devotion through dissection.  
**Setting:** A long, cathedral-like corridor lined with surgical alcoves, each hosting a “priest” performing operations under hanging candles of congealed blood.

**Description:**  
The party is guided (or coerced) through _The Surgeon’s Way_, where crimson-robed physicians whisper blessings as they carve. Patients lie motionless — some sedated, others conscious — while organs are transferred between tables with reverent precision. Every cut is a prayer, every stitch a tithe.  
On the walls, murals depict **Vaelmir**, the Sanguine Regent, piercing his heart with the Dagger of Ameretat — the birth of immortal flesh.

**Encounters:**

- A surgeon asks the party if they wish to “offer a tithe” — a drop of blood for safe passage. Refusing may mark them as debtors.
    
- If they comply, their blood is drawn into an ornate vial that glows briefly, then stills — a sign that their “essence has been recorded.”
    
- A faint hum emanates from the walls — the pulse of the **Crimson Basilica** itself.
    

**Tone:** Surgical sanctity. Worship disguised as medicine.

## **Scene 3 — The Vein Cathedral**

**Theme:** Worship of blood as divine architecture.  
**Setting:** The party enters a vast, breathing chamber — a cathedral made of living tissue and Soulgold veins. Arteries throb across the ceiling, feeding glowing tanks filled with etheric blood.

**Description:**  
Here stands the _Vein Cathedral_ — the heart of the Tithe’s domain. Rows of supplicants kneel, connected by tubes to blood fonts, praying as their life force circulates through the cathedral. Choirs hum hymns in slow heartbeats, their throats lined with metallic threads to control pitch and pulse.  
A massive heart-machine beats slowly behind a lattice of Soulgold, pumping Ether-Blood upward into glass arteries.

**Encounters:**

- A priest delivers a sermon:  
    “We give, and we are eternal. We bleed, and we are reborn.”
    
- Players might notice **Iron Clutch branding** etched into the machinery — proof of collaboration.
    
- Investigation reveals the “blood” being cycled includes traces of **mutagenic Ether compounds** — the Tithe’s secret experiments.
    

**Tone:** Awed horror. The sacred made biological.

## **Scene 5 — The Sanguine Throne**

**Theme:** Immortality is debt eternal.  
**Setting:** The inner sanctum — a circular chamber where crimson light falls from stained Soulgold veins. At its center sits **Lord Vaelmir D’Arcos**, motionless upon a throne of bone and glass, the **Dagger of Ameretat** embedded in his chest, pulsing faintly.

**Description:**  
Vaelmir’s voice echoes as if from inside their veins. His eyes glow like diluted sunlight in wine. The chamber itself seems to breathe in rhythm with him.  
Behind him, thirteen figures hang suspended by blood-tubes — the _Veinbound_, nobles who traded their lives for eternity, now thralls tethered to his essence.

**Encounters:**

- Vaelmir speaks as a prophet, not a villain:  
    “Flesh is the first truth. Blood the only scripture. You have entered the covenant — what will you offer to remain whole?”
    
- His followers watch silently; one coughs Ether mist from her lungs, smiling in devotion.
    
- Depending on the party’s choices earlier, Vaelmir may offer alliance (“You bleed beautifully, my children”) or declare them heretics (“You hoard what should flow”).
