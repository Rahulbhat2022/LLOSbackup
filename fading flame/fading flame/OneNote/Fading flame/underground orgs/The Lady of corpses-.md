**The Lady of Corpses – Weaver of Flesh**  
Deep in the forgotten alleys where the sick and dying are abandoned, **the Lady of Corpses** lingers—a patchwork being of countless hands, faces, and torsos, stitched together in grotesque harmony. Her voice shifts between tones, a chorus of the dead speaking as one. She does not breathe, nor does she bleed, but her hands are always warm—always working. Those who seek her come broken, missing limbs or hope, and she offers them new flesh, taken from those who no longer need it. She was once part of the nights veil, but she was exiled as her practices extended the purpose of the church. They believe in giving the dead a last fight, but the lady corpses, defiles and twists and bends the dead.  
Her craft is masterful, but her mercy is thin. She does not ask if you wish to keep your humanity—only if you wish to function. A lost arm may be replaced with one too long, too pale, fingers twitching with memories of its former owner. A ruined eye may see again, but it might glimpse things best left unseen. Payment is not always in coin; sometimes, it is a promise, a favor, or simply a piece of yourself you no longer need.  
Despite the horror of her form and work, the desperate still whisper her name with reverence. Soldiers, criminals, and even nobles with shameful wounds seek her out under cover of night. To some, she is salvation, a chance to be whole again. To others, she is a living nightmare, an artist who does not care whether her canvas screams.
 
**She used to have more followers and apprentices, many of which are spread in the city now or dead, but have left her as she is pretty extreme in her practices.**
 
**Her main place of residence is her halls of flesh. Where the walls and interiour is made purely from flesh. Imbued with preserve dead.**
