The eternal flame was once governed by chronia. But in this world it was broken by the [[Ameretat]] for her to gain the power of immortality. Breaking it also created more power towards necormancers and has created more immortals. 
With no one to govern this power now the balance of life and death is greatly in jeopardy.
The shards of the flames grant the power of what was once domains that gods had. to wield one you must be atleast lvl 11 associated with the domain. it levels up with you for each 3 levels above 11 you can attune to another. 
The eternal flame has split into 5 parts. 
currently, 

[[The waxed wick]] has Shard of flame
[[Church of the Eternal light]] has shard of life
[[The monestary]] has shard of order
[[sanctuary of chronos]] has shard of stasis
[[Church of the fading soul]] has  shard of death
