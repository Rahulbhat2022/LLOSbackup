The over goddess of all worlds. She keeps watch over the worlds trying to ensure balance and prosperity of her friends. In this world she is "asleep".  She has forced her celestial presence away from this world as she suspected that [[The old ones]] still hide at the edge of the universe. Therefore as a way to draw them out she has taken a leave, waiting for them to take physical shape again. To ensure things go according to her plan, she planted one within their mix.
