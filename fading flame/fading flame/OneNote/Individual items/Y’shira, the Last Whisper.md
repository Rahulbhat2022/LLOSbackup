an entity they do not call a deity but rather a **force of inevitability**. She is not a judge nor a savior—she is **the silent witness to every life’s end**.

- **Depiction**: A faceless, veiled woman of dark marble and bone, always depicted in profile, as if listening.
- **Role**: Y’shira is **the keeper of the last breath**, the lingering presence that remains **just before** a soul fades.
- **Beliefs about Her**:
    - She does not grant power, only **observes**.
    - She does not take souls but ensures they **are not forgotten**.
    - She exists in **the space between life and oblivion**, and the Night’s Veil acts in her stead.
