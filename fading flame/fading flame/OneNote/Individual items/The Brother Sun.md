 **Appearance:** The Brother Sun is a radiant, deep red sun that burns brightly in the sky. His light is intense and strong, casting an unwavering glow across the land. He symbolizes the essence of **hope** and **light** in the world.
- **Role:** The Brother Sun is the beacon of life, strength, and renewal. He is the force that illuminates the world, bringing warmth, growth, and the promise of a better tomorrow. His light is steadfast, guiding those who seek progress, courage, and purpose. He is a symbol of the **day**, action, and the will to fight against the darkness, both literally and metaphorically.
- **Worship:** His followers revere him as the source of hope and the protector of life. They view him as a divine force that empowers them to overcome adversity and bring light to dark places, whether in the form of knowledge, growth, or literal light. Many warriors, leaders, and those who struggle against the odds look to him for strength.
