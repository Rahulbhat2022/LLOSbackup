**Aetherium: The Synthesized Arcane Alloy**  
**Description:**  
Aetherium is a rare, artificial material created by alchemists and artificers attempting to mimic the properties of Ether. Though less potent, it offers a stable and reliable source of arcane power without the volatility of Voidglass. Aetherium is forged through a combination of purified arcane crystals, rare metals, and a stabilizing agent (like powdered silver or diamond dust). The resulting alloy shimmers with a soft iridescent glow, subtly pulsing with latent magical energy.
 
**Properties of Aetherium**  
**General Traits:**

- **Resilient Magic Conduit**: Aetherium excels at channeling and amplifying magic, though not as dramatically as Ether. It is often used to craft staves, wands, or armor for spellcasters and Bloodhunters.
- **Inert to Corruption**: Unlike Ether, Aetherium is not vulnerable to corruption. It cannot be turned into Voidglass, making it a safer option for experimentation and use in high-stress environments.
