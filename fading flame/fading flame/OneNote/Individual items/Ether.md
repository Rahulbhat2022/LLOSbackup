Ether is a potent and highly addictive magical substance harvested from rare crystals found deep in the mines. It offers both physical and metaphysical effects, drawing its allure from its ability to temporarily connect users to the Source—the raw, chaotic essence of magic.
 
It is origanlly created from dead mages long ago whos power collect into shards of ether.
 
**Harvesting Ether**

1. **The Crystals**
    - **Appearance**: Ether crystals glow faintly with a pulsing inner light, shifting colors based on their potency. Low-grade crystals emit a dim, steady glow, while high-grade ones shimmer and crackle with magical energy.
    - **Mining Challenges**: Ether crystals are found in treacherous mines laced with magical hazards—unstable pockets of raw Source energy, mana bursts, and mutated creatures drawn to the crystals.
    - **Refinement Process**: Before consumption, Ether crystals must be carefully refined. This involves grinding them into a fine, shimmering powder and purging impurities through an alchemical process. Poorly refined Ether can be lethal, leading to unpredictable magical effects or instant overdose.
 
**Effects of Ether**  
**Cantrip-Level Ether**

- Commonly referred to as "Spark Dust" on the streets.
- Grants users the ability to cast minor spells like _Prestidigitation_, _Thaumaturgy_, or _Mage Hand_.
- Effects last 1-2 hours, inducing a euphoric sense of empowerment and creativity, often accompanied by vivid hallucinations of light and color.
- Overuse leads to migraines, nausea, and “mana shakes”—uncontrollable tremors caused by overstimulation of the body’s magical channels.

**Spell-Level Ether**

- Known as "Arcane Fire" or "High Ether," this grade is far more potent and dangerous.
- Temporarily grants non-spellcasters the ability to cast spells equivalent to the crystal’s imbued spell level.
- Requires the user to undergo a risky surgical procedure to implant a magical focus near a major vein or artery, allowing the Ether to integrate with their bloodstream and connect them to the Source.
    - For the wealthy, these procedures are performed by expert surgeons using sterilized tools and enchanted equipment.
    - For the poor, black-market surgeries often lead to infections, botched implants, or death.
- The “high” from spell-level Ether is described as transcendent, providing an unparalleled sense of power and enlightenment. However, it is extremely addictive and almost always leads to:
    - **Overload**: Prolonged exposure causes the body to break down from the influx of uncontrolled magic, leading to organ failure or spontaneous magical combustion.
    - **Mana Burn**: Permanent damage to the user’s magical channels, leaving them unable to feel joy or experience normal emotions without Ether.
    - **Soul Bleed**: A phenomenon where heavy Ether users feel as though their soul is slowly draining away, as the ether is burning away their breath of life. Eventually if you die by this you become breathless.
 
**Addiction and Dependency**

1. **Physical Dependency****￼**Ether affects both the body and the mind. Long-term users experience:
    - Intense cravings for the high.
    - Physical deterioration, such as pale skin, sunken eyes, and glowing veins that pulse with residual magic.
    - “Mana Spasms”—random discharges of magical energy from the body, often uncontrollable and dangerous.
2. **Psychological Effects**
    - Users describe a profound sense of loss and emptiness when not under Ether’s influence, as if disconnected from something greater than themselves.
    - Long-term users often lose touch with reality, unable to distinguish hallucinations from the physical world.
    - Paranoia and obsession with obtaining the next dose often lead to theft, betrayal, or violence.
3. **Overdose****￼**Taking too much Ether, especially high-grade doses, overwhelms the body with raw magic. Overdose symptoms include:
    - Intense seizures that unleash destructive magical effects.
    - Uncontrollable transformations or mutations (e.g., turning into a magical beast or a living flame).
    - Death, often spectacular and horrifying, leaving behind a charred or crystallized corpse.
