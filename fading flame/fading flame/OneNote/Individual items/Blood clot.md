
      

**Blood-Ether Elixir**  
_Potion Type:_ Rare, Consumable  
_Appearance:_ A thick, swirling red liquid that glows with an eerie, pulsating light. The liquid seems to move as if alive, the veins of blood stretching and contracting within it.  
**Creation Process:**  
The Blood-Ether Elixir is crafted by combining the potent life force of blood with the volatile essence of Ether. The ritual requires fresh blood from a willing or unwilling donor, which is then mixed with Ether in a specially designed vessel. The final product is a volatile, potent concoction that must be handled with care, as it can have unpredictable effects on both the body and the mind.  
**Effects:**

- **Life-Extension:** The elixir grants the user temporary vitality, increasing their hit points by 2d10 for 1 hour. This effect is often sought after by the rich, particularly those looking to stave off death for a little longer.
- **Enhanced Strength:** For the duration, the user gains advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, as the Ether enhances their physical capabilities and blood fuels their muscles.
- **Bloodlust:** The user gains the _Blood Frenzy_ ability for 1 hour. Whenever they deal damage with a melee weapon attack, they gain temporary hit points equal to half the damage dealt (rounded up), up to a maximum of 10 temporary hit points at a time. However, each time they use this ability, they must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 15) or become consumed by bloodlust, attacking the nearest creature in melee range regardless of whether it's an ally or foe.
- **Exhaustion Aftermath:** After the elixir’s effects wear off, the user must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 15) or suffer 1 level of exhaustion. If they fail the save by 5 or more, they suffer 2 levels of exhaustion instead. This is a side effect of the body being overloaded with both Ether and blood.

**Side Effects:**

- **Blood Dependency:** The more frequently one consumes the Blood-Ether Elixir, the more dependent the user becomes on its effects. After 3 doses, the user starts experiencing withdrawal symptoms — reduced maximum hit points (a permanent decrease of 5 max HP) and a -1 penalty to Constitution saving throws when not under the effects of the elixir.
- **Corruption of the Flesh:** Prolonged use of the elixir (10+ doses) may cause the user’s flesh to become warped and corrupted. This might manifest as visible veins running with crimson glow, skin becoming unnaturally pale or stretched, or the user gaining a more monstrous appearance as their body begins to reject the unnatural combination of blood and Ether.
