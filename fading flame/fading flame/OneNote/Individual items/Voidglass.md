The Corrupted Ether
Voidglass is a **multifunctional magical substance** with terrifying power, used as a drug, weapon enhancer, and spellcasting focus augment. This deep purple orb, laced with black veins, exudes a dark energy that tempts even the strongest-willed individuals.
 
It is made from already consumed and drained ether. turning the last drop of its magic into something stronger and twisted
 
**Voidglass as a Drug(500gp)**  
**For Spellcasters**

1. **Enhanced Magic**:
    - Spellcasters ingest Voidglass shards or inhale its vapor, temporarily supercharging their connection to the Source.
    - Effects: Spells are cast at +1 spell level for 1d6 rounds, and cantrips deal an additional 1d8 damage.
    - Downside: At the end of the effect, the user must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 18) or take **3d10 necrotic damage** and lose one spell slot until their next long rest.
2. **Addictive Power**:
    - Each use requires a Wisdom save (DC 14(dc increases by 1 for each stage of addiction)). On a failure, the user gains a level of addiction.
    - Addiction Effects:
        - **Mild Addiction (1)**: -1 to Concentration checks.
        - **Moderate Addiction (2)**: -1 to all saving throws.
        - **Severe Addiction (3)**: Permanent reduction of 1 spell slot and eventual transformation into a shadow wraith. Remove 1 more spellslot for each gained level of addiction.
        - **Death(7)**

**For Non-Spellcasters**

1. **Temporary Spellcasting**:
    - Consuming Voidglass enables non-spellcasters to cast one spell (up to 3rd level) from the Wizard or Warlock spell list for 1 hour.
    - Downside: They take **3d6 necrotic damage** immediately and permanently lose that hp from their hp max. unless cured by a greater restoration

**Voidglass as an Orb for Spellcasting Focus**  
**Effects When Bound to a Focus**

1. **Power Amplification**:
    - Adds +1 to spell attack rolls and increases spell save DC by 1.
    - Spells gain a necrotic edge, dealing an extra 1d6 necrotic damage on hit.
2. **Corrupted Surge**:
    - Any spell cast using the Voidglass-enhanced focus has a 10% chance of triggering a **Wild Magic Surge** or corrupting the spell's effects (e.g., healing spells deal damage).
3. **Risk of Shattering**:
    - If a natural 1 is rolled on a spell attack, the orb cracks, dealing **2d10 force damage** to the caster and rendering the focus useless until repaired.
 
**Voidglass as a Weapon Enhancer**  
**Infusion Process**

- Voidglass orbs can be socketed into weapons by skilled artificers or warlocks, creating **Voidforged Weapons**.

**Effects on Weapons**

1. **Necrotic Edge**:
    - The weapon deals an additional **1d8 necrotic damage** on a successful hit.
2. **Siphoning Strike**:
    - Once per short rest, when the wielder hits an attack they can activate the orb to siphon life force from the target, the damage dealt is healed to the wielder.(entire damage of the attack)
3. **Corruption Overload**:
    - After using the Siphoning Strike, the wielder must make a Wisdom save (DC 15). On a failure, roll 1d4.
    
    1. The closest creature to you becomes the source of your fear and you’re are frightened by it, You may repeat the save at the end of each of your turns.  
    2. 1 but charmed instead  
    3. Attack rolls you make gain advantage untill the end of your next turn. But enemies also have advantage on attack rolls against you.  
    4. wild magic surge.
