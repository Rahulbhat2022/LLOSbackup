### Crafting Rules

**Time Requirements:**

- Tier 1: 1 hour
- Tier 2: 2 hours
- Tier 3: 4 hours
- Tier 4: 8 hours

**Skill Bonuses:**

- **Minor Poison Handling**: DC reduced by 1 for Tier 1 poisons
- **Poison Handling**: DC reduced by 1 for Tier 1-2 poisons, can apply as half action
- **Poison Expertise**: Gain 1 additional success when crafting any poison
- **Venom Mastery**: Poisons last 1 additional application on weapons
