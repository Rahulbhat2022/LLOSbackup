
Stats
We will move on from the dexterity does all stat distribution.  


**Strength** – Straightforward and familiar. Strength measures an adventurer’s raw physical power  their ability to lift, break, and overpower.

**Agility** – Unlike _Dexterity_ in many systems, _Agility_ is more focused and streamlined. It governs actions that rely on speed, balance, and flexibility rather than serving as a “catch-all” for finesse.

**Composure** – Composure represents how well an adventurer can remain calm and collected under pressure. It’s primarily used for ranged attacks, where steady breathing and focus are crucial, but can also be combined with other abilities or skills to reflect poise in tense situations.

**Fortitude** – Comparable to _Constitution_, but with a more hardened edge. Fortitude reflects endurance, pain tolerance, and the sheer will to push through exhaustion and injury.

**Intellect** – Much like _Intelligence_ in D&D, Intellect governs knowledge, reasoning, and problem-solving ability.

**Resolve** – A more streamlined version of _Wisdom_. Resolve represents mental strength, conviction, and mental fortitude. Some adventurers channel their Resolve into their combat techniques or divine magic, making it the spellcasting ability for devotees.

**Wits** – Wits governs quick thinking and fast reactions. It’s used for Perception and Insight checks, reflecting awareness and mental agility. While both Wits and Resolve cover aspects of D&D’s _Wisdom_, they’re separated here to distinguish intuition, from mental strength. 

**Presence** – Similar to _Charisma_ but more refined in scope. Presence measures the strength of one’s personal aura, the power to influence, command, or inspire others, whether through natural charm or supernatural force.

**Wounds**

**Physical wounds**

When a creature takes physical wounds they divide the amount of successes by 2 and take that amount of deep wounds and any remainder as shallow wounds. 
A creature that is unconscious, or paralyzed does not divide by 2 and takes deep wounds equal to successes. 

**Shallow wound:**

Surface level wound involves damage sources that would not cause lingering injuries or would be lethal in most cases. Things like cuts and fractures. In this system surface wounds serve as the first level of defence that a adventurer has, and represents the level of injury an adventurer can “power through” without receiving penalties. Though receive more of this and surface level wounds can suddenly turn into a deep wound.
You have an amount of wounds max equal to your fortitude score
  

**Deep wound:**
Deep wounds represents forms of damage such as stabs and gashes. Any form of wound that would leave an adventuring actively in lethal danger if the wound is not taken care of. These wounds are absolutely to be avoided(duh) as when a adventurer gains a deep wound they have an amount of turns equal to their fortitude score to secure that wound. If time passes without taking care of the wound, then the adventurer gains another deep wound, if all deep wound slots and the time has passed again the character dies.

**Stabilizing a deep wound**
A deep wound may be "stabilized". The drawback from is temporarily fixed for you and you may power through the fight. 
If you take a wound again (if relevant and close to the initial deep wound), you must roll a fortitude toughness roll. You need successes equal 2+the amount of deep wounds you have with the dc of 6+1 for every deep wound you have. 
On a fail you gain the drawback from the deep wound you had. 



**Mental wounds.** 

Mental wounds are different than physical wounds. They are revolved around a creatures psyche and are affected mostly through spells and magic. A creature has an amount of mental wound points equal to their Resolve score. Unlike physical wounds, there is no surface level mental wound, and each wound will eat away at a creatures psyche. But these are easier to recover from in combat. Each lingering spell will consume a point of a creatures psyche and when all of the creatures mental wound slots are filled, the creature becomes the equivalent to brain dead. 

  

Action system:

Much like dnd this system has its array of actions they are the following:

  

Action: similar to a dnd action where you can do a attack, a feature that uses an action. Key difference is that the amount of attacks and features you can do in an action increases but rather the amount of action. In dnd a fighter can get more attacks per action as they level, here instead you can get more attacks out of action through abilities, but primarily through getting more actions. Certain spells take up more actions. And proficient spellcasters can cast spells in 1 action that take others 2. 

What this hopes to achieve: less constrianed actions where you are forced to take an action because it is just the best use of your action. Instead you can use a action to attack and another to move or help someone. Just more smaller versatility. 

Half action

Half actions are the replacement for bonus action from dnd. Here they are directly convertible from an action. Something that can be done as a half action can be done twice with an action. Note that in extension when a feature says it takes half as much time to do or consumes half of the amount of actions, it divides down into half actions aswell. 

Example: A spellcaster is going to cast a ritual spell that takes 3 actions. This particular caster is quite proficient ritualist, and therefore has the “Advanced ritual casting” which allows the caster to cast ritual spells that take up to 3 actions in half the ritual casting time. This will thus translate to casting time of 1 action and a half action. 

What this hopes to achieve:
Less constraints on certain abelites locked to just your bonus action, just better fluidity.

Reaction

Like dnd a reaction is a type of action that can be done between turns, ie when it is not your turn. Unlike dnd a reaction is not purely once a turn. Instead the feature that uses the reaction will determine a cost for the reaction, which usually is an amount of action that are subtracted from your next turn. 

Example: An adventurer is going to be hit with a sword, the adventurer thus takes the dodge reaction. The dodge reaction cost half of an action per attack its trying to dodge. Thus on the following adventurers turn they have they have that amount of half actions reduced from their turn. 

What this hopes to achieve: more participation between turns, instead of having to just wait for your turn. Taking a reaction to be able to provide protection to your party members, or just actively dodge instead of everything being statically taken into account with AC.

Free action

Free actions are actions that are “free”. They dont cost anything and are usually “one offs”. An example of this is a surprise attack. When getting the jump on a creature an adventurer gets a free action on the creature. Another free action is shortly communicating to another adventurer. 

Interacting

An interact is like in dnd, an interact with object action, this can be drawing a sword or grabbing a shield. But in this system you can also make a base skill check during combat using the interact action. This could be making a perception check for your surroundings, or even to quickly feint the enemy using a performance check.

Movement 
During your turn you use a half action to move.

Careful movement
When moving you suffer no penalties but only move up to half your movement

Fast movement
When moving any you gain added +1 DC when blocking an attack but -1 DC in dodging (does not stack if you take multiple movement half actions ). If casting spells while moving in this manner any DC needed to succeed is increased by 1. You move up to your full movement. 








Starting out:
Each adventurer and commoner alike start with on their turn:
1 action, 1 half action, movement, and an interact 
As many wound equal to your fortitude. Half of which will be deep wounds. 
As many mental wounds as your sanity.

These starting out wounds and action will be buffed and increased by leveling up in your classes, by your backgrounds, or even by your races. 

Note: it will be explained later more, but with this system damage is more brutal, combat is more lethal. But to be a good defensive character is way more about how much you can reduce the damage someone is doing rather than having the hp to endure it.
Three main examples of reducing damage depending on archetype of character.
A fully armored warrior with a shield will take have their armor reduce the amount of damage. And the warrior can take the tank reaction to reduce the incoming damage using their shield.
A nimble assassin can take the dodge action when an attack is incoming to evade the entire attack. Offset by the fact that the assassin will have to take the dodge reaction for each attack. 
A powerful source weaver will use a deflection spell, depending on which one they cast, the effect will be different but will provide a protection for the source weaver. This is offset by the caster having to use their spellcasting resources. 








## Magic Strings and Casting Components

### Core Magical Theory

Magic originates from the second sun, Known as the Source, a celestial anomaly that emits quantum particles of light stretched into strings. These filaments behave like photons but are elongated and entangled, forming a web of potential energy that permeates the world. Only those with magical sight can perceive them, appearing as glowing threads woven through reality.

These strings are influenced by resonance, tension, and entanglement. Spellcasting is the act of manipulating these strings through three primary components: vocal, somatic, and material.

### Physics engine of magic
- **Wave-particle duality**: Like photons, they exhibit both wave-like and particle-like behavior. This allows them to be "plucked" (Somatic) or "tuned" (Vocal).
- **Superposition**: A single string can exist in multiple states until observed or manipulated. Divination is a school of magic, for example, that takes this into account studying the possibilities of state particles will enter when the collapse when observed. 
- **Entanglement**: Strings can be linked across space and time. Manipulating one can affect another, enabling effects like teleportation, remote sensing, or simultaneous casting.


## Casting Components

### Vocal: Resonance

The vocal component involves producing specific frequencies with the voice to resonate with the strings. Each school of magic has a unique harmonic signature. By matching this frequency, the caster activates the latent energy within the strings.

 Vocal casting is akin to tuning an instrument to the correct pitch. The caster must maintain vocal precision to avoid destabilizing the string.


### Somatic: Weaving

The somatic component is the physical manipulation of strings. Casters use gestures to pluck and weave the filaments into constellations, geometric patterns that define the spell’s structure.

These gestures are not symbolic but functional, directly interacting with the quantum filaments. The complexity of the gesture corresponds to the complexity of the spell. You literally draw and pull on the strings of magic as the source of energy for the casting of your spells. 


### Material: Anchoring

The material component involves using physical objects to stabilize and anchor the spell. These items are chosen for their resonant compatibility with the spell’s frequency.

Materials act as conductors or dampeners, helping to shape and contain the energy of the spell. Rituals often rely heavily on this component.

**Magical learning points.**
In each of your classes you will have a table which tells you how many magical learning points you have. This works quite like your usual skill tree from games. You will have different schools of magics with individual points. The primary part of the skill tree will be to put points in what level spell you can learn. But also points which branch out from this primary part. They will note how proficient you are at casting that level of spells. Allowing you instead of excelling at higher level spells, you could excel in power of low level spells, even as a high level mage.

**Learning spells.** 
The language of magic in this world only allows itself to be read by those who have the ability to grasp it. For the average commoner a book containing high level spells, looks like scribbles. For example when you want to learn a 5 th level spell you must have a point in the adequate skill tree allowing you to cast a 5 th level spell from that school. You also must have points assigned in that spell level in your class table. 

**When learning a spell.**
When you find a spell scroll or any indication of how to cast a spell, you must make an arcana check using your spellcasting ability. The DC set for a success is 6+spell level. To learn the spell successfully you need an amount of successes equal to the spell level to the power of 2. And each attempt at learning a spell takes an hour. 

**Spell levels and spell points**
In this system there are spells ranging from level 1-9. A spellcaster has twice their spellcasting level in amount of spell points. The cost of the spell is detailed on the spell description and also what happens when more spell points are expended to upcast it. 

**Raw casting rules**
You can expend an amount of points as your class allows you into raw casting. Depending on the amount of points spent, the power of the spell increases while also its volatility. 

**Casting a spell**
Each spell will have a section that lists how many actions and half actions it will cost for each component of the spell and the amount of successes needed to pass that part of the spell. 
Once all of the component are finished the spell is cast. 

# **Schools of Magic**

### **Kinetics**

Magic of force and motion.  
Kinetics mages manipulate the tension of magical strings transforming them to kinetic energy and imbuing them in objects or their surrounding push, pull, lift, or strike.  Their craft governs telekinesis, shockwaves, pressure shaping, and the redirection of momentum.

---

### **Lumistry**

Magic of light and perception.  
Lumists use the source to bend the photons of light from the radiant sun to create radiance, illusions, refractions, and veils of invisibility. Their workings blur the line between what is seen and what is truly present.

---

### **Thanaturgy**

Magic of death and decay.  
Thanurgists work with the source to command rot, necrotic dissolution, spiritual residue, and the animation of bodies. This works mainly by collapsing the chemical bonds of molecules in living tissue mainly carbon bonds. 

---

### **Verdant Dynamics**

Magic of living growth and the natural world.  
Verdant practitioners study the self-organizing patterns of living strings, using them to guide plant growth, commune with beasts, shape ecosystems, and awaken the latent vitality woven through wild places.

---

### **Thermaturgy**

Magic of heat, cold, and the winds between them.  
Thermaturgists alter the oscillation of magical strings to raise temperature into flame, collapse it into frost, or move it through air and weather. Their art commands firebursts, chilling waves, and gathered winds.

---

### **Metametrics**

Magic of physical transformation.  
Masters of Metametrics rewrite the structural configuration of an object’s strings, reshaping its substance or altering its properties. Stone may turn to mud, metal to wood, or a tool into something entirely new.

---

### **Vitalic Synthesis**

Magic of healing and restoration.  
Vitalists mend the disrupted bonds and encourage restoration of wounds, and re-aligning fractured bodily patterns. Their magic heals not by adding energy, but by restoring lost structure.


---

### **Aegistry**

Magic of protection and reinforcement.  
Aegists weave defensive matrices by tightening, layering, and locking strings into geometric wards. Their barriers deflect force, absorb strikes, and steady the caster against both mundane and arcane harm.

---

### **Chronophotometry**

Magic of potential futures and uncollapsed timelines.  
Chronophotometrists study the light of the Azure Sun that has not yet settled into a single moment. By observing strings super positioned across time rather than space, they read potential paths, glimpsing what may come rather than what must.

**Flanking**
When a creature has 2 or more enemies in direct melee, the creature has an increased DC for successes on d10 against the enemies within melee ranged. This increased dc is +1 for every creature above one increasing the dc to a maximum of 10. Enemies attacking the creature have their dc for success decreased by +1 (does not increase with more people). When flanking the target counts as **Distracted**.


**Help action.** 
When taking the help action in this system you can add you skill modifier to each of the d 10 roll made by your ally. 

**Block (half action cost)**
When getting hit by a melee or ranged attack you may make the block action. Using a shield or anything similar you can block incoming attacks from that creature. Depending on the shield used you can increase the dc for the creature to get successes. 
**Dodge (half action cost)**
The dodge action has to be taken for each individual creature attacking. Dodging attack allow for way better wound reduction than blocking but is more resource intensive. When a creature makes attacks against you, you may take the dodge reaction. You roll an amount of dice equal to the amount of points in agility plus the amount of points in dodge.

spellcasting rules

Each individual component of casting a spell is individual. Once that part is completed it remains locked in. It remains this way for 1 round. If no other components are attempted within 1 round the magic dissipates and the successes on components are lost. To maintain the magic for longer than one round you may make a concetration roll see below. 

Concetrating on a spell. 
Concentration works differently than dnd. You may hold concentration on however many spells as you wish but each turn while concetrating on a spell you must expend spellpoints equal to the spell level. Thus when casting a 3rd level concentration spell to keep it up you must expend 3 spell points a round to keep it up(no action required for concentration).

Maintining concetration
The only time concetration is immidiatly dropped is when you dropped on any condition where you lose mental faculties. 
When you take a shallow wound you need to make a focus+spell casting ability save dc 6 needing successes equal to the spells level in successes. 
When taking a deep wound the dc increases to 8 instead. These dc's are movable by anything that aids are messes with your focus.
