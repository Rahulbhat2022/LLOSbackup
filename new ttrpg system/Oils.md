Here's the **Monster Hunter's Oil Compendium** restructured with 3 tiers where each creature type has its own oil that improves with each tier:

## Monster Hunter's Oil Compendium

### Basic Oil Components

**Primary Bases:**
- **Beast Essence** - Concentrated animal pheromones and musk
- **Cursed Salt** - Blessed salt infused with protective wards
- **Wraith Dust** - Ethereal residue from spectral encounters
- **Dragon Marrow** - Rare extract from draconid bone

**Stabilizers:**
- **Refined Oil** - Weapon coating base
- **Distilled Spirits** - Alcohol preservative  
- **Crushed Meteorite** - Meteoric iron powder

**Enhancers:**
- **Silver Dust** - Purified silver shavings
- **Grave Moss** - Herbs that grow in cursed ground
- **Void Crystal** - Crystallized magical energy

### Special Notes

**Mortals**: No standard monster oils work on mortals - they require poisons or specialized alchemical preparations.

**Blighted**: These corrupted beings require special **Purification Oils** (same recipes as Shambler Oils but with Cursed Salt instead of Grave Moss).

### Crafting Rules

**Time Requirements:**
- Tier 1: 30 minutes
- Tier 2: 1 hour  
- Tier 3: 2 hours

**Skill Bonuses:**
- **Minor Poison Handling**: DC reduced by 1 for Tier 1 oils
- **Poison Handling**: DC reduced by 1 for Tier 1-2 oils, can apply as half action
- **Poison Expertise**: Gain 1 additional success when crafting any oil
- **Venom Mastery**: Oils last 1 additional application on weapons
