### Tier 4 — Veteran

> Unlocked at level 12. A Tier 4 fighter is seasoned, dangerous, and genuinely hard to kill.

|Skill|Description|Prerequisite|Tier|Cost|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Power Strike IV**|Power Strike now adds 4 damage dice. On a hit, target makes Fortitude + Toughness DC 7 or is knocked prone in addition to any push effect.|Power Strike III|4|2|
|**Weapon Mastery**|Choose one weapon type (sword, axe, spear, hammer, etc). When using that weapon type, add 1 automatic success to all attack rolls and reduce the DC of block/parry reactions by 1 while wielding it.|Weapon Training|4|2|
|**Dual Flow**|When you have two weapons drawn, you may combine their half action attacks freely within a single turn — attack with one, then the other, in any order, as two separate half actions. Both count as part of your turn's action economy normally.|Weapon Flow|4|2|
|**Unbreakable**|You cannot be forced prone, disarmed, or moved against your will unless your attacker rolls at least 2 more successes than your defence total.|Unyielding|4|2|
|**Juggernaut**|While wearing heavy armour, when you take the block reaction, add your armour's soak value again as bonus soak dice against that attack. Additionally, enemies who attempt to shove or move you have +2 DC on those checks.|Enduring Physique|4|2|
|**Guardian's Stand**|Allies within 1 movement of you have +1 DC against enemy attacks while you are conscious and not Restrained. You may take the block reaction on behalf of an adjacent ally (costs your reaction, not theirs).|Stalwart Guard|4|2|
|**Battlefield Commander**|Once per short rest as a half action, issue a battlefield order to up to 3 allies in line of sight. Each of them may immediately move up to half their movement as a free action.|Tactical Awareness|4|2|
|**Precision Counter**|When you use Counterstrike, your counter-attack cannot be parried or blocked.|Counterstrike|4|2|
|**Indomitable**|Once per encounter when you fail a Fortitude + Toughness roll of any kind, you may reroll it and take the new result.|Iron Will|4|2|

### Tier 5 — Legend

> Unlocked at level 17. These are rare, defining capabilities — the skills that make a legend. Each one should change how the Fighter is played, not just add numbers.

|Skill|Description|Prerequisite|Tier|Cost|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Power Strike V**|Power Strike adds 5 damage dice. The +1 DC attack penalty is removed — Power Strike is now your default strike, refined to full precision. Once per encounter, a Power Strike that deals a deep wound automatically rolls the worst possible result on the deep wound table (a 1 — Arterial Hemorrhage) instead of rolling randomly.|Power Strike IV|5|2|
|**Weapon Mastery II**|Your chosen weapon type now grants 2 automatic successes on attacks and reduces block/parry DCs by 2. Additionally, once per turn when you attack with your mastered weapon, you may choose to apply one of the following without rolling — target is pushed 2m, target drops a held item, or target is staggered (loses their half action next turn).|Weapon Mastery|5|2|
|**Perfect Counter**|When you use Counterstrike or Riposte, if your counter-attack succeeds, the target cannot take any reactions until the start of their next turn — they are fully on the defensive.|Precision Counter|5|2|
|**Unstoppable**|Once per encounter, declare Unstoppable before moving. Until the end of your turn, you cannot be stopped, slowed, knocked prone, or have your movement reduced by any means. Enemies in your path who do not move are automatically shoved (treat as a Power Strike success for the push).|Juggernaut|5|3|
|**Warlord's Presence**|Your presence on the battlefield is a force. All allies within 2 movements of you add 1 die to their Resolve + Focus checks. Once per encounter as a free action, you may issue a battle cry — all allies in earshot immediately remove 1 stack of any condition (fear, pain, bleed, etc) of their choice.|Battlefield Commander|5|3|
|**Deathless**|When you take a deep wound that would start your death countdown, you may spend a reaction to immediately stabilise it — no roll required. This does not heal it; it simply stops the clock. Once per encounter. Additionally, you do not die at the end of your deep wound countdown — you fall unconscious instead, and stabilise naturally unless someone takes action to finish you.|Warrior's Endurance|5|3|
|**Second Wind Mastery**|Second Wind now recovers both shallow and deep wounds. For deep wounds, roll half your Fortitude score as before — each success closes one deep wound (stabilises and removes its active condition). Can now be used twice per short rest.|Second Wind|5|2|
|**Paragon's Reflex**|You may take one additional reaction per round with no action cost. The reaction must be a block, parry, counterstrike, or guardian block — not an offensive reaction.|Quick Reflexes|5|3|
|**Bladestorm**|Once per encounter as a full action, make a separate melee attack against every creature within your weapon's reach. Each attack rolls independently. Each target defends independently. You may use Power Strike on any one of these attacks.|Whirlwind|5|3|
|**Indomitable Will**|You cannot be broken by morale, fear, or mental pressure. You are immune to the Distracted condition from non-damage sources. When you take a mental wound, make a Resolve + Focus DC 5 roll — on success, do not take it.|Indomitable|5|3|
