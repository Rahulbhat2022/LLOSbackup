


**Weld** (1 point)  
S (half action)(2 successes) M (half action)(2 success) Duration: Permanent. Range: Touch.  
You emit focused radioactive energy to fuse two pieces of the same material together at a molecular level. You can weld metal to metal, stone to stone, or wood to wood, creating a bond as strong as if the materials were originally one piece. The welding area can be up to 10cm in diameter. This only works on non-magical materials.

For every 2 additional spell points expended, you can increase the welding area by 10cm in diameter or weld different compatible materials together (metal to stone, etc.). For every 2 points expended the time taken increases by half an action. 

**Melt** (2 point)  
S (half action)(2 successes) V (half action)(2 success) Duration: Instant. Range: 5 meters.  
You bombard a small area with destructive radiation, causing molecular bonds to weaken and materials to soften or melt. Target a 5cm cube of non-magical material. Metal becomes molten and soft for 1 minute, stone crumbles to sand, ice melts instantly, and wood becomes pulpy. This can be used to damage objects, create openings, or disable simple mechanisms.

For every 2 additional spell points expended, you can increase the affected area by 5cm in all dimensions or extend the duration by 1 minute (for materials that soften rather than permanently change).

### Thermaturgy

**Warm Flicker** (1 point)  
S (half action)(2 successes) V (half action)(2 successes) duration: instant or concertation.
You create a small, controlled thermal effect. Choose one of the following effects when you cast the spell:

- **Warmth:** You touch an object, causing it to emanate gentle heat. It can dry wet clothes, warm a drink, or provide comfort. For the duration, it sheds dim light in a 2-meter radius.
- **Chill:** You touch an object or creature. A living creature must make a Fortitude + Toughness check against your spellcasting success threshold. On a fail, they are chilled to the bone, and the DC for their next Agility-based skill check is increased by 1. 

This spell can be cast over a longer period of time as well. By constantly expending 1 spell point you may slowly raise or lower the temperature using this spell

**Reconfigure Matter** (2 points)  
S (half action)(2 successes) M (half action)(2 successes) duration instant
Range: self
You target a 5-meter cube of non-living, mundane material and temporarily rewrite its structural configuration, transforming it into another simple material of similar mass. For example, you could turn a section of a stone wall into sand, a patch of dirt into thick mud, or a wooden door into brittle glass.

- **Environmental Control:** If you transform an area of ground, it becomes difficult terrain. Any creature standing in the area when it is transformed must make an Agility + Acrobatics check against your spellcasting success threshold or be restrained by the shifting matter until the start of their next turn.
- **Object Alteration:** If you target an object held or worn by a creature (like a shield or section of armor), the creature can make a Fortitude + Toughness check to negate the effect. Depending on the object the dm will determine amounts of successes needed to alter the item. 

For every 2 additional spell points expended, you can increase the size of the cube by 5 meters on one side.

#### **Level 2**

**Biomorphic Adaptation** (4 points)  
S (Action)(4 successes) V (action)(3 successes) Duration: Concentration, 10 minutes. Range: Self.  
You rewrite the structural strings of your own body, temporarily reshaping your form to suit your needs. When you cast this spell, choose one of the following effects. You can't change this effect for the duration of the spell unless you cast it again.

- **Adaptive Form:** You grow features suited for a specific environment. You might develop gills and webbed digits to breathe underwater and gain a swim speed equal to your base movement, or grow sharp claws and pads to gain a climb speed equal to your base movement.
- **Mutable Visage:** You transform your appearance. You can change your height, weight, facial features, voice, and skin coloration, though you retain your basic limb structure. You add 2 dice to any Deception or Performance checks made to maintain your disguise.
- **Natural Weapons:** Your limbs transform into deadly natural weapons, such as bone spurs, sharp talons, or hardened fists. Your unarmed strikes now count as a weapon you are proficient with, and they deal damage equal to the successes rolled on **1** dice.

For every 2 additional spell points expended, you may choose an additional effect from the list to gain.


#### **Level 2**

**Weapon Shift** (3 points)  
S (Reaction)(4 successes) Cost: half action. Duration: Instant. Range: 15 meters.  
When a creature you can see makes an attack with a non-magical weapon, you can rewrite its structure for a crucial moment. The attacker must make a Resolve + Focus check against your spellcasting success threshold. On a failure, their weapon momentarily turns to a useless material (e.g., the blade becomes rope, the arrowhead turns to dust), causing the attack to automatically fail.

For every 2 additional spell points expended, you increase the DC of the target's Resolve + Focus check by 1.
