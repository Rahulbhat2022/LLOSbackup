

## Creature Types
- **Beasts** - Wolves, bears, natural predators and animals
- **Mortals** - Humans, elves, dwarves, and other bipedal civilized races
- **Draconids** - Dragons, wyverns, drakes, and serpentine monsters
- **Insectoids** - Giant spiders, , Giant insects, and swarm creatures, especially who have Exoskeletons. 
- **Cursed Ones** - Lycanthropes, skinwalkers, and other shape-shifting afflicted
- **Blighted** - Plague-bearers, diseased creatures, and mortals infested by voidglass corruption
- **Vampires** - Bloodthirst-cursed nobles, feral bloodlings, and life-draining parasites
- **Specters** - Wraiths, phantoms, and ghostly apparitions
- **Demons** - Infernal creatures, hellspawn, and beings from burning dimensions
- **Elementals** - Beings of fire, water, earth, and air given form by Source energy
- **Constructs** - Golems, animated armor, and magical automatons powered by Source strings
- **Chimeras** - Unnatural fusions created by twisted Source manipulation and alchemical experiments
- **Aberrants** - Void-touched horrors, reality-benders, and entities warped by Source corruption
- **Shamblers** - Fungal colonies, moss-walkers, and ambulatory plant networks
