## **Weapon Material Effectiveness**

### **Iron**

**Effective Against: Mortals, Beasts**

- **Reasoning**: Iron is the foundation of mortal civilization - tools, weapons, blood. Natural creatures and mortals have no inherent resistance to well-crafted steel. Iron disrupts no magical defenses because these creatures rely on flesh, bone, and natural toughness rather than supernatural protection.
- **Effect**: Standard damage, no penalties against natural armor

### **Silver**

**Effective Against: Cursed Ones, Vampires, Specters**

	- **Reasoning**: Silver's purity makes it antithetical to corruption and curse magic. The lunar metal reflects the Source's light in a way that burns through the twisted magical bonds that hold lycanthropes together and prevents vampiric regeneration. Against specters, silver acts as a "conductor," allowing physical weapons to interact with incorporeal forms.
- **Effect**: +1 dice, reduces regeneration, can strike incorporeal beings

### **Sanctified Steel**

**Effective Against: Demons, Blighted**

- **Reasoning**: Iron blessed through divine ritual carries sacred resonance that disrupts infernal essence and purifies corruption. The sanctification process aligns the metal's atomic structure with divine frequencies, making it burn demonic flesh like acid. Against the Blighted, the holy blessing counteracts the voidglass corruption that animates diseased tissue.
- **Effect**: +2 damage against Demons, +1 against Blighted, prevents disease transmission

### **Red Meteorite**

**Effective Against: Draconids, Aberrants**

- **Reasoning**: This space-born metal was forged in stellar furnaces and exposed to cosmic Source radiation during its journey. Draconids, being ancient beings who predate current magical laws, are vulnerable to "primordial metal" that remembers the universe's earlier state. Aberrants, as reality-warping entities, are destabilized by matter that has experienced the void between worlds.
- **Effect**: against Draconids ignores natural armor; +2 damage against Aberrants, immunity to madness effects when wielding

### **Boron-lattice**

**Effective Against: Elementals, Constructs**

- **Reasoning**: Boron's molecular structure naturally absorbs and disrupts magical energy, similar to how it absorbs neutrons in nuclear reactions. Elementals, being pure concentrations of Source-manipulated matter, find their cohesion disrupted by boron's "magical dampening field." Constructs, animated purely by woven Source strings, suffer system failures when their controlling magic is absorbed and dissipated.
- **Effect**: +2 damage, disrupts elemental form (Elementals lose special abilities for 1 round when hit), shuts down Construct special abilities permanently on critical hits

## **Strategic Considerations**

**No Material Advantage**: Insectoids, Chimeras, Shamblers

- These creatures require oils, tactics, or specialized techniques rather than specific materials
- Maintains the importance of the oil system and encourages diverse tactical approaches


## Bones of Jormungandr

These bones are closer to metal than they are otherwise "normal" bones, and they carry a particular magical and alchemical properties still, particularly when used in equipment. 


**Weapon Feature — Venom of Jormungrandr**
When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you may infuse them with a latent poison as part of that strike. This toxin lies dormant for up to 24 hours, slowly circulating through their body.
At any point during that time, you can use an action to trigger one of the following effects:
    • Slow Release: The target must succeed on a DC 6 needing 3 successes toughness+fortitude roll or take 2d10 successes poison damage at the end of each of its turns for the next minute. On a success, the damage is halved.
    • Immediate Release: The target must make a DC 6 needing 3 successes toughness+fortitude roll or take 3d10 successes of poison damage and become paralyzed until the start of your next turn. On a success, they take half damage and are not paralyzed.
Uses: Once per long rest. This ability is refreshed immediately if any creature dies within 1 minute of triggering either slow or immidiate release.


**Armor and Shield Features**

Light Armor & Bracers
    • 3 less poison damage take per instance. 
    • When you take the Hide action, you may release a cloud of noxious gas 4 meter radius, creating an area of magical darkness that only you can see through. Any enemy creature in this area counts as distracted. It lasts for 3 turns and can be used twice per long rest. 
    
Heavy Armor
    • 3 less poison damage take per instance.
    • Once per day, you can exude a slick, viscous coating across your armor, lasting 1 minute. While active:
        ○ Any slashing or bludgeoning attack has a +1 dc against you.
        ○ -2 dc on checks to escape a grapple and you enemies have +1 dc to escape grapple from you. 
        ○ Any creature grappling you, or grappled by you, takes 2d10 successes of damage each round it is in contact with you. 


## Beast Metal

**Effective Against: All living creatures. No exceptions.**

- **Reasoning:** Beast Metal is not forged. It was never ore, never mined, never smelted. It is a fragment of the blade buried in Daria's chest 
- **Base Weapon Bonus:** +2 dice to attack rolls
- **Base Armor Bonus:** +2 soak against all physical damage
- **Curse:** The Obsession (see below — mandatory, cannot be removed)

---

### Weapon Features

**Bestial Regeneration _(Base- +2)_**

When you deal a deep wound to a creature with this weapon, immediately recover 1 shallow wound. When you kill a creature with this weapon, immediately recover 1 deep wound.

**Rite of Flame**


When you hit a creature with this weapon, you may choose to ignite the wound. The target gains a **Brand** — they take 1 shallow wound at the start of each of their turns for 3 rounds as the wound burns from the inside. Multiple Brands stack.

A creature may attempt to suppress a Brand as a half action — Fortitude + Toughness DC 6. On success, that Brand is extinguished. On failure, it persists and the DC for future suppression attempts on that Brand increases by 1.

**Uses:** Twice per short rest.

**Brand of Castigation**

When you hit a creature, you may mark them with the Brand of Castigation as a free action. While marked:

- The marked creature cannot become hidden from you by any means — you always know their exact position regardless of darkness, invisibility, or distance within the same plane.
- Your attacks against the marked creature have DC reduced by 1.
- If the marked creature moves more than 20m away from you, you immediately know their direction and distance, and may move up to your full movement toward them as a free action — no action cost, no reactions triggered.

Only one creature can be marked at a time. The Brand of Castigation lasts until the marked creature dies or until you mark a different target.

#### Mark of Obsession _(+3 Upgrade — replaces +2 base)_

**To unlock +3:** The following condition must be triggered thrice:
The wielder must have taken at least 3 deep wounds in a single encounter while wielding this weapon and survived. The metal recognises endurance. Note: this must happen under extreme pressure and adrenaline, thus no cutting yourself out of combat just to get the feature. 

When the +3 upgrade activates, the weapon's base bonus increases from +2 to +3 dice on all attack rolls. 

Additionally, **Unkillable Momentum** becomes active — the weapon grows stronger as the wielder is consumed by combat:

| Deep Wounds Currently On Wielder | Additional Effect                                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**                            | +1 die to damage rolls                                                                    |
| **2**                            | Attacks ignore 2 points of armour soak                                                    |
| **3**                            | On a hit, the target cannot heal any missing hp until start of your next round.           |
| **4+**                           | All of the above, and Bestial Regeneration now triggers on any hit — not just deep wounds |

The metal rewards being hurt. The closer you are to death, the more it gives you. This is not coincidence. This is what it did to Daria.

#### Bestial Regeneration _(Armor version)_

Additionally, your unarmed strikes deal damage as if you were wielding a light weapon and deal 1d10 as base.

### The Obsession

_Mandatory curse. Present in all Beast Metal equipment. Cannot be removed, suppressed, or transferred._

The metal came from a blade that transformed Daria against her will. It does the same to everyone who carries it, more slowly, more completely, more permanently.

The Obsession has three stages. They are not reversible, by any means. 

---

**Stage One — The Hunger** _Begins immediately upon equipping any Beast Metal item._

- You must make a Resolve + Focus DC 5 check at the start of any encounter where you have not dealt at least 1 wound to a living creature in the past 24 hours. On failure, you are Compelled to attack the nearest living creature on your first turn — ally or enemy. You may spend a full action suppressing the compulsion instead of attacking, but the check repeats next turn.

---

**Stage Two — The Pull** _Triggers after you have dealt killing blows to 10 or more creatures with Beast Metal._

Lose stage one gain these instead:

- You cannot willingly retreat from combat. If an encounter is active and you are not incapacitated, leaving the combat area requires a Resolve + Focus DC 7 needing 3 successes check as a full action. On failure, you cannot leave this turn.
- When you drop to 2 or fewer shallow wounds in combat, you must make a Resolve + Focus DC 6 check or immediately attack the nearest creature — ally or enemy — as a reaction(half action cost). The check can be made once per trigger. On success, you retain control.

---

**Stage Three — The Shape** _Triggers after you have used Mark of Obsession (+3) in 5 or more separate encounters._

Lose stage 2 gain these instead. 
- Once per encounter when you take a deep wound, the DM makes a secret Resolve + Focus DC 8 check for you, needing 3 successes.  On failure, you partially transform — your movement increases by 4m, your unarmed strikes deal damage as a base of 3d10, and you cannot use any class features that require precise thought or social interaction. This lasts for 3 rounds. You are not fully in control during this time. You make very instinctual brutish movement, at times may have difficulty separating ally from enemy. The Dm will describe the fog and you may act accordingly.  
- After an encounter in which a partial transformation occurred, make a Resolve + Focus DC 6 check. On failure, you retain 1 physical feature of the transformation permanently — elongated fingers, a second set of teeth, eyes that don't reflect light correctly. The GM chooses. Each one is cumulative. You take one point of mental wounds at the end of the encounter. 
- You now register as a Cursed One to all detection and divination effects, not just for mechanical purposes. What you are becoming is no longer ambiguous.


---

### Lore Note

> _The blade was not made to create Beast Metal. Beast Metal does not exist as a material in any alchemical catalogue, any forge record, or any smith's knowledge. What fragments have been recovered come from one source: the blade in Daria's chest. No one knows how it got there. No one knows who made it. No one knows what it was trying to do. What is known is that Daria has carried it for longer than most cities have stood, and that she is still — technically — herself. The metal remembers her. Everything made from it remembers her too. Whether that is a comfort or a warning depends on how well you know what Daria has become._

### Weapon Feature — Resonant Strike

An Orichalcum weapon passively absorbs residual Source energy from every spell cast within 10 meters of the wielder, including their own. The weapon stores up to **3 charges**. Charges for one minute, after which point the metal resets to 0.

**Gaining charges:**

- A spell is cast within 10m of you (by anyone): gain 1 charge

**Spending charges on a hit:**

| Charges spent | Effect                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1             | The strike releases stored energy into the target. They take an additional 1d10 wound successes. Reduce with Fortitude + Toughness.        |
| 2             | As above but 2d10, and the target's next spell (if any) costs 2 additional spell points as the discharge disrupts their Source connection. |
| 3             | As above but 3d10, and you may end one spell that the target has active, as long as the spell's level is 3rd or lower.                     |

### Armour Feature — Light Armour and Medium

Orichalcum-inlaid light armour weaves thin veins of the metal through the primary material. It charges passively like the weapon.

- The armour stores up to **2 charges** using the same rules as the weapon (spells cast within 10m, including your own).
- **Spell Deflection (reaction, 1 charge):** When a damage spell lands on you, spend 1 charge to reduce the incoming wound successes by 2 before your normal defensive roll. 
- **Rushing Steps (1 charge):** Once per turn you can take the movement half action without expending a half action.

**Cetus Hide**
### Weapon Feature — Crushing Tide

A weapon edged or headed with Cetus scale does not cut so much as it _stops_ whatever it meets. The scale's absorptive geometry works in reverse when driven forward — force delivered through it concentrates rather than disperses.

- **Unyielding Impact (Once a turn):** The first attack made with this weapon cannot have their successes reduced by armour soak. The strike finds the path of least resistance the way water finds a crack — armour does not help. Natural toughness and magical reduction still apply normally.
- **Weight of the Deep:** 3/day you may choose to put the full mass of the strike behind it. Any roll explodes on a 9 and 10. 

---

### Armour Feature

- **Scale Absorption:** All damage types are reduced by 1. Does not affect any damage types you already reduce.
- **Unyielding Surface:** When a creature attempts to shove, knock you prone, or forcibly move you, they have +2 DC on that check.
- **The Tide Holds:** Once per encounter when you would be reduced to 0 wounds, you may instead remain at 1 shallow wound. The suit absorbs the killing blow the way Cetus absorbed the crushing dark of the deep ocean
