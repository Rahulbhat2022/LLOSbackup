## Grit

Grit is the Gunslinger's core resource, a measure of adrenaline, instinct, and focus

**Maximum Grit = Composure score.**

**Gaining Grit:**

- Score the first successful attack of an encounter: gain 1 Grit
- Reduce a target to 0 wounds: gain 1 Grit
- Successfully dodge an attack while at Close range to the attacker: gain 1 Grit

**Grit resets to 0** at the end of an encounter as the adrenaline in your veins settles down.
