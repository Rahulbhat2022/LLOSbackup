Reference Guide

The Codex

Rules, magic, and lore for the world of Solace: a city of ash and ambition, lit by a sun that may already be dying.

Magic originates from the Source: a celestial anomaly that emits quantum particles of light stretched into strings. These filaments permeate the world like threads of potential energy woven through reality. Spellcasting is the art of manipulating these strings through Vocal resonance, Somatic weaving, and Material anchoring. Only those with magical sight can perceive them at all.- On the Nature of the Source, Kemura Academy

Schools of Magic

Spells

Nine schools, each a different means of manipulating the Source strings. Each component (Somatic, Verbal, Material) demands its own actions and its own threshold of successes.

Combat

Combat Guide

A short walkthrough of how a hit actually resolves, from the attacker's first roll to the wound that lands, plus a flowchart of the whole sequence.

Combat

Actions & Reactions

Unlike D&D, you gain more actions as you level, not more attacks per action. Reactions cost from your next turn. Every decision is a real trade-off.

Combat

Wounds

This system is more brutal than most. Combat is lethal. Deep wounds bleed out. Survival is about reducing damage, not absorbing it.

Character

Stats & Skills

Eight core attributes, each governing a distinct domain. Weapons often combine two stats: a bow uses both Strength and Composure.

Weapons

Character

Arts

Unlike the normal skills on your character sheets, arts are more complex skills that many spend their life on perfecting. In this category you have skills such as tools, weapon arts and defensive arts.

Bestiary

Creature Types

Fourteen creature categories. Each has specific material and oil vulnerabilities: the right preparation before a hunt is half the battle.

Crafting

Monster Oils

Applied to weapons before a hunt. Three tiers per creature type: each tier requires rarer components but delivers dramatically better results.

Crafting

Poisons

Where oils target monsters, poisons target mortals. Four tiers from common blade coatings to legendary mind-shattering serums.

Character Build

Classes

Your class defines your combat role, resource economy, and skill progression. Each class has five tiers of skills unlocked as you level up.

Character Build

Subclasses

Subclasses are universal: any character can take one regardless of class. They represent a deeper specialisation or a secondary discipline.

Character Build

Backgrounds

Backgrounds represent who you were before adventuring: the contacts you made, the skills you picked up, and the resources you still carry.

Crafting

Ether Cores

One-use crystals of concentrated Source energy. Consumed directly for raw temporary power, or permanently infused into a spellcasting focus by a craftsman for lasting effect. Four strains. Four completely different identities.
